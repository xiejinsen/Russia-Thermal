# TXN-20261007-02 — Dense Paper / Claim Explorers

state: CLOSED
date: 2026-10-07
purpose: replace vertically expensive Paper and Claim card lists with high-density spreadsheet-like research indexes after direct user review.

User-observed problem:
- Papers and Claims are already numerous and will continue to grow;
- one record consumed too much vertical space;
- only a few records were visible per screen;
- finding a specific paper or Claim required excessive vertical scanning.

Implemented:
- reusable `DenseResearchTable.astro`;
- sticky table header inside a bounded scroll viewport;
- reusable ExplorerToolbar gained optional sticky mode below the site header;
- Papers converted from large cards to dense tabular rows;
- Claims converted from large cards to dense tabular rows;
- full-text search remains available;
- visible-record count updates as filters change.

Paper filters:
- Country;
- Year;
- Deep Read complete vs reference/supporting only;
- Direction.

Paper columns:
- title / ID / authors;
- year;
- country;
- venue;
- Deep Read state / review basis;
- Claim count;
- Direction count / compact Direction context.

Claim filters:
- Status;
- Confidence;
- Decision role;
- Direction.

Claim columns:
- proposition / Claim ID;
- status;
- confidence;
- decision role;
- supporting-source count;
- pressure-source count;
- Direction count / compact Direction context.

Interaction principle:
- list pages optimize scanning and filtering;
- detail pages retain high information density for deep reading;
- do not use large cards for high-cardinality research-object indexes.
