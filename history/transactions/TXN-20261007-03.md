# TXN-20261007-03 — Evidence Dense Explorer and Deep-Read Readability

state: CLOSED
date: 2026-10-07
purpose: improve technical reading comfort while applying the high-cardinality Explorer pattern to Evidence.

User-observed problems:
- explanatory Deep Read prose was too small for sustained reading;
- Evidence has high cardinality and the expanded card stack consumed too much vertical space.

Implemented:
- enlarged Paper Deep Read explanatory typography while keeping metadata compact;
- Q1-Q10 body prose increased to a reading-oriented size;
- Why-this-paper-matters and Evidence Boundary prose enlarged;
- Evidence converted from expanded card stack to dense spreadsheet-like table;
- Evidence uses the same compact single-row sticky filter toolbar as Papers / Claims;
- sticky table header and bounded table-body scrolling;
- searchable actor / person / Claim / Capability / Direction context preserved.

Evidence filters:
- Source type;
- Country;
- Year;
- Usage role;
- Support / pressure / context relation;
- Direction.

Evidence columns:
- Source;
- Type;
- Year;
- Country;
- Role;
- Support count;
- Pressure count;
- Direction context.

Navigation:
- Paper evidence opens Paper detail;
- non-Paper evidence retains direct original-source navigation;
- Paper rows retain a separate Original-source link.

Design principle updated:
- Papers / Claims / Evidence are high-cardinality dense Explorers;
- metadata may be compact;
- explanatory research prose must remain comfortably readable.
