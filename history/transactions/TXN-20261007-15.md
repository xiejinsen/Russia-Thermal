## Findings

### 1. Russia retains a real extreme-confinement mechanism capability

PAPER-RU-FILM-002 provides unusually direct experimental evidence:
- slit height 12.5 um;
- width 10 mm;
- aspect ratio 1:800;
- five working liquids;
- liquid superficial velocity 0.0026–0.266 m/s;
- gas superficial velocity 0.266–7.73 m/s;
- five flow regimes;
- Saffman-Taylor instability, transverse-pressure-gradient instability and wettability as pattern-determining factors.

This is the strongest part of the Russian residual.

### 2. The shear-film platform is active and electronics-targeted, but system overhead is not closed

PAPER-RU-FILM-001 confirms:
- direct processor / power-electronics motivation;
- verified methodology from convection through intense local heating;
- local heating around 1 kW/cm2 in the reported test envelope;
- CHF advantage over pool-boiling references.

However, the public record does not close:
- gas/liquid parasitic power;
- pressure drop;
- acoustics;
- separator / condenser / nozzle volume;
- sealing;
- orientation / shock recovery;
- phone-scale system COP.

### 3. High heat flux is not differentiated

PAPER-CN-FILM-001:
- gradient wire-mesh capillary thin-film boiling;
- CHF 202.8 W/cm2;
- strong HTC;
- passive capillary-pressure / permeability optimization.

PAPER-CN-FILM-002:
- pressure-controlled nanoporous thin-film boiling;
- roughly 1400 W/cm2 under constant-pressure bilateral operation;
- 1500–2000 W/cm2 under alternative pressure/heating control;
- record CHF 2074 W/cm2.

Therefore absolute heat-flux leadership cannot support a Russia collaboration thesis.

### 4. Surface chemistry is supporting, not differentiating

PAPER-RU-FRUMKIN-BIPHILIC-001 confirms:
- Kutateladze + Frumkin cross-institution capability;
- laser-created copper cavities;
- selective fluorinated-silane hydrophobization;
- cavity pitch/density as an important boiling-control variable.

But generic biphilic / patterned wettability is already crowded globally and does not justify a standalone direction.

## Strategic interpretation

The direction is not a current phone cooling bet.

It is a high-risk mechanism/IP reserve that may be useful if:
- future phone architectures already contain an air-moving element;
- an extremely thin liquid layer can be integrated without excessive loop complexity;
- Russian flow-regime / rupture knowledge reduces failure risk or experiment cost.

Absent that system window, retain the science and do not fund a full device path.
