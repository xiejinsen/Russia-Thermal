Main conclusion:
passive telemetry alone is insufficiently specific for a strong mechanism-state claim.

Best candidates:
- reversible -> irreversible crisis transition under controlled excitation;
- rewetting/recovery state using hysteresis and time-to-rewet features.

Weaker candidates:
- long-term capillary/wetting degradation: lab-first / low-medium identifiability;
- oxidation/surface chemistry: offline QA prior, not online target;
- fill ratio: confounder/background.

Strategic transitions:
- Failure-Aware UTVC remains KEEP/NARROW and may advance to minimum-dataset design.
- Health-Aware UTVC remains KEEP/NARROW but should be lab-first.
- no new Direction created for a combined observer.
