## New / refined evidence findings

### 1. Dryout is a state-history process, not only an instantaneous limit

China capillary-fed boiling evidence shows that repeated dryout can alter subsequent wettability / capillary state and materially reduce later CHF in the tested system.

Global transient heat-pipe evidence independently shows:
- finite time-to-dryout;
- throttling-dependent time-to-rewet;
- persistent post-dryout thermal hysteresis;
- spatiotemporally varying wick saturation as a useful hidden state.

This creates a technical bridge between Failure-aware and Health-aware reasoning.

### 2. Global baselines are stronger than a generic RC/anomaly comparator

The expanded baseline now includes:
- transient saturation-state dryout / recovery models;
- internal vapor temperature / pressure ground truth;
- boiling-aware vapor-chamber hydrodynamics using two-phase relative permeability.

In the surfaced vapor-chamber model example, ignoring boiling / two-phase wick hydrodynamics can grossly overpredict dryout capacity.

Therefore a Russia-informed hypothesis must beat more than generic external-temperature anomaly detection.

### 3. Kutateladze residual narrowed to mechanism ground truth / taxonomy

Russian papers retain real mechanism depth:
- confinement-dependent transition from surface-drying crisis to hydrodynamic crisis;
- staged dry-spot -> transition-boiling -> sharp-temperature-rise behavior;
- topology-linked anisotropic drying-front propagation;
- reversible / irreversible crisis framing from the existing core paper.

However, internal measurement and sophisticated dryout modeling are not uniquely Russian.

The most defensible remaining collaboration residual is now:
mechanism-resolved laboratory ground truth / crisis taxonomy that can falsify or extend an internally owned phone-UTVC state model.
