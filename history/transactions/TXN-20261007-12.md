## Findings

### 1. China reliability evidence now forms a coherent product-path chain

Deep reading shows that the China comparator is stronger than the previous summary implied:

- PAPER-CN-AGE-001 establishes oxygen/oxidation -> wetting/capillary degradation -> thermal failure without gross structural damage.
- PAPER-CN-AGE-002 extends the mechanism into an "oxygen footprint" service-life method:
  - 150–200 C accelerated aging;
  - around 1% wick-surface oxygen maps to a predicted 13+ year service life at 80 C;
  - oxygen content versus predicted life has R2 = 0.98;
  - reported prediction error is approximately 8%.
- PAPER-CN-AGE-003 turns oxidation state into production QA:
  - color, capillary climb and thermal performance are linked to oxidation grade;
  - optimized combined image / color model reports testing accuracy above 95%.
- PAPER-CN-AGE-004 adds direct mobile-form-factor evidence:
  - 0.7 mm thickness;
  - 3.95 g;
  - stable reported performance after 30 days at 90 C accelerated aging.

This is mechanism -> remaining-life proxy -> manufacturing QA -> mobile-scale aging validation.

### 2. MPEI still has a real but narrower residual

PAPER-RU-AGE-001 remains unusual because it reports:
- 42 calendar months of actual periodic operation;
- one engineered hierarchical evaporator surface;
- comparatively stable integral thermal performance;
- degraded post-operation capillary imbibition.

The surviving insight is therefore:
a two-phase surface can exhibit subclinical capillary-state degradation over real calendar time before gross thermal-performance collapse is obvious.

### 3. Evidence rarity is not the same as a strategic control point

The MPEI evidence is valuable as long-horizon ground truth, but:
- it is R410A / thermosyphon / non-phone geometry;
- current public evidence does not show a time-resolved precursor usable for remaining-life prediction;
- it does not demonstrate incremental value after oxygen chemistry and production/process state are known;
- China has stronger direct product-path reliability tooling and mobile-scale aging evidence.

Therefore retaining MPEI as a Primary Strategic Candidate would over-weight rarity of the evidence type relative to transferability and controllable product leverage.
