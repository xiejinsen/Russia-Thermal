## Research decision and falsification

**MISIS is valid academic materials capability but not a new standalone phone TEC or magnetic device collaboration bet**. Generators convert heat to electricity; coolers consume electrical work to remove heat and entail hot-side rejection. Materials measured at 575 or 700 K cannot substitute for near-ambient stacked microTEC device COP. Cryogenic/strong-magnet field cycle cannot be treated as phone battery-powered refrigerator. University press/selected works are not an annual thermal-output denominator.
