## 2026-10-04 — Method correction: joint authorship + foundational mathematical physics

### User-driven correction
Two evaluation rules were refined before the next research round.

### 1. Cross-border coauthorship
A China–Russia coauthored paper no longer automatically weakens the Russian capability claim.

Capability attribution now considers:
- pre-existing research lineage;
- experimental platform ownership;
- corresponding/senior/project leadership;
- method/model origin;
- independent follow-on capability.

Joint work can count simultaneously as:
- Russian capability evidence;
- collaboration-readiness evidence;
- knowledge-transfer evidence.

It is **not** automatically counted as independent Chinese capability without additional China-led/independent evidence.

### 2. Foundational mathematical physics
A new enabling layer is explicitly added:
- applied mathematics / mathematical physics;
- nonlinear stability;
- exact / reduced-order modeling;
- interfacial-wave / thermocapillary theory;
- advanced thermal-fluid numerical methods.

Preliminary Russian signals were registered in:
- evaporative-convection exact solutions;
- thermocapillary stability;
- thin-film microchannel modeling.

This is a **candidate**, not a final advantage.

### Decision correction
Kabov/BJUT joint authorship is reinterpreted as joint knowledge-transfer evidence; Kutateladze's pre-existing shear-film lineage/platform can still support Russian capability attribution.

### Research-state effect
**No progress increase: remains ~66%.**

This is a methodology/scope correction. The foundational layer must still undergo a systematic Russia–China comparison.
