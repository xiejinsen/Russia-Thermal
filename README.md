# Russia Mobile Thermal Technology & Collaboration Insight

**Continue in a new chat:** [CONTINUE_HERE.md](CONTINUE_HERE.md)

Evidence-backed research on Russian thermal-management capabilities that may contribute to next-generation **smartphone thermal management**, with tablets as a secondary reference platform.

Last repository review: **2026-10-05**

## Project objective

> Identify Russian thermal-management capabilities that are differentiated, mobile-relevant and complementary to strong China/mobile-industry capability, then convert the strongest signals into testable collaboration PoCs and 0–36 month R&D options.

The project is allowed to **kill or reframe** attractive hypotheses when stronger China/global evidence, smartphone constraints, IP crowding or system overhead invalidate them.

## Scope

- **Primary platform:** smartphones
- **Secondary platform:** tablets
- **Other domains:** mechanism / technology-source only
- **Preferred evidence:** 2023–2026
- **Main evidence window:** 2021–2026
- **Older evidence:** lineage, foundational physics, IP or lab capability

Strong thermal science alone is insufficient. A promoted direction needs direct mobile/chip relevance or a quantified transfer path.

## Evidence language

Decision-relevant statements distinguish:

- **Source fact**
- **Author/company claim**
- **Cross-source observation**
- **Analyst inference**
- **Unknown / Partner Request / Experiment Required**
- **Hypothesis**

Absence of public evidence is uncertainty, not proof of absence.

## Core research rules

1. Prefer primary papers, patents, official institutional sources and direct technical evidence.
2. Pressure-test promoted Russian claims against the strongest relevant China/global comparator.
3. Use institution-first naming:
   **Institution → Team / PI → Capability / mechanism → Decision state**.
4. Keep one independent evidence object per independently editable file where practical.
5. Keep coherent decision matrices/deep dives intact even when comparatively large.
6. Do not increase research completion for repository formatting or governance work.
7. A substantive research round is archived only after affected evidence, workstream, decision, progress and QA layers are refreshed.
