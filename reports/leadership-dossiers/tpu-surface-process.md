## China / global baseline

China/global work already strongly covers:
- laser-modified UTVC wick;
- biphilic/wettability patterns;
- structured surfaces;
- product-oriented ultra-thin VC engineering.

Therefore:
**generic laser/biphilic novelty is already killed.**

Residual:
TPU-specific process execution and potentially useful sealed-VC-compatible patterning.

## Best collaboration question

> Can TPU fabricate a thin-copper, low-outgassing, vacuum-stable, DI-water-compatible surface pattern that measurably improves confined rewetting/dryout behavior beyond a strong generic laser-treated reference?

## What to ask first

1. Minimum copper thickness and feature height?

6. Background-IP boundary around RU2812668C1 and related know-how?

## Main unresolved risks

- strongest patent is steel-focused;
- phone-thickness copper manufacturability unproven;
- field test is not VC reliability;
- environmental durability does not equal vacuum compatibility;
- public OEM collaboration evidence limited.
