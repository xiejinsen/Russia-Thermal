## Why this scan was done

A final conference/journal-driven scan was used to test whether the institution-first research had missed active Russian heat-transfer groups relevant to mobile thermal management.

- AVTFG — multiphase / phase transition / micro-nanosystems.
- Russian National Heat Transfer Conference (RNKT).

- High Temperature / Teplofizika Vysokikh Temperatur.

These venues are useful for discovery; publication/attendance alone is not partner evidence.

## Most important new finding

### Bauman MSTU / Nikolay Zubkov

This is the one genuinely new capability family that deserves visibility.

2025 work directly measures capillary pressure in copper heat-pipe wick structures produced by deformational cutting.

Why interesting:
- different manufacturing route from mesh / sintered powder / laser texturing;
- copper;
- capillary-porous structures;
- current work, not only historical lineage.

Why not promoted to Tier A/B:
- no sub-mm phone VC;
- no sealed-device performance;
- no demonstrated product integration.
