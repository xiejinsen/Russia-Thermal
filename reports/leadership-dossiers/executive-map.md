## Recommended management message

> Russia is not broadly ahead of China in smartphone thermal hardware.  
> The value is selective: Kutateladze Lab 1.3 offers failure-mechanism depth and unusually strong collaboration readiness; MPEI offers long-duration surface/capillary aging knowledge with a credible engineering realization chain; TPU is a process challenger.  
> China remains stronger in ultra-thin device engineering, manufacturing/product reliability and broad surface-process implementation.  
> The recommended collaboration model is complementary rather than substitutive.
