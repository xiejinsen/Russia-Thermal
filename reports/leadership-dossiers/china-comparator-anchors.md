## Failure / dryout / rewetting comparator

### Guangdong University of Technology line

Representative evidence:
- PAPER-CN-DRY-001

Public author/institution context:
- Jiangyou Long / Xiaozhu Xie line;
- Guangdong University of Technology is the principal public affiliation recovered for the capillary-fed boiling / hydrophilicity-degradation / steam-induced-rewetting work.

What it demonstrates:
- repeated dryout/boiling cycling;
- hydrophilicity degradation;
- steam-induced rewetting;
- capillary-fed wick failure physics.

Decision impact:
Kutateladze Lab 1.3 must not be positioned as uniquely strong in generic dryout/rewetting.
Its retained value is narrower: reversible-to-irreversible crisis / dry-spot interpretation.

## Laser / surface-process comparator

### East China Jiaotong University line

Representative evidence:
- PAPER-CN-TPU-001

Public affiliation recovered:
- School of Mechatronics and Vehicle Engineering, East China Jiaotong University.

What it demonstrates:
- laser ablation applied directly to UTVC wick structures;
- measured capillary improvement;
- fabricated UTVC device comparison;
- explicit manufacturability framing.

Decision impact:
generic laser/biphilic processing is not TPU-specific whitespace.
TPU remains relevant only if its process execution survives sealed, thin-copper, phone-relevant constraints.

## Shanghai Jiao Tong University — cross-cutting strong baseline

Representative evidence:
- OFFICIAL-SJTU-GONG-001
- OFFICIAL-SJTU-GONG-CN-001
- OFFICIAL-SJTU-LIU-001
- PAPER-CN-SJTU-DRY-001

What SJTU adds to the baseline:
- micro-/nanoscale liquid-vapor phase-change physics;
- dielectric-fluid evaporation in ultra-thin composite wick structures;
- near-junction thermal management for heterogeneous integrated chips;
- embedded thermal management for 3D chips;
- dryout-mitigation microchannel boiling;
- current HFE-7100 / silicon-based jet-pin-fin microchannel work.

Decision impact:
SJTU materially raises the China baseline from isolated mechanism papers to a broader mechanism-to-chip-engineering capability cluster.

It does not eliminate:
- Kutateladze Lab 1.3's narrower reversible-to-irreversible crisis / diagnostic residual;
- MPEI's actual multi-year engineered-surface / capillary-aging residual.

## Leadership takeaway

China is not a weak comparator baseline.

Across the three primary Russia candidates and newly discovered supporting nodes, the strongest China pressure comes from:
- **GDUT** — capillary-fed dryout / rewetting mechanism;
- **Chinese copper-water VC reliability ecosystem + Newidea / Shenzhen VC Thermal** — reliability / aging / product process;
- **East China Jiaotong University** — laser-modified UTVC wick / device path;
- **PKU / Fudan / SJTU / SEU** — embedded, package-integrated and high-heat-flux microchannel cooling;
- **BIT / XJTU / USTC** — ultra-thin two-phase hardware, capillary/wick and vapor-chamber comparators.

Therefore the Russia collaboration thesis should be framed as:
**narrow complementary mechanism/process value, not broad country-level superiority.**
