## Leadership answer

Russia should be considered a selective **scientific mechanism, measurement, and academic-method complement** to China's stronger phone-scale VC and manufacturing expertise, not a substitution for Chinese ultra-thin handset thermal solutions. Do not require a globally unique scientific discovery as a condition for *potential* collaboration. Require concrete complementarity, feasible research artifacts, and credible usefulness over existing Chinese and internal approaches.

**One first feasibility thesis (Kutateladze Lab1.3), one conditional evidence reserve (MPEI), and several topic-specific watch/method options, not equally ranked investments.** All partnership interest, data access, IP, and internal people/equipment/cost unknown. No outreach or tests authorized.

## Selection framework (without imaginary numerical scores)

Assess every team on: (i) verified current institution, lab and people; (ii) representative original results 2021–2026; (iii) demonstrated scientific mechanism/apparatus versus marketing; (iv) application distance to smartphone; (v) China baseline and potential complementarity; (vi) falsifiable output and ability to retrieve required data; (vii) traceable provenance/contradictory evidence.

Different **evidence-based management lanes** are not publication counts, university league rank, quantitative weighted scores or proof of Russia-China product superiority.

## What differs among top candidates

P1 is strongest **for a carefully bounded partner-feasibility request**, because the Lab1.3 roster and original mechanism/diagnostic paper line are coherent. This does not establish uniqueness, raw dataset access or better phone hardware.

P2 is stronger as a **conditional data-access option** than a generic longevity project; 42 months of observation in a large R410A thermosyphon must not be represented as handset VC survival.

The ITP Ural and ICM experiments make Russia's current candidate map broader than P1/P2, but they remain **knowledge/method reserves** until transfer scale and unique research artifact are demonstrated.

ITMO original basis is thinner than a mature phone hardware claim: current semiconductor capillary cooling is only a 3-page proceedings lead. TPU latest wettability report does NOT undo surface-process HOLD.

**Update 2026-10-09:** TSU completed an original-publication / current-lab bounded audit and remains an academic PCM/CFD **WATCH** rather than an unreviewed scout; see [TSU gate](../analysis/academic-team-mapping/tsu-phone-relevance-decision-gate-2026-10-09.md). NovSU / UUST remain scoped scouts with different coverage depth, not automatically inferior teams.
