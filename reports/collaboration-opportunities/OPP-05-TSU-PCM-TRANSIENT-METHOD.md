## Smartphone issue and China/internal counterfactual

Phone thermal throttling under burst/idle cycles could theoretically be altered by tiny latent-heat buffers; actual recovery/re-solidification, skin-temperature compliance, mass, thinness, leakage and aging are the key unanswered system costs. Chinese phone-scale thermal/PCM and internally available transient CFD approaches are the mandatory same-input alternatives; internal tools unknown.

## Falsifiable academic output if later authorized

An open/model-reproducible reduced-order PCM + heat-spreader transient thermal response with calibrated uncertainty, repeated pulses, complete melt/freeze, fixed package mass/volume and a fair no-PCM + Chinese/public PCM reference. Decision test: does a feasible geometry **change** peak temperature or allowed burst-duration/recovery tradeoff after packaging costs, not merely reduce temperature in idealized infinite buffer conditions?

**Promotion:** phone-like constraint validity and demonstrable nonredundant sensitivity/decision benefit. **Stop:** PCM never resets under real duty cycle, unacceptable volume/weight/leak risk, or no uplift vs locally available modeling. No external outreach and no implied physical phone prototype. No need to fund generic active microchannel research from a CFD paper.
