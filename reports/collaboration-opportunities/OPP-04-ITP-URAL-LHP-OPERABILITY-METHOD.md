## Smartphone issue and China/internal counterfactual

Question: could an LHP variant move heat in a size-constrained terminal without startup instability, dryout and unacceptable parasitics? Chinese 2025 miniature 0.7-mm/3.95-g mobile LHP device evidence is more directly relevant to form factor than ITP's 12-mm test evaporator; existing local modeling and Chinese collaborators must be used as baselines before Russian academic need can be demonstrated.
