## 1. Management decision

Do **not** treat MPEI as a second active bet.

Retain it as a conditional reliability-data opportunity because the unusual asset is:

> actual 42-month calendar operation followed by evidence of capillary/surface-state degradation while integral thermal performance remained comparatively stable.

China already has a stronger product-facing chain across oxidation physics, lifetime prediction, production QA, accelerated aging and VC aging-test methods.

Therefore the package is justified only if MPEI can provide information that those baselines do not.

## 2. Decision question

Does the long-duration MPEI evidence contain a recoverable precursor or degradation-state variable that adds predictive / diagnostic value **after** oxygen chemistry, process state and standard thermal metrics are already accounted for?

## 3. What the partner contributes

Publicly supported residual:

- long-calendar engineered-surface operation;
- hierarchical evaporator surface experience;
- post-operation capillary / wetting-state assessment;
- thermosyphon engineering context;
- current MPEI / Newfrost implementation lineage.

This is rare **calendar-duration mechanism evidence**, not a proven phone observer.

## 4. What we must keep internally

Internal ownership:

- copper-water phone-VC geometry;
- product oxidation / process controls;
- accelerated-aging plan;
- production QA;
- product telemetry;
- health-state estimator;
- reliability model;
- manufacturing;
- foreground IP.

## 5. Contact condition

Do not initiate a dedicated collaboration conversation unless there is a reasonable chance of accessing one or more of:

- time-resolved operating history;
- periodic capillary / wetting measurements;
- raw thermal histories rather than only final summary values;
- post-mortem surface / chemistry characterization;
- repeat samples or comparable surfaces;
- sufficient metadata to align degradation state with exposure history.

If only the published 42-month endpoint is available, keep the package as literature knowledge.

## 6. First engagement questions

1. Was the 42-month system monitored continuously or periodically, and which raw channels remain available?
2. Are intermediate surface / capillary-state measurements available, or only beginning/end measurements?
3. Are multiple samples / devices available to assess repeatability?
4. What maintenance, refill, shutdown, contamination or environmental events occurred during the 42 months?
5. What surface chemistry / morphology characterization exists before and after operation?
6. Can the team reproduce a related experiment in copper-water or another phone-relevant sealed configuration?
7. What role did Newfrost play in fabrication, assembly, operation and post-mortem analysis?
8. What data can be shared and under which constraints?

## 7. Minimum useful study

A future bounded study should compare:

### Domestic/product baseline
- oxygen / oxidation state;
- production process QA;
- standard thermal resistance / temperature metrics;
- accelerated-aging history.

### MPEI-informed candidate features
- capillary imbibition / wickability state;
- wetting / contact-line state where available;
- morphology changes;
- other long-duration surface-state variables.

Question:

> Does the MPEI-informed state predict remaining transport margin or future degradation earlier / better than the baseline?

## 8. Promotion gate

Promote only if:

- accessible long-duration data are sufficiently time-resolved or otherwise discriminating;
- a reproducible precursor exists;
- it adds value after chemistry/process controls;
- or a phone-relevant copper-water reproduction demonstrates a new degradation mode not captured by domestic baselines.

## 9. Kill / downgrade gate

Move to WATCH / reference-only if:

- evidence is only a terminal R410A / large-groove endpoint;
- no precursor signal can be recovered;
- the effect is explained by existing oxidation/process metrics;
- no phone-relevant reproduction is feasible;
- data access is too limited for falsification.

## 10. IP / collaboration boundary

Best collaboration role:
- reliability mechanism interpretation;
- long-duration data analysis;
- selected reproduction experiments.

Not:
- ownership of our production QA or product health model.
