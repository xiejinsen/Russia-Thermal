# 1. Decision in one sentence

**Russia should be treated as a selective mechanism / reliability complement to China, not as a broad smartphone-thermal technology leader.**

The practical consequence is equally simple:

> **Advance one narrowly defined collaboration thesis, preserve one conditional reliability reserve, and keep the remaining Russian directions as reserves / watch / hold unless new matched phone-scale evidence appears.**

# 3. Why only P1 survives as a Strategic Candidate

## Surviving thesis

Kutateladze Lab 1.3 may add value as a:

**mechanism-resolved failure-state ground-truth / model-falsification partner**

around:
- reversible vs irreversible crisis behavior;
- confinement-dependent crisis modes;
- topology-linked drying-front behavior;
- spatial / temporal dry-spot evolution.

## Why this is not a generic dryout bet

Strong comparator evidence already covers:
- China dryout mitigation;
- China microchannel redistribution;
- internal pressure / temperature sensing;
- saturation-state dynamics;
- boiling-aware vapor-chamber models;
- generic external dryout observers.

Therefore the only credible residual is:

> **Does the Russian mechanism taxonomy expose repeatable state information that changes a phone-relevant validation, protection or margin decision beyond those baselines?**

## Evidence pressure

Current reachable evidence chain:
- **22 sources**
- **5 Russia**
- **17 China/global comparators**
- **18 Deep Reads**

This is why P1 is a bounded hypothesis rather than a broad technology claim.

---

# 4. Why P2 is Reserve rather than a second bet

MPEI contributes unusual public evidence:

- approximately **42 months actual calendar operation**;
- post-operation capillary / wetting-state degradation;
- long-duration engineered-surface experience.

But China already has a stronger product-facing reliability chain:
- oxidation failure physics;
- oxygen-linked life prediction;
- production oxidation grading;
- accelerated mobile aging;
- VC aging-test prior art.

So MPEI is valuable only if collaboration unlocks something not present in the public endpoint:

- time-resolved long-duration history;
- repeatability;
- intermediate capillary / surface-state data;
- post-mortem chemistry / morphology;
- or a phone-relevant copper-water reproduction.

If only the published endpoint is available:

> **do not open an active project.**

---

# 5. Why P3 is Hold

TPU shows real capability in:
- laser surface processing;
- wettability control;
- boiling / evaporation diagnostics;
- process execution.

However China already demonstrates:
- laser-treated UTVC wicks;
- oxidation / corrosion / laser comparisons;
- 0.39 mm integrated UTVC geometry;
- 0.4 mm wettability-patterned UTVCs;
- biphilic / self-driven VC prior art;
- hierarchical laser-wick prior art.

Therefore:

> **laser, biphilic and spatial wettability concepts are not sufficient differentiation.**

Reopen TPU only if it proves a **process-specific increment** under matched phone-relevant conditions, such as:
- lower outgassing;
- better sealed durability;
- better transient rewetting;
- higher yield;
- better target-fluid compatibility;
- unique diagnostics.

Until then: **no proactive engagement.**

---

# 6. Russia vs China — the correct strategic framing

## China remains stronger in

- ultra-thin VC / heat-spreader engineering;
- copper-water device manufacturing;
- capillary structure / wick engineering;
- reliability and production QA;
- mobile microchannel integration;
- phone-system thermal integration;
- product-facing UTVC modeling.

## Russia retains selective value in

- mechanism-resolved boiling-crisis physics;
- extreme-confinement two-phase mechanism studies;
- rare long-calendar surface / capillary-state evidence;
- selected diagnostics / experimental ground truth;
- exact / stability-oriented analytical modeling;
- niche mechanism / IP reserves.

The collaboration model should therefore be:

**Russia mechanism/data**  
×  
**China/domestic product baseline**  
×  
**our internal phone architecture/model/validation**

—not Russia replacing the domestic product stack.

---

# 7. What must remain internally owned

Even if collaboration proceeds, our team should retain ownership of:

- phone system architecture;
- package / thickness / geometry constraints;
- production working-fluid and sealing decisions;
- product telemetry;
- final failure / health model;
- controller;
- manufacturing integration;
- phone validation;
- foreground product IP.

Russia should contribute only the narrow external control point that survives comparator pressure.
