## 1. Executive conclusion — what should leadership decide?

**Russia merits selective access to specialized thermal research methods and scientifically discriminating datasets, not a generic bet on better smartphone cooling hardware.** China and international research already cover thin two-phase devices, engineered wicks, dryout mitigation, transient state models, oxidation-related VC failure and lifetime methods. The defensible Russian residual is conditional scientific value in **failure-mechanism ground truth** and **long-calendar exposure histories**, where access and incremental usefulness are **not yet established**.

Therefore authorize *at most a controlled feasibility discussion* of **P1 Kutateladze Institute Lab 1.3** (Pavlenko/Surtaev), conditional on future permission; keep **P2 Moscow Power Engineering Institute (MPEI)** (Ivanov lineage) as **data-dependent reserve**; keep **TPU wettability/process line HOLD** and ITP Ural, TSU, ICM as method WATCH/RESERVE. No approval for external outreach, paid PoC, intellectual property transfer, data license or hardware trial is recorded.

This is a **decision on which questions deserve permission to investigate**, not a recommendation that Russian technology already outperforms Chinese phone thermal solutions.

## 3. Why P1 is the only active *scientific feasibility thesis*

**Research anchor.** Surtaev, Malakhov, Perminov, Polovnikov and Pavlenko, “Investigation of heat transfer, critical heat flux and dry spots dynamics during boiling of dielectric fluids HFE-7100 and Novec 649,” *International Journal of Heat and Mass Transfer* (2026), DOI [10.1016/j.ijheatmasstransfer.2025.127855](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijheatmasstransfer.2025.127855). [Original canonical record](../01-evidence/papers/PAPER-RU-DRY-001/README.md); [apparatus-bounded supported claim](../02-claims/CLM-PAV-002.md).

**What happened:** research observes dry-spot size/duration/spatial evolution via optical and infrared methods under dielectric boiling up to irreversible crisis. **What did not happen:** no instrumented sealed ~submillimeter copper–water smartphone VC, no proven improvement to commercial online observer, no licensed raw ground-truth dataset.

**Why a partner rather than generic academic reading:** potentially repeatable regime labels distinguishing reversible/irreversible crisis **if** definitions, timing alignment, confound controls, replication, and data rights exist. The deliverable is a *validation/falsification artifact* (mechanism ontology + controlled labeled experiments + held-out cases), not a co-designed Russian smartphone vapor chamber.

**Strong counterfactual:** SJTU Chinese experimental counter-flow microchannel dryout mitigation [Li et al., DOI 10.11949/0438-1157.20230936](https://doi.org/10.11949/0438-1157.20230936); global transient wick saturation/time-to-dryout and recovery [DOI 10.1016/j.ijheatmasstransfer.2025.126837](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijheatmasstransfer.2025.126837); global internal pressure/vapor-temperature pulsed dryout [DOI 10.1016/j.ijheatmasstransfer.2025.127222](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijheatmasstransfer.2025.127222). None is an apples-to-apples phone comparison. They establish that **generic mitigation, temporal modeling and internal observation are not unique Russian inventions**.

**P1 progressive gates:** (G0) authorized feasibility discussion only; (G1) prove the raw labels/histories exist and rights allow use; (G2) verify repeatable distinctions and avoid leakage between runs; (G3) matched held-out testing against China/global/internal physics-aware and simple external-sensor baseline; (G4) phone-observable state or margin-relevant decision gain; (G5) only then consider a real phone research PoC. **Stop** if G1 fails or if no incremental held-out decision value.

## 4. Why P2 remains reserve, not an independent investment

**Research anchor.** N. S. Ivanov, “Long-term operational stability of a hierarchical evaporator surface in a two-phase thermosyphon,” (2026), DOI [10.1016/j.pes.2026.100314](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pes.2026.100314), [canonical source](../01-evidence/papers/PAPER-RU-AGE-001/README.md). Reported **42 calendar months of periodic operation** of R410A on microgroove + Al2O3 hierarchical surface, comparatively stable integral thermal response with diminished capillary imbibition documented post-run. The existence of a hidden *chronological precursor* is not demonstrated: [CLM-MPEI-008 is OPEN, not supported](../02-claims/CLM-MPEI-008.md).

**China pressure:** real copper–water VC oxygen-linked degradation [DOI 10.1016/j.applthermaleng.2025.125619](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.applthermaleng.2025.125619); accelerated oxygen-footprint chemistry-to-predicted-service-life study [DOI 10.1016/j.applthermaleng.2026.131067](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.applthermaleng.2026.131067). Reported model-converted lifetime is *not* 13 years of real calendar testing. Neither set of apparatus is equivalent to the other; China nonetheless provides a more direct copper–water VC failure/QA path.

**P2 gates:** first require actual intermediate dated thermal/capillary/wetting/chemistry series, repeatability and usage metadata; isolate added degradation physics versus Chinese oxygen/oxidation controls; test relevance to phone copper–water sealing. **If only beginning/end observations are available, keep documentary reserve.** No early-warning or remaining-life model project should be commissioned.

## 6. 2027–2029 — four research questions, not forecasts

1. **Failure-state semantics:** Can spatiotemporal dryout/crisis labels, across repeated loads, distinguish a reversible event from impending irreversible margin loss and change phone validation? **P1**; require held-out incremental evidence.
2. **Health under stable outward thermal resistance:** Is there an observable and reproducible capillary/wetting deterioration precursor that survives oxygen and process confound controls and matters before thermal degradation? **P2 reserve**; chronology gate first.
3. **Transient thermal buffer + recovery:** Do PCM/foam concepts help real burst–idle duty cycles once phase reset, added mass/thickness, leakage, skin temperature and full-system comparison are included? **TSU method watch**, not a new product bet.
4. **Ultra-thin two-phase return limits:** Can capillary-return models and controlled operating-limit studies define usable bounds within actual phone geometry, material, orientation and manufacturing tolerances? **ITP/ICM methods watch**, no extrapolated proof.

See [original-source-bounded trend synthesis](../analysis/trends/q07-phone-thermal-2027-2029-evidence-synthesis-2026-10-09.md).

## 7. Collaborate versus China/vendor/in-house — decision rule

**Do not buy a method just because Russia publishes it.** Require a specific nonredundant artifact with (a) original authorship and usable scope, (b) legal/practical access, (c) independent repeatability and uncertainty, (d) matched strongest China/global/internal baseline, (e) a measurable changed design, calibration, guard band or validation decision, and (f) feasible phone-scale pathway. Internal architecture, firmware/thermal control, production integration, product instrumentation and IP ownership remain internal unless separately authorized.

Quantitative ROI, available internal headcount, Russian partner interest and licensing price are **UNKNOWN**. Do not invent a cost/benefit number. Leadership's present decision is only whether an authorized P1 exploratory contact gate is worthwhile.

## 9. Accepted unknowns, epistemic boundaries and reopen rules

**Accepted as outside public-research completion:** no phone prototype data, inaccessible raw experimental data, publication-time author roles not individually confirmed, partner willingness, IP licensing, internal staffing/cost inventory, and national exhaustive institution census. These must be disclosed, not silently assumed.

**Critical disambiguations:** current lab roster V. E. Zhukov ≠ proven historical paper V. I. Zhukov; official Denis A. Nesterov ≠ proven publisher Dmitry A. Nesterov. NSU LabPET Naumov cannot be substituted as NSU EITP current head. TPU 2026 biphilic IJHMT article ≠ distinct 2026 *Surfaces and Interfaces* coating paper.

**Reopen only if:** a new primary study disproves P1/P2 mechanism interpretations, an official author affiliation changes priority partner ownership, a controlled comparator shows a material unique Russian artifact, or authorization gives access to data/tests relevant to an explicit decision gate. Otherwise **stop accumulating papers**.
