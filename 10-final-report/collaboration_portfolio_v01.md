## Why the ranking is useful

The three candidates now test three different hypotheses:

- Pavlenko: **high-flux mechanism survives scaling**
- MPEI: **long-life surface survives high-flux miniaturization**
- TPU: **precision wettability survives sealed manufacturing**

This creates a more informative Stage-0 portfolio than testing three variants of the same surface idea.
