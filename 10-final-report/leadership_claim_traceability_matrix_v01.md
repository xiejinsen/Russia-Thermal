## 1. Claim-type rule

Every management claim is one of:
- **SOURCE FACT** — directly supported by paper/patent/official source;
- **ANALYST INFERENCE** — reasoned synthesis from multiple sources;
- **DECISION** — project portfolio judgment;
- **UNKNOWN / GAP** — unresolved;
- **INTERNAL TARGET** — project-defined PoC/Stage-0 threshold, not a source fact.

Never convert ANALYST INFERENCE to SOURCE FACT, UNKNOWN to a negative fact, or INTERNAL TARGET to a literature result.
