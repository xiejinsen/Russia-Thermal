## 1. Why confidence is split

A single score is misleading. Each direction is scored on four independent dimensions:

1. **Evidence Confidence** — how strongly the retained Russia capability claim is supported and traceable.
2. **Phone Transfer Confidence** — how much current evidence supports transfer into smartphone geometry, fluid, process and system constraints.
3. **Partner Readiness** — whether a current active team/capability exists for Stage-0 discussion. This is not contract readiness.
4. **IP Clarity** — how well background IP and plausible narrow foreground are mapped. This is not legal FTO.

Scale: **HIGH / MEDIUM-HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW-MEDIUM / LOW**

## 4. Presentation rule

The leadership visual should show four small badges, not one composite score.

Example:
- Evidence: HIGH
- Phone: LOW-MEDIUM
- Partner: MEDIUM-HIGH
- IP: MEDIUM

This deliberately communicates: **strong scientific evidence does not mean strong product readiness.**

## 5. Investment interpretation

Stage-0 spend is justified when Evidence Confidence is MEDIUM-HIGH/HIGH, the PoC is cheap and discriminating, and there is a clear Kill gate—even if Phone Transfer is still LOW-MEDIUM.
