Final version should answer, in one page:

- What is the thermal problem worth solving?
- Why is Russia relevant?
- What is already strong in China / industry?
- What are the 2–4 surviving strategic bets?
- What should we do in the next 6 months?
- What should we explicitly not do?
- What evidence would change the decision?

## 4. Final 6-month asks — template

The final version should state:
- partner to contact;
- exact information to request;
- coupon/sample deliverable;
- PoC cost/time;
- internal owner/capability;
- go/no-go review point.

## 5. Decision confidence

Final version must label each major recommendation:
- HIGH
- MEDIUM
- LOW

and separately show:
- evidence maturity;
- engineering maturity;
- partner readiness;
- IP clarity.

A recommendation cannot be called HIGH confidence merely because the academic evidence is strong.
