# Three-Year R&D Roadmap v0.1

Last reviewed: 2026-10-04

> **DRAFT ROADMAP FRAME — TIMING IS NOT FINAL**

## Roadmap principle

The roadmap follows **evidence maturity**, not calendar optimism.

A direction moves forward only after its prior gate passes.

---

## 0–6 months — falsify and select

Objectives:
- close Stage-0 manufacturing/process blockers;
- obtain partner data / coupons;
- verify thin-geometry transfer;
- verify product-path fluid transfer;
- clarify background IP;
- run Stage-0 coupon matrix;
- select at most two Stage-1 arms.

Deliverables:
- partner data-request + Stage-0 experiment packets;
- sample/coupon specs;
- normalized Stage-0 data;
- updated patent/IP position;
- GO / NO-GO decision.

Primary question:
> Which Russian mechanism still creates measurable value after real phone geometry and process constraints?

---

## 6–18 months — device proof and joint IP

Objectives:
- sealed Stage-1 VC/device comparison;
- strong China-style reference;
- transient/moving-hotspot testing;
- reliability and process validation;
- foreground-IP drafting where justified;
- early package/VC/frame co-design.

Deliverables:
- sealed device demonstrator;
- thermal Pareto comparison;
- 500+ cycle evidence;
- orientation/process sensitivity;
- patentable control point if real;
- product-architecture integration concept.

Primary question:
> Does the collaboration move the device frontier, not just a coupon metric?

---

## 18–36 months — architecture/product direction

Objectives:
- package / board / VC / frame integration;
- manufacturing process compatibility;
- supplier path;
- extended reliability;
- system control co-design where useful;
- product-line fit.

Deliverables:
- product-relevant prototype;
- manufacturing process window;
- validated IP portfolio;
- final partner model;
- product/program recommendation.

Primary question:
> Is this a repeatable product capability and strategic control point?

---

## Portfolio-management rule

At every gate:
- kill weak directions;
- reallocate resources;
- keep one reserve mechanism;
- avoid carrying technically interesting but non-differentiated work for prestige reasons.
