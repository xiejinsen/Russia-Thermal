# 1. Product problem

Smartphone ultra-thin two-phase spreaders can fail not because average thermal resistance is poor, but because a local dry region crosses from a **recoverable state** into **irreversible dryout / thermal runaway**.

The product question is:

> Can we predict and shift the irreversible-dryout boundary in a phone-scale wick before the hotspot becomes unrecoverable?

This matters under:
- moving hotspots;
- short high-power bursts;
- small liquid inventory;
- <0.5 mm-class two-phase structures;
- skin-temperature and reliability limits.

---

# 2. Why Russia / why this team

Kutateladze / Pavlenko–Surtaev–Shvetsov–Zhukov has an unusually coherent current line around:

- dielectric-liquid boiling crisis;
- reversible → irreversible dry-spot transition;
- high-speed IR / reflected-light diagnostics;
- dry-area / contact-line statistics;
- layer-height-driven crisis-mode change;
- directional drying-front dynamics on modified surfaces.

This is **not** evidence that Russia is generally better at dryout/rewetting.

The value is narrower:

> **deep diagnostics of the transition from recoverable local dryout to irreversible boiling crisis.**

# 6. Phone-transfer gap

Current blockers are concrete:

- exact transferable modification recipe is **partner-only**;
- published mesh/process geometry is not yet proven on ~60–100 μm-class phone wick;
- open-area / permeability penalty is unknown on fine transfer;
- HFE-7100 is a mechanism bridge, **not** the future product-fluid default;
- copper + DI-water behavior is unproven;
- vacuum / degassing / sealing / cycle retention is unproven;
- background-IP / field-right boundary must be discussed.

The biggest risk is:

> a beautiful boiling-mechanism result that disappears when the structure becomes thin, copper-based, DI-water compatible and manufacturable.

# 8. Success / Kill

## Internal Stage-0 promotion target

Must first show:
- process technically plausible on <=100 μm-class phone metal/wick;
- geometry/process repeatability;
- no catastrophic permeability penalty.

Then require at least one thermal improvement versus strong matched reference:

- **>=15% lower evaporator thermal resistance; OR**
- **>=20% higher dryout/capillary limit; OR**
- **>=20% faster rewetting;**

plus at least one failure-mechanism improvement:

- materially later irreversible-dryout onset; OR
- lower dry-spot propagation at matched heat flux; OR
- better post-cycle wetting/capillary retention.

**These are internal screening thresholds, not literature claims.**

## Kill / reframe

Kill phone-transfer thesis if:
- modification intrinsically requires thick mesh/geometry;
- fine transfer destroys open area/permeability;
- adhesion/process stability fails;
- benefit exists only in legacy HFE-7100;
- copper/DI-water route loses failure-boundary advantage;
- strong domestic reference matches/exceeds irreversible-dryout and recovery behavior.

If physics remains interesting but transfer fails:
**reframe to mechanism-only.**
