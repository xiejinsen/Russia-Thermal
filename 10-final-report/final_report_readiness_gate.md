# Final Report Readiness Gate

Last reviewed: 2026-10-04

## Purpose

Prevent attractive but immature research directions from being promoted into final recommendations.

A direction cannot become a **Final Strategic Bet** until all mandatory gates pass. **G0 is a hard precondition**: general thermal excellence without mobile-terminal/chip relevance cannot enter the final portfolio.

## Mandatory gates

| Gate | Required evidence | Pass condition |
|---|---|---|
| **G0 — Mobile/chip relevance** | 00 / 01 / 08 | direct mobile/chip thermal relevance OR a credible quantified transfer path |
| G1 — Real phone problem | 01 / 08 | problem tied to user/system/product constraint |
| G2 — Current Russia capability | 03 / 04 | active team + recent primary/official evidence |
| G3 — Strong comparator | 07 / 08 | modern China/global baseline, not weak control |
| G4 — Phone transfer | 01 / 08 | geometry/power/noise/reliability/manufacturing path |
| G5 — Falsification | 08 | explicit success and kill criteria |
| G6 — IP / prior art | 05 | broad crowded claims removed; plausible narrow control point |
| G7 — Partner readiness | 04 / 09 | current lead + role/contact + capability + unresolved issues |
| G8 — PoC | 09 | smallest discriminating PoC defined |
| G9 — Traceability | evidence | original sources locally linked and centrally indexed |
| G10 — Unknowns | QA | unresolved evidence visible and decision impact stated |

## Final-report states

### PRIMARY BET
All mandatory gates pass at a level sufficient for near-term investment.

### RESERVE BET
Mechanism is attractive but one or more engineering/partner/IP gates remain.

### WATCH
Evidence is interesting but insufficient for planned investment.

### KILLED / REFRAMED
Strong evidence shows the broad thesis is not strategically differentiated or does not survive constraints.

### UNRESOLVED
Search/evidence is incomplete. Do not convert uncertainty into a positive or negative conclusion.

## Finalization rule

The final report must not be labeled complete until:

1. at least one Primary Bet or an explicit "no collaboration bet justified" conclusion exists;
2. every Primary/Reserve Bet has passed traceability QA;
3. all final partner names have fresh role verification;
4. IP-sensitive claims are reviewed to the required legal/technical depth;
5. roadmap gates reflect actual PoC maturity;
6. executive summary, full report and evidence appendix agree.

## Update trigger

Update this gate only when:
- a major evidence gap closes;
- a direction changes Tier/state;
- partner readiness changes;
- PoC result becomes available;
- IP conclusion changes.
