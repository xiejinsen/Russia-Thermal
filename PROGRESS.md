## High-signal findings so far

1. **Kutateladze Institute of Thermophysics SB RAS (Novosibirsk)** is already a high-priority candidate because its current/recent work spans boiling/evaporation, dielectric fluids, microchannels, modified heat-transfer surfaces and synthetic-jet heat transfer.
2. Its official laboratory page records technical cooperation with **Huawei** on boiling heat-transfer enhancement in 2021–2023 and further Huawei-related consulting in 2024–2026. This is direct evidence that some of its capabilities are considered relevant by a major mobile/electronics company.
3. **Institute of Thermal Physics UB RAS (Yekaterinburg)** has a distinctive loop-heat-pipe lineage, including flat evaporators, multi-source heat transport, flexible LHPs and recent 2025 work.
4. **TsAGI / PNRPU / CIAM** form a potentially valuable Russian aeroacoustic/fan-noise capability cluster. Mobile-scale transfer remains unproven and must be tested rather than assumed.
5. **SPbU Electrophysics** has an active ionic-wind/EHD modeling line; direct smartphone cooling evidence has not yet been found, so this is an exploratory cross-domain candidate.
6. Kutateladze researchers published a 2023 experimental/numerical study of **impinging synthetic-jet heat transfer**, making the institute relevant not only to passive/two-phase thermal management but also to emerging active cooling.
