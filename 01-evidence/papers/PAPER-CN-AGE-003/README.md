Title:
Detection and grading of oxidation for copper-water heat pipe wicks based on the machine learning methods

Authors:
Xiaojun Guo; Yong Li; Guangwen Huang; Rui Tang; Fan Yang; Zhifeng Xin; Bowen Wu

Direct reported results:
- production-oriented oxidation grading is developed for copper-water heat-pipe wicks;
- wick color characteristics, capillary climb and completed heat-pipe thermal performance are all used to characterize oxidation degree;
- color-feature-only grading exceeds 80% average accuracy;
- image-based grading exceeds 85% average accuracy;
- a combined color + convolution-image feature model with attention exceeds 92% overall classification accuracy;
- highest reported training and testing classification accuracies exceed 97% and 95%, respectively;
- the authors state the resulting accuracy meets actual production needs.

Boundary:
manufacturing QA comparator rather than an online in-field health observer;
not an exact multi-year engineered-surface analogue and not proof that all long-term degradation is oxidation.
