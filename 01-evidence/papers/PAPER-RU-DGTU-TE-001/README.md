Title:
Experimental studies of a thermoelectric device for cooling discrete electric radio elements

Authors:
Asiyat M. Ibragimova; Oleg V. Evdulov

Journal:
Journal of Instrument Engineering, 66(8), 704-710, 2023

Affiliation:
Dagestan State Technical University

Direct reported results:
- a laboratory thermoelectric cooling device was built and tested for a simulator of a discrete radioelectronic element;
- the design used standard thermoelectric modules arranged in primary and additional cooling sections;
- full-scale tests measured temperature evolution versus thermoelectric-module current and heat-source power;
- the authors identify Cryotherm DRIFT-1.5 modules as suitable for the tested design;
- the reported device reduced the simulated electronic-element temperature to approximately 272 K at a supply current near 5 A;
- reported time to reach the operating condition was approximately 90 s;
- reported disagreement between experimental and calculated data was no more than approximately 10%.

Boundary:
this is direct experimental electronics-cooling evidence, but it does not establish mobile-device COP, battery-energy viability, millimeter-scale package thickness, hot-side heat rejection integration or smartphone reliability.
