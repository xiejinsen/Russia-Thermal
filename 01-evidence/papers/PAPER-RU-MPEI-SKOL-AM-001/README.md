Title:
Engineering a loop thermosyphon with 3D-printed developed microstructured surface of the evaporator

Authors:
Yu. V. Lyulin; M. M. Afonin; D. G. Firsov; S. A. Evlashin; A. V. Dedov

Affiliations:
- MPEI: Lyulin, Dedov
- Skolkovo Institute of Science and Technology: Afonin, Firsov, Evlashin

Direct reported results:
- flat-evaporator loop thermosyphon with a 3D-printed developed pillar-array heat-transfer surface;
- ethanol working fluid;
- tested from 25 to 530 W;
- reported minimum total thermal resistance about 0.18 K/W at the maximum tested heat input;
- structured evaporator improved device efficiency versus the smooth configuration.

Boundary:
device is not smartphone-scale; geometry, volume, orientation and working fluid differ from phone UTVC requirements.
