Title:
The Hydrodynamic Crisis of Nucleate Boiling in a Horizontal Thin Layer of Dielectric Liquid HFE-7100

Authors:
Zhukov; Pavlenko

Direct reported results:
- HFE-7100 horizontal liquid layers from 1.5 to 35 mm;
- crisis mode changes with liquid-layer height;
- below a critical height, surface drying becomes the limiting crisis mode;
- a dry spot appears near the transition condition.

Boundary:
millimeter-scale open boiling mechanism evidence, not phone-scale confinement.
