Title:
Calculation model of thermoelectric system for cooling discrete semiconductor devices

Authors:
Oleg V. Evdulov; Asiyat M. Ibragimova; Timur Yu. Magomedov

Journal:
Herald of Dagestan State Technical University. Technical Sciences, 49(2), 9-17, 2022

Affiliation:
Dagestan State Technical University

Direct reported results:
- the work models a multi-section thermoelectric cooling system removing heat from both upper and lower surfaces of discrete semiconductor devices;
- the finite-element model evaluates transient and steady thermal behavior of the semiconductor-device / thermoelectric-system assembly;
- the reported design study covers semiconductor-device heat loads up to approximately 75 W;
- the paper reports strong electrical-input overhead for the evaluated standard thermoelectric module, making system efficiency a central transfer constraint.

Boundary:
supports design/modeling continuity around electronics cooling; it does not demonstrate mobile-device energy efficiency or thin-package integration.
