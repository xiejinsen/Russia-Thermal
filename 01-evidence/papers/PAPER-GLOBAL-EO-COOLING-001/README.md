Title:
Closed-loop electroosmotic microchannel cooling system for VLSI circuits

Authors:
Linan Jiang; James C. Mikkelsen; Jae-Mo Koo; David Huber; Shuhuai Yao; Lian Zhang; Peng Zhou; James G. Maveety; Ravi Prasher; Juan G. Santiago; Thomas W. Kenny; Kenneth E. Goodson

Direct reported results:
- demonstrated a closed-loop two-phase microchannel electronics-cooling system driven by an electroosmotic pump;
- the silicon heat exchanger used 100 micrometer-class channels attached to a 1 cm2 test chip;
- the electroosmotic pump reported up to 160 kPa backpressure and 7 ml/min flow under its own tested chemistry/geometry;
- the closed loop removed 38 W with about 2 W pump power and reported junction-to-ambient thermal resistance near 2.5 K/W.

Decision use:
strong global prior-art pressure against treating electroosmotic pumping for electronics cooling as a novel Russian control point.

Boundary:
the system is old VLSI / microchannel work, not a smartphone heat pipe; its pressure, voltage, fluid chemistry, volume and thermal metrics are not directly comparable to NovSU's current prototype.
