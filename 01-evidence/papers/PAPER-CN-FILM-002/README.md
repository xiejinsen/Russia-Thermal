Title:
Manipulating thin film boiling to achieve record-breaking high heat flux

Authors:
Yuxiang Zhang; Xuan Zhao; Jiahua Li; Qingyang Wang; Dawen Zhong; Deyin Zheng; Xiaoze Du; Lin Chen

Direct reported results:
- DI-water thin-film boiling through a nanoporous membrane / Pt-coated heater platform;
- bilateral fixation improves sample pressure-bearing capacity;
- constant-pressure operation with bilateral fixation reaches roughly 1400 W/cm2;
- asynchronous and simultaneous pressure/heating trajectories raise achieved CHF into roughly 1500–2000 W/cm2;
- record reported CHF: 2074 W/cm2;
- the paper cites a theoretical TFB upper limit around 5000 W/cm2;
- pressures above roughly 240 kPa can place the membrane sample under severe mechanical loading;
- the test infrastructure includes externally controlled pressurized liquid supply.

Boundary:
strong headline heat-flux comparator, but not phone-scale system proof; pump/compressor power, acoustic cost, loop volume and packaging are not closed by the headline CHF result.
