Title:
Experimental research on the heat transfer performance of ultra-thin vapor chambers with composite wicks for electronics cooling

Authors:
Tengqing Liu; Yaokang Zhang; Shuangfeng Wang; Qianxi Zhang; Huifeng Kang

Direct reported results:
- five 0.4 mm-thick SWM-based UTVC variants are fabricated and tested;
- the study directly compares bare spiral-woven-mesh (SWM), screen-mesh composite (SWM-SM), and wettability-patterned (SWM-WP) architectures;
- both normal and enhanced SWM variants are combined with WP / SM branches;
- wick structure and device orientation are tested;
- the paper explicitly targets portable-electronics cooling and sub-0.4 mm UTVC wick design;
- wettability-patterned surface modification is therefore demonstrated inside an actual ultra-thin sealed vapor chamber, not only on an open droplet coupon.

Decision use:
direct target-system comparator against TPU spatial-wettability / routing differentiation.

Boundary:
does not establish that every possible TPU pattern/process is matched, nor does it prove identical transient rewetting or durability behavior.
