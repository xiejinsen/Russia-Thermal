Title:
Experimental study and modeling of transient operation modes of the microprocessors cooling system of on-board ship systems

Authors:
Aleksandr I. Andreev; Aleksandr E. Semenov; Nikolay D. Shishkin; Roman A. Ilyin

Affiliation:
Astrakhan State Technical University

Direct reported results:
- experiments used an Intel Core i7-13700K under stress load;
- the cooling system used distilled-water liquid cooling, a mini refrigeration compressor, a pump and microchannel heat exchangers;
- the processor-side microchannel heat exchanger used approximately 100 um channels;
- reported cooling-system response time was approximately 50-90 s during boosted operation;
- the paper reports cooling capacity up to approximately 250 W for the studied microchannel/minicompressor system and analyzes control/synchronization of cooling response with processor load.

Boundary:
direct active electronics-cooling experiment, but the demonstrated system is a high-power onboard-computing / processor setup rather than a mobile-terminal form factor; compressor/pump volume, acoustic, power, weight and integration constraints remain open.
