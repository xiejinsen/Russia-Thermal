Title:
The influence of microchannel internal structure elements spatial distribution on the hydrodynamic characteristics of the fluid flow

Authors:
N. B. Fatkullina; S. S. Kurbanova; O. A. Solnyshkina

Journal:
Technical Physics Letters / Pisma v Zhurnal Tekhnicheskoi Fiziki, 50(23), 46-49, 2024

Affiliation:
Ufa University of Science and Technology

Direct reported results:
- the paper numerically studies liquid flow through a microchannel containing non-deformable microscale structural elements;
- it evaluates how spatial distribution and specific surface area affect permeability and flow fields;
- the work is funded by RSF project 24-19-00697, which is the current UUST structured-microfluidics / electronics-cooling program.

Boundary:
this paper establishes hydrodynamic design lineage for structured microchannels; heat-removal performance and phone-level pumping/package tradeoffs require the broader project evidence and later experiments.
