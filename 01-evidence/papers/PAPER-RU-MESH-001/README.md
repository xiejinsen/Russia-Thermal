Title:
Electrochemical Modification of the Metal Mesh Surface for Heat Transfer Enhancement during Boiling of a Thin Layer of HFE-7100

Authors:
A. E. Brester; D. A. Shvetsov; V. I. Zhukov; A. N. Pavlenko

Direct reported results:
- steel mesh 40 is tested in a 6 mm horizontal HFE-7100 layer at different pressures;
- the steel mesh is electrochemically modified using the dynamic matrix method of hydrogen bubbles under three process conditions;
- the optimal modification condition increases heat-transfer coefficient by up to 81% compared with the surface without mesh coating;
- the work is positioned as a heat-transfer enhancement route for dielectric-liquid two-phase cooling.

Boundary:
process/manufacturing bridge only;
full-text process parameters and exact CHF gain were not recovered in the current review;
not phone-scale dryout proof and not evidence of superiority over strong China treated-mesh / UTVC controls.
