## Q1 — Problem and target mapping

The paper studies whether electrochemical modification of a steel mesh can improve boiling heat transfer in a thin horizontal layer of dielectric liquid HFE-7100.

For this project it is an applied surface/process bridge adjacent to vapor-chamber wick engineering. It is not a phone UTVC experiment and not a direct failure-state observer study.

## Q2 — Novelty vs strong baseline

The demonstrated novelty is a hydrogen-bubble dynamic-template electrochemical treatment applied to steel mesh under three process conditions, with an optimum condition improving nucleate-boiling heat transfer.

This is useful Russian process evidence, but it does not survive as a strategic differentiator after comparison with strong China treated-mesh, laser/oxidation/chemical wick modification and dryout-mitigation architecture evidence.

## Q3 — Falsifiable hypothesis

Electrochemical micro/nanostructuring of a metal mesh can increase nucleation activity and liquid-vapor interfacial performance enough to raise boiling HTC in HFE-7100 relative to an untreated reference.

The paper reports results consistent with this hypothesis in the tested thin-layer setup.

## Q4 — Capability lineage / competing route

The paper extends the Pavlenko/Shvetsov/Zhukov experimental line from boiling-crisis mechanism observation toward engineered boiling surfaces.

The strongest competing routes are already broad:
- China nanowire / superhydrophilic copper-mesh boiling enhancement;
- China UTVC laser, oxidation and chemical wick modification;
- China/SJTU microchannel dryout-mitigation architectures.

Therefore this paper strengthens the existence of Russian experimental process competence, but not the retained strategic control point.

## Q6 — Experiment / method design

The paper compares boiling on electrochemically modified mesh surfaces under three process conditions with a reference surface.

The surface modification is produced by the dynamic hydrogen-bubble template method. Boiling experiments are then run in a 6 mm HFE-7100 layer at different pressures.

The full article was not available in the current review path, so detailed electrochemical current density, treatment time, mesh opening, uncertainty budget and complete CHF dataset remain outside the verified deep-read boundary.

## Q7 — Quantitative evidence / reproducibility

The publisher abstract reports that the optimal surface-modification condition increases the heat-transfer coefficient by up to 81% compared with the surface without mesh coating.

The abstract also states that mesh coatings can increase HTC and CHF in HFE-7100 boiling, but the exact CHF improvement for this specific optimized electrochemical condition was not recovered and is therefore not added.

## Q8 — What it proves / does not prove

It supports:

- an active Russian line in engineered mesh surfaces for dielectric-liquid boiling;
- real electrochemical surface-modification capability;
- measurable HTC improvement in the tested HFE-7100 thin-layer setup.

It does not prove:

- phone-scale wick manufacturability;
- sealed copper-water compatibility;
- superior dryout mitigation versus China;
- mechanism-state observability;
- a unique Russian hardware or process advantage.

## Q9 — Decision contribution / control point

The paper does not strengthen the core reason to keep DIR-FAILURE-AWARE-UTVC as a Strategic Candidate.

Instead it clarifies what *not* to use as the collaboration thesis: generic mesh treatment / HTC enhancement is too crowded and too far from the phone target.

The surviving control point remains mechanism-resolved crisis taxonomy and laboratory ground truth that can challenge or label internally owned state models.

## Q10 — Next action / promotion or kill gate

Do not allocate the primary collaboration thesis to reproducing the electrochemical mesh process.

If this process is ever revisited, benchmark it against strong domestic treated-wick controls in a sealed phone-relevant geometry.

For the current Failure-aware direction, keep the paper as supporting evidence of experimental breadth only. Promotion must still come from mechanism-ground-truth value.
