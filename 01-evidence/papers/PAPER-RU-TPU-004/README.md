Title:
Pool boiling on the aluminum alloy, copper and WC-coated copper with micro-finned textures and developed multimodal roughness formed by nanosecond laser radiation

Authors:
E.G. Orlova; D.O. Glushkov; A.O. Pleshko; K.N. Gulkin; A.M. Abdelmagid; P.N. Maximov; M.M. Popov; D.V. Feoktistov

Direct reported results:
- aluminum alloy, copper and WC-coated copper are compared;
- polished and nanosecond-laser-treated surfaces are tested;
- laser treatment forms micro-finned and anisotropic / multimodal hierarchical roughness;
- textured surfaces are subsequently hydrophobized;
- degassed distilled water is used for pool boiling;
- bubble characteristics, heat-transfer coefficient and critical heat flux are measured;
- results are compared with established boiling models;
- evolution of functional properties after prolonged heat-flux exposure is explicitly assessed.

Decision use:
closest current TPU bridge to copper-water heat-transfer processing and thermal-exposure durability.

Boundary:
open pool boiling rather than sealed VC; exact per-texture HTC / CHF values were not recovered in this review; does not prove sub-150 um geometry, vacuum/outgassing compatibility, sealed-fluid behavior or confined spatial-rewetting advantage.
