Title:
Electroosmotic flow in optimally operated micro heat pipes

Direct reported purpose/result:
- analyzes electroosmotic flow as an enhancement mechanism for capillary-driven micro heat pipes used for electronics cooling;
- evaluates a practical range of applied voltage and ion concentration;
- concludes from the model that electroosmosis can enhance micro-heat-pipe thermal performance under appropriate operating conditions.

Decision use:
direct prior-art pressure on the specific idea of electroosmotic assistance inside micro heat-pipe thermal transport, not only on electroosmotic pumps as standalone microfluidic devices.

Boundary:
modeling study rather than a smartphone-scale sealed-device demonstration; it does not invalidate NovSU's current experimental know-how or orientation-tolerance implementation work.
