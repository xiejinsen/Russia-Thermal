Title:
Enhanced capillary-driven thin film boiling on cost-effective gradient wire meshes for high-heat-flux applications

Authors:
Feng Zhou; Jingzhi Zhou; Xunfeng Li; Qihan Chen; Xiulan Huai

Direct reported results:
- deionized-water capillary-driven thin-film boiling;
- four wire-mesh wick samples with different porosities;
- gradient composite wick designed to balance capillary pressure and permeability;
- gradient wick also promotes bubble separation and liquid replenishment;
- reported CHF: 202.8 W/cm2;
- maximum HTC reported in the publisher abstract: 121.9 kW/(m2 K) at 88.5 W/cm2;
- reduced-pressure effects are also investigated.

Boundary:
strong independent China thin-film engineering comparator; not the same gas-shear free-surface platform and not direct phone product validation.
