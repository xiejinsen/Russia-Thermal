Title:
Three-dimensional pore-scale simulations of thin-film evaporation on micro-pillar wicks

Authors:
Junyang Li; Shuai Gong; Chaoyang Zhang; Ping Cheng

Direct reported results:
- 3D pore-scale modeling resolves meniscus recession / thin-film evaporation in micro-pillar wick structures;
- geometry and wettability are linked to wickability and capillary dryout behavior;
- the legacy technical review records analytical and numerical dryout predictions as mutually consistent;
- the work directly treats capillary dryout as a mechanism-level wick-transport boundary rather than only a device-level temperature threshold.

Decision use:
China mechanism-model comparator for Failure-aware UTVC pressure testing.

Boundary:
modeling evidence does not duplicate Kutateladze reversible/irreversible crisis taxonomy or experimental dielectric-fluid dry-spot statistics.
