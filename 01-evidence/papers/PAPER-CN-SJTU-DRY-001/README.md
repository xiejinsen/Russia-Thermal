Title:
Experimental investigation on flow boiling heat transfer characteristics in short flow passage counter-flow microchannels

Authors:
Yun Li; Jie Cao; Xia Hua; Huiying Wu

Affiliation:
School of Mechanical Engineering, Shanghai Jiao Tong University (principal academic affiliation in the paper).

Publication timing:
- received: 2023-09-08;
- published: 2023-11-25;
- online: 2024-01-22.

Direct reported purpose/results:
- short-flow-passage counter-flow microchannels are proposed specifically to address premature downstream dryout;
- working fluid: deionized water;
- mass flux: 118–219 kg/(m2 s);
- inlet subcooling: 50 C;
- versus conventional parallel-flow microchannels, CHF improves by 160.6%–204.4%;
- average HTC improves by 91.2%–115.4%;
- pressure drop is reduced by 76.9%–80.4%;
- pumping power is reduced by 44.9%–48.2%;
- flow-boiling instability is reported as effectively suppressed.

Decision use:
strong SJTU architecture-level comparator showing direct dryout-mitigation and two-phase microchannel engineering capability.

Boundary:
microchannel flow boiling, not sealed phone VC and not the same reversible/irreversible dry-spot diagnostic regime as Kutateladze.
