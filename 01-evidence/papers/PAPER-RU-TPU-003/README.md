Title:
A New Approach to Reducing Slagging Based on Laser Modification of Heating Surfaces: Field Tests and Mathematical Modeling

Authors:
D.V. Feoktistov; D.O. Glushkov; K.K. Paushkina; E.G. Orlova; E.Yu. Laga; A.V. Zhuikov; K.Yu. Vershinina

Direct reported results:
- laser-treated steel surfaces with microchannel / anisotropic textures are tested in a real brown-coal boiler;
- field-test duration: approximately 60 days;
- microscopy, X-ray fluorescence spectral analysis and slag-mass analysis are used;
- the Microchannels texture shows increased resistance to slag adhesion;
- the source reports cleaning intervals can increase by up to 64%;
- absorbed heat per unit area is reported to increase by approximately 1.6–2.2 times in the analyzed application;
- a coupled thermal model tracks changing slag-layer thickness in the flue-gas -> slag -> pipe-wall -> coolant path.

Decision use:
strong evidence of TPU process translation / durability discipline outside the lab.

Boundary:
steel / boiler / slagging system only; does not prove phone-thickness copper processing, sealed-fluid compatibility, wettability-pattern retention or confined rewetting benefit.
