Title:
Mathematical modeling of a microprocessor liquid cooling system

Authors:
A. I. Andreev; A. E. Semenov

Affiliation:
Astrakhan State Technical University

Direct reported results:
- the work studies a microprocessor liquid-cooling system and maintenance of electronic-component temperature;
- the authors report experiments on an existing microprocessor cooling system while controlling coolant and processor operating parameters;
- the work builds a mathematical model for cooling-system behavior and evaluates relationships among coolant flow, temperature and processor operating conditions.

Boundary:
supports continuity of an active microprocessor liquid-cooling research line; it does not establish mobile-terminal packaging, pump-power suitability, reliability or consumer-device integration.
