Title:
Investigation of heat transfer, critical heat flux and dry spots dynamics during boiling of dielectric fluids HFE-7100 and Novec 649

Authors:
Surtaev; Malakhov; Perminov; Polovnikov; Pavlenko

Direct reported results:
- experiments use HFE-7100 and Novec 649;
- high-speed IR and optical/reflection diagnostics are used;
- ML-assisted dry-spot segmentation is reported;
- dry-spot statistics are tracked toward CHF;
- large persistent dry regions precede irreversible crisis in the reported setup.

Boundary:
- not a sealed ultra-thin phone VC experiment;
- not proof of phone-scale product advantage.
