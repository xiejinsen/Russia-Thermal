Title:
An experimental investigation of adiabatic two-phase flow patterns in a slit microchannel with 1:800 aspect ratio

Authors:
Yu.A. Dementyev; E.A. Chinnov; D.Yu. Kochkin; F.V. Ronshin; A.A. Evstrapov; V. Gusev; O.A. Kabov

Direct reported results:
- slit-channel height: 12.5 um;
- width: 10 mm;
- aspect ratio: 1:800;
- five working liquids: HFE-7100, three ethanol-water compositions, and Milli-Q water;
- liquid superficial velocity range: 0.0026–0.266 m/s;
- gas superficial velocity range: 0.266–7.73 m/s;
- five flow regimes identified: Jet-Droplet, Jet, Jet-Churn, Churn, Droplet-Annular;
- for completely wetting liquids, flow-pattern boundaries can be generalized in liquid Capillary number and gas Weber number;
- Saffman-Taylor instability, transverse-pressure-gradient instability and wettability are identified as flow-pattern-determining factors.

Boundary:
extreme-confinement mechanism evidence; adiabatic, not an integrated phone cooler and not a heat-removal / parasitic-power validation.
