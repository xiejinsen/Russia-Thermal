Title:
Capillary characteristics of the wick structure with surface modification for ultra-thin vapor chamber

Authors:
Jiu Yu; Wenqi Fang; Guoliang Hu; Yangyi Guo; Zu'an Chen; Jianbang Zeng; Wencai Zhu; Yong Li

Direct reported results:
- systematically compares thermal oxidation, chemical corrosion and laser etching on UTVC wick surface morphology, wettability, capillary performance and completed-device heat-transfer performance;
- capillary climbing height after modification increases by 11.83%–14.79% for spiral woven mesh and 21.25%–25% for copper mesh versus original wick;
- maximum UTVC heat-transfer power is reported as 8 W for original wick, 10 W for chemical corrosion, 10.5 W for thermal oxidation and 10.5 W for laser etching;
- thermal resistance decreases by 13.89%–24.49% and effective thermal conductivity increases by 16.7%–33.3% relative to the original-wick UTVC;
- thermal oxidation is judged the most efficient overall route from the combined thermal-resistance and effective-conductivity comparison.

Boundary:
direct UTVC comparator for generic surface modification;
does not test TPU's proposed spatial biphilic / routing function and does not establish long-term phone-process durability.
