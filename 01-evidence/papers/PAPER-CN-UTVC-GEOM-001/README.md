Title:
Experimental Investigation on Ultra-Thin Vapor Chamber with Composite Wick for Electronics Thermal Management

Authors:
Shiwei Zhang; Haoyi Huang; Jingjing Bai; Caiman Yan; Huarong Qiu; Yong Tang; Fangqiong Luo

Direct reported results:
- sealed UTVC dimensions: 82 mm × 58 mm × 0.39 mm;
- composite wick uses two copper-mesh layers plus multiple spiral-woven meshes;
- support-column diameter, filling ratio and SWM count are varied experimentally;
- 0.5 mm support-column case reports ETC 3473 W/(m K);
- 30% filling ratio with 3 SWMs reports ETC 3837 W/(m K);
- 3-SWM configuration reports ultimate heat-transfer power of 26 W;
- manufacturing / test details are explicit enough to serve as a phone-relevant geometry and process benchmark.

Decision use:
primary geometry / manufacturability comparator for any Russian wick, surface or structured-device transfer claim.

Boundary:
strong mobile-electronics UTVC baseline, but not proof that every Russian mechanism is non-transferable.
