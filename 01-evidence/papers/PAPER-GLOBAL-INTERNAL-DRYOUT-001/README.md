Title:
Transient dryout and rewetting in a screen-wick heat pipe under pulsed heat loads with internal temperature and pressure measurement

Direct reported results:
- internal distributed vapor temperature and miniature pressure measurements were used during transient dryout/rewetting;
- internal measurements identified vapor superheating, localized dryout behavior and delayed temperature response;
- transient dryout increased thermal resistance and produced persistent post-transient hysteresis;
- rewetting behavior was sensitive to filling ratio and inactive-region design.

Boundary:
high-temperature screen-wick heat pipe, not smartphone UTVC. Its decision value here is diagnostic: internal state measurements expose information beyond external wall temperature alone.
