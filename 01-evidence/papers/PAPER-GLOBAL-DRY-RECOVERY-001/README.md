Title:
Transient recovery from heat pipe dryout by power throttling

Direct reported results:
- transient dryout / recovery characterized experimentally;
- time-to-rewet depends on throttling power;
- post-dryout thermal hysteresis is an observable external thermal signature;
- recovery requires power reduction below a characteristic rewetting level for sufficient duration.

Boundary:
demonstrates generic transient signatures and control relevance; not mechanism-specific irreversible-crisis classification in a phone UTVC.
