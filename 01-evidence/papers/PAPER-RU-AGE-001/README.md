Title:
Long-term operational stability of a hierarchical evaporator surface in a two-phase thermosyphon

Author:
N.S. Ivanov

Direct reported results:
- 42 calendar months of actual periodic operation;
- hierarchical microgroove + Al2O3 nanoparticle evaporator surface;
- R410A thermosyphon;
- integral thermal performance remained comparatively stable;
- post-operation capillary imbibition performance declined;
- post-operation morphology/capillary assessment was performed.

Boundary:
- not a smartphone VC;
- not DI-water/copper proof;
- not sub-mm geometry proof;
- not evidence of broad Russia reliability superiority.
