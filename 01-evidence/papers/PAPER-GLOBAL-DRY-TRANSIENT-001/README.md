Title:
A transient heat pipe model considering wick saturation effects that predicts dynamic evaporator dryout and recovery

Direct reported results:
- transient heat-pipe model includes spatial/temporal wick saturation;
- predicts time-to-dryout, time-to-rewet and dryout-induced thermal hysteresis;
- validated against commercial heat pipes spanning multiple sizes and wick types;
- explicitly motivated by transient electronic workloads.

Boundary:
heat-pipe rather than phone UTVC; model access to internal saturation does not prove an online phone observer from sparse product telemetry.
