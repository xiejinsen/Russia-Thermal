Title:
Experimental studies of the electronic board cooling system

Authors:
Oleg V. Evdulov; A. M. Khaibulaev

Journal:
Herald of Dagestan State Technical University. Technical Sciences, 49(1), 6-13, 2022

Affiliation:
Dagestan State Technical University

Direct reported results:
- the authors built and tested a laboratory thermoelectric system for non-uniform cooling of an electronic-board simulator;
- the setup measured transient temperatures under different thermoelectric-battery supply currents;
- for a reported total board heat generation of 120 W, source temperatures decreased to approximately 345 K and 344 K from approximately 428 K and 396 K;
- reported disagreement between prior calculations and experiment was approximately 8%.

Boundary:
direct electronics-cooling experiment at board scale; not evidence of smartphone-level thickness, parasitic power, battery efficiency or consumer-device packaging.
