Title:
A thin and lightweight miniature loop heat pipe for cooling mobile electronic devices

Authors:
Qingjie Cui; Ziyi You; Xiang Ma; Xiaoping Yang; Yonghai Zhang; Jinjia Wei; Qie Sun; Mingyang Du; Feng Zhang; Fatuan Li

Direct reported results:
- miniature loop heat pipe explicitly targets compact/mobile electronics;
- total thickness: 0.7 mm;
- mass: 3.95 g;
- stepped-serrated nickel-fiber main wick plus braided copper supplementary wick;
- copper-based evaporator with polymer vapor/liquid lines and condenser assembled using solder-free bonding;
- maximum equivalent thermal conductivity: 24,449 W/(m K);
- accelerated aging at 90 C reports stable performance over 30 days;
- supplementary comparison reports maximum heat flux of 7 W/cm2 and total thermal resistance of 1.15 C/W for this work.

Boundary:
strong mobile-geometry accelerated-aging baseline, but not actual multi-year calendar operation;
mLHP architecture is not identical to a smartphone vapor chamber and the 30-day aging result is not a long-life field equivalence.
