Title:
Study of capillary pressure of copper heat-pipe wick structures produced by deformational cutting

Authors:
Nikolay N. Zubkov; Maria F. Grossman

Venue:
MashTech-2025, Vol. 2, pp. 53-62

Affiliation:
Bauman Moscow State Technical University

Direct reported results:
- copper M2 capillary-porous structures were fabricated using deformational cutting;
- capillary pressure was experimentally measured with distilled water and ethanol;
- the authors report capillary-pressure characteristics comparable to or exceeding literature mesh, sintered-powder and fiber wick structures.

Boundary:
process/wick-level evidence only; no sealed sub-mm smartphone vapor chamber or phone-scale permeability/thermal qualification is demonstrated.
