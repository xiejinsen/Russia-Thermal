## Q1 — Problem and target mapping

The paper studies how shrinking gap height changes HFE-7100 boiling, vapor morphology and critical heat flux across different structured surfaces.

This is a direct confinement comparator to Kutateladze's thin dielectric-liquid work.

## Q3 — Falsifiable hypothesis

As confinement tightens, boiling passes from enhancement toward deterioration because vapor congestion and liquid-access constraints increasingly dominate; hierarchical surfaces can retain more performance.
