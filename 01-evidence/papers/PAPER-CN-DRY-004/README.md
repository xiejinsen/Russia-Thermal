Title:
Coupled effects of surface structuring and capillary-length-scale confinement on pool boiling heat transfer and critical heat flux of HFE-7100

Authors:
Shi; Zhong; Li; Peng; Jiang

Direct reported results:
- HFE-7100 pool boiling is compared under unconfined and 5 / 3 / 1 mm gap conditions;
- smooth, microchannel and hierarchical surfaces are compared;
- at 1 mm confinement the legacy technical review records hierarchical-surface peak HTC around 26.62 kW/(m2 K);
- hierarchical-surface CHF retention is reported around 71% of the unconfined value, versus roughly 56–57% for simpler surfaces;
- the study directly examines the transition from enhancement to deterioration as confinement increases.

Decision use:
direct China HFE-confinement comparator against broad Russian confinement / geometry differentiation.

Boundary:
1 mm remains above phone-scale internal VC dimensions and does not reproduce Kutateladze reversible-to-irreversible crisis labeling.
