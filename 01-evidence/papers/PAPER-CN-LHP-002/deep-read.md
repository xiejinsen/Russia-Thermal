## Q1 — Problem and target mapping

The paper studies stable operation and failure of a dual-compensation-chamber multi-evaporator LHP under distributed heat loading.

It is not smartphone scale but directly addresses routing, startup sequencing and capillary-limit failure.

## Q2 — Novelty vs strong baseline

Its project value is explicit failure-boundary treatment rather than nominal LHP performance.

## Q3 — Falsifiable hypothesis

Stable operation is maintained only while available capillary driving force exceeds cumulative hydraulic / two-phase pressure losses across the multi-evaporator system.
