Title:
Experimental Study on a Dual Compensation Chamber Multi-Evaporator Loop Heat Pipe System

Authors:
Deqing Huang; Yuankun Zhang; Hui Li; Chunsheng Guo

Direct reported results:
- multi-evaporator LHP with dual compensation chambers is tested experimentally;
- optimal charge ratio is reported around 75%;
- startup interval around 8–10 min is identified in the tested operating strategy;
- stable total heat load is reported around 270 W;
- per-stage failure threshold around 70 W is reported in the legacy technical review;
- failure is linked to insufficient liquid supply when cumulative pressure drop exceeds available capillary driving capability.
