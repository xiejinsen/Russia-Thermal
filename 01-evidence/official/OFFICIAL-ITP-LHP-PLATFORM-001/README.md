Source:
Institute of Thermal Physics, Ural Branch of RAS — Laboratory of Heat-Transfer Devices official results page.

Direct facts:
- the laboratory describes loop heat pipes as a core institutional development line;
- the page documents miniature loop heat pipes developed for cooling high-speed CPUs in notebook/mobile-computer class systems;
- the official page reports miniature LHP devices with approximately 5 mm evaporator diameter, 2 mm vapor/liquid lines and operation under arbitrary 1-g orientation;
- the page also documents broader electronics, server and space thermal-control applications of the laboratory's LHP technology.

Boundary:
the notebook/electronics devices are historical platform evidence rather than proof of a current sub-mm smartphone implementation;
the page does not establish current smartphone product deployment or a current OEM contract.
