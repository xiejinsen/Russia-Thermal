Source:
Russian Science Foundation project card 23-29-10187, led by Yuri V. Kiliba at Novgorod State University.

Direct facts:
- the project studies electroosmotic heat pipes and electroosmotic pumping to reduce gravity/orientation sensitivity;
- the stated application space includes portable equipment and equipment whose position changes in operation;
- the team measured a maximum electroosmotic pressure head of 2.4 kPa at 100 V using a polypropylene membrane with 5 micrometer pores;
- 2024 publications include "Electroosmotic Pump in Power Electronics Cooling Systems" and "Research of Cooled Element Overheating in Heat Pipes".

Decision use:
current Russian research-program evidence for orientation-tolerant, actively assisted heat-pipe concepts relevant to mobile/portable electronics as a transfer candidate.

Boundary:
the evidence does not establish smartphone-scale thickness, conversion-electronics overhead, parasitic power, EMI, sealing, lifetime, manufacturability or product-level benefit.
