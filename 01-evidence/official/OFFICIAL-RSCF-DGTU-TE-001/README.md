Source:
Russian Science Foundation project 23-29-00130.

Direct facts:
- project title: Development of high-current thermoelectric energy converters and cooling systems based on them for radioelectronics and medical technology;
- principal investigator: Oleg V. Evdulov;
- host organization: Dagestan State Technical University;
- the project explicitly targets cooling systems for radioelectronic equipment and medical technology;
- reported 2024 results include a heat-removal method for intermittently heat-loaded radioelectronic elements using phase-change storage plus high-current thermoelectric batteries;
- reported 2024 results also include non-uniform cooling of electronic boards using phase-change working substances plus high-current thermoelectric batteries;
- the project reports experimental verification of developed thermoelectric systems, with maximum model/experiment discrepancy within approximately 10% over the reported measurement range.

Boundary:
the project demonstrates current institutional continuity and experimental thermoelectric-device capability, but does not establish smartphone-scale power efficiency, packaging thickness, battery impact or product deployment.
