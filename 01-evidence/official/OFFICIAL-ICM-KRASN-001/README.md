Source:
Institute of Computational Modeling SB RAS official 2025 report, structure page and thermal-project pages.

Direct facts:
- 2025 work includes high-thermal-conductivity bases for electronic devices and high-precision thermal-stabilization systems;
- Denis A. Nesterov remains deputy director for science;
- the institute has developed flat-heat-pipe / LTCC electronic-module concepts, two-phase flat-heat-pipe thermal models and thermal-design software for onboard electronics;
- prior validated models explicitly include prediction of wick dryout onset / limiting heat load.

Decision use:
Russian comparator for flat-heat-pipe integration with electronics, thermal modeling and dryout-limit prediction.

Boundary:
most demonstrated integration targets are spacecraft/onboard electronics, not smartphones; geometry, thickness, cost and manufacturing transfer must be re-evaluated.
