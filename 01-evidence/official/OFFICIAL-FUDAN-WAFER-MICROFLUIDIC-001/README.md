Fudan University official 2026 research digest covering an ECTC 2026 paper on wafer-level SiC packaging with embedded microfluidic cooling.

Direct facts:
- Fudan reported a wafer-level integrated 1200 V SiC MOSFET package using room-temperature wafer bonding and embedded microfluidic cooling;
- the design places microchannels near the core heat-generation region and uses coupled thermal-fluid modeling;
- the university reports a 35.14% reduction in total thermal resistance versus the compared conventional SiC module architecture;
- Pan Liu and Guangyin Lei are named as corresponding authors / current faculty anchors.

Decision use:
strong China-side evidence for chip-package co-designed embedded microfluidic cooling with wafer-level integration.

Boundary:
