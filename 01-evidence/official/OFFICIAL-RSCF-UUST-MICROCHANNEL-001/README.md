Source:
Russian Science Foundation project card 24-19-00697, led by Olga A. Solnyshkina at Ufa University of Science and Technology.

Direct facts:
- the 2024-2026 project studies heat/mass transfer in structured microfluidic systems and explicitly motivates integrated liquid microcooling for 3D electronics;
- the target channels have characteristic hydraulic dimensions at or below roughly 100 micrometers;
- 2024 work established numerical and experimental microchannel methods plus silicon plasma-etching process work;
- 2025 work built micro/mini-channel thermal test infrastructure, temperature-field diagnostics and a copper pin-array heat exchanger, while developing silicon/glass microchannel fabrication methods.

Decision use:
current Russian comparator for microchannel geometry, pressure-drop-aware optimization, microfabrication and temperature-field diagnostics for compact electronics cooling.

Boundary:
the public work is a research platform rather than a demonstrated smartphone cooling subsystem; pumping power, packaging, leakage, reliability, thickness and system-level value remain open.
