Title:
Study of capillary pressure of copper heat-pipe wick structures produced by deformational cutting

Authors:
Nikolay N. Zubkov; Maria F. Grossman

Direct reported results:
- copper M2 capillary-porous structures produced by deformational cutting;
- capillary pressure measured with distilled water and ethanol;
- authors report capillary-pressure characteristics comparable to or exceeding mesh, sintered-powder and fiber wick structures in the literature.
