Source:
Lomonosov Moscow State University Institute of Mechanics official project page.

Direct facts:
- current project studies heat and mass transfer in strongly nonequilibrium systems;
- project motivation explicitly states that micro- and nanoelectronics require new heat-exchange devices and identifies evaporation/boiling as efficient heat-removal mechanisms;
- methods include kinetic theory and molecular-dynamics modeling of evaporation/condensation and interfacial transport;
- project lead is Vladimir Yu. Levashov, laboratory head.

Decision use:
Russian foundational comparator for high-intensity phase-change / interfacial transport modeling relevant to electronics cooling limits.

Boundary:
the capability is foundational modeling rather than a compact-electronics cooling-device platform.
