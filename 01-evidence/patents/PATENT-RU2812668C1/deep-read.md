## P1 — target problem
Create a controlled micro/nanostructure on a steel heat-exchange surface using a defined cleaning + nanosecond-laser process.

## P2 — mobile/chip relevance
**PARTIAL.**
Surface-feature scale can enter the phone thickness conversation, but:
- the claim is steel-specific;
- application context is industrial/power heat exchange;
- no sealed VC is claimed.

## P3 — prior-art crowding
Broad laser texturing / heat-transfer-surface roughening is crowded.

Therefore the patent does not rescue a generic "laser surface" strategic thesis.

## P4 — independent claim
The single independent method claim specifies:
- abrasive treatment for 10 min using diamond pastes decreasing from 40 μm to 1 μm;
- ultrasonic clean for 5 min at 35 kHz in 95 wt.% distilled water / 5 wt.% ethanol;
- 24 h normal-condition drying;
- 1064 nm nanosecond fiber-laser processing;
- pulse energy 0.3–1.0 mJ;
- repetition 2–140 kHz;
- pulse duration 1–120 ns;
- scan speed 100–500 mm/s;
- spot 20–100 μm;
- longitudinal/transverse overlap 1–10 times.

## P6 — embodiment / manufacturability

- developed multimodal roughness;
- mean height ~9–18 μm;
