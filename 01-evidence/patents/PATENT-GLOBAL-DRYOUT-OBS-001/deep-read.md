## P1 — Problem
Characterize dryout behavior of a heat pipe and control a heat-generating component when dryout onset / limit is approached.

## P2 — Target relevance
DIRECT mechanism-to-control prior art for generic external dryout observability, though not specific to ultra-thin smartphone VCs.

## P3 — Prior-art / comparator role
This prevents the project from treating power plus external temperature-difference based dryout detection/control as a novel Russian collaboration control point.

## P4 — Claim-reviewed control point
The reviewed claims monitor component power and temperature difference across heat-pipe locations and compare them with criteria associated with dryout.

## P5 — Control action
An operating parameter of a coupled heat-generating component may be reduced when power / temperature-difference criteria indicate dryout onset or limit.

## P6 — Characterization scope
The system can store temperature/time margins around onset and dryout limit for characterization.

## P7 — Lineage
Global prior art; used only as a comparator, not as a partner-capability source.
