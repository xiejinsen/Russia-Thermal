Title:
Apparatus and methods for characterizing a heat pipe and for controlling an operating parameter of at least one heat generating component coupled to the heat pipe

Direct claim-reviewed scope:
- monitors component power and temperature difference across heat-pipe locations;
- threshold can indicate dryout onset / dryout limit;
- operating parameter may be reduced when power and temperature-difference criteria indicate dryout;
- characterization stores temperature/time margins around onset and limit.

Boundary:
strong prior art against generic novelty of external temperature/power dryout observability; not proof of mechanism-specific health-state inference in an ultra-thin smartphone VC.
