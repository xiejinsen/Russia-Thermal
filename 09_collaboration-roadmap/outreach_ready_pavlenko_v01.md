## 2. First-contact objective

Determine whether Lab 1.3 can support a bounded Stage-0 collaboration that transfers its irreversible-dryout / boiling-crisis diagnostic capability to thin copper / fine-mesh coupons relevant to sub-mm mobile two-phase devices.

## 3. Mandatory questions

| ID | Ask | Minimum useful answer | Decision unlocked |
|---|---|---|---|
| P-M1 | What is the minimum repeatable mesh / structured-surface geometry the lab can currently fabricate or test? | wire/feature scale + material + active-area range | whether <=100–150 μm Stage-0 geometry is plausible |

| P-M5 | What current spatial/temporal diagnostic resolution is available on the relevant dielectric/boiling setup? | shareable range | whether irreversible-dryout metrics can be resolved at Stage-0 |
| P-M6 | Are there any shareable background-IP or field restrictions relevant to a new mobile/VC project? | none / yes with high-level boundary / legal review required | whether clean foreground-IP work can start |

## 4. Optional questions

- Any existing copper or <=100 μm mesh attempts, even negative?
- Any water / non-HFE fluid experience on the same surface process?
- Any vacuum/bake/repeated-boiling durability data?
- Typical sample turnaround / batch repeatability?

## 5. Do not ask

- confidential Huawei/customer contract text;
- non-shareable recipes beyond what is required to judge feasibility;
- generic questions already closed publicly such as whether the lab has dry-spot diagnostics.

## 6. Continue / stop rule after first exchange

**Continue to Stage-0 preparation if:**
- <=100–150 μm transfer is plausible;
- lab can test external phone-like coupons or provide comparable coupons;
- process range is shareable enough to define geometry/process risk;
- no obvious field/IP conflict blocks new work.

**Pause / keep mechanism-only if:**
- process fundamentally requires thick source geometry;
- external coupons cannot be accepted and no thin partner coupon can be supplied;
- IP/field restrictions make a phone-specific foreground project impractical.

## 7. Smallest experiment unlocked

Only after mandatory questions pass:
- strong China-style thin reference;
- best Pavlenko thin-transfer arm;
- same DI-water / heater / condenser / geometry envelope;
- measure reversible onset, irreversible transition, dry-spot growth and rewetting.

No sealed Stage-1 VC before coupon success.

## 8. Foreground-IP boundary

Target:
**sub-mm, product-fluid, manufacturing-retained irreversible-dryout / rewetting control**

Avoid:
- generic modified mesh;
- generic hydrophilic coating;
- generic boiling enhancement.
