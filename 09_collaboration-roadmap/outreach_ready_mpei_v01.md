## 6. Continue / stop rule

**Continue if:**
- a real geometry scale-down ladder can be fabricated;
- copper / thin geometry is plausible;
- aging/capillary data are sufficient to define an indicator hypothesis.

**Pause / reliability-only if:**
- hierarchy is fundamentally geometry-bound;
- no thin/copper fabrication route exists;
- capillary aging cannot be linked to any measurable Stage-0 variable.

## 7. Smallest experiment unlocked
