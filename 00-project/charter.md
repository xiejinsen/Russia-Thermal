# Russia-Thermal V2.1 Project Charter

## Decision goal

Identify which Russia-based mobile-thermal capabilities remain differentiated or strategically complementary after strong China/global comparison, who actually owns those capabilities, and what smallest validation would justify collaboration.

## Product scope

Primary:
- smartphones.

Secondary:
- tablets where the mechanism is transferable.

Out of scope as a product target:
- general aerospace, boiler, detector, server or industrial thermal systems unless they provide transferable mechanism / method / reliability evidence.

## Phase-1 research mode

Phase 1 is public-evidence insight work.

It may establish:
- credible scientific/engineering capability;
- narrow differentiation;
- collaboration relevance;
- testable transfer hypotheses.

It must not silently claim:
- phone product validation;
- partner willingness;
- mass-production readiness;
- legal/FTO clearance;
- current confidential project status.

## Decision philosophy

The project prefers:
- narrow evidence-backed residuals;
- explicit China/global comparators;
- falsifiable transfer questions;
- Keep / Narrow / Kill / Watch / Hold decisions.

Broad country-superiority narratives are rejected unless directly supported.
