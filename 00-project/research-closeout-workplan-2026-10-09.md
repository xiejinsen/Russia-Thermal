## Mission and completion rule

Close Q01–Q10 with original-source traceability and distinction among observed fact, analytical inference, handset-transfer hypothesis, and post-authorization unknown. Produce a final leadership-grade decision report and synchronized repository SSOT. Do not extend breadth unless a specific missing source could change P1/P2/HOLD/DO-NOT-INVEST or a decision-critical researcher affiliation.

## Q-specific closeout rule

Q01 bounded institution corpus; Q02 verified lab/person roles or transparent identity exception; Q03 actual experimental/model capabilities and limits; Q04 representative 5–10 works per relevant priority team, avoid claims of exhaustive productivity; Q05 same-problem China and independent global counterexample; Q06 phone geometry, working fluid, mass/noise/power/packaging/reliability transfer limits; Q07 four conditional 2027–29 research questions; Q08 collaborator residual vs internal alternatives and data-rights unknown; Q09 named PI/institution, falsifiable work package, requested deliverables and authorization gates; Q10 keep Phase1 P1 Kutateladze PRIMARY feasibility, P2 MPEI CONDITIONAL RESERVE, TPU HOLD, watch and kill boundaries unless genuinely decision-changing new source.

## Per-round output template

1. North-star and ten-question status delta.
2. Exact examined original objects and relevant source URLs.
3. What changed in canonical evidence and why; what was rejected; confidence and material unknowns.
4. Correct GitHub commit links and affected files.
5. Next highest-value bounded task and acceptance gate.

## Closure / stop criteria

- No remaining public-source material contradiction able to invert the portfolio.
- Every Q has an explicit acceptance status and cited supporting corpus; nonpublic access/experiments are limitations, not research-completion failures.
- Report states no authorized institutional outreach, raw dataset use or verified phone-scale proof.
- Relevant canonical documents and question ledger agree; no prior Phase1 conclusions silently rewritten.
- Web deployment and PPT are deferred presentation tasks and do not block research closeout.
