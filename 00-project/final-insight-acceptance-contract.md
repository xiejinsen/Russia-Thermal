## North-star outcome

Answer: **Which Russia-based academic teams possess demonstrable, smartphone-thermal-relevant knowledge or capabilities that add a *specific and defensible increment* over the strongest available Chinese/domestic and global alternatives; what exact joint research should our team pursue, preserve as a reserve or reject, and what must remain internally owned?**

The output is a **decision-grade insight and partner portfolio**, not a general Russia bibliography, university ranking or promise of product performance.

## Ten mandatory final questions and required evidence gates

| ID | Final question leadership must be able to answer | Minimum acceptable answer/evidence | Refuse to claim / failure condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q01 | **Which relevant Russian academic institutions exist?** | Institution landscape and scope, canonical institution identifiers, university/RAS/facility coverage, evidence of important omission checks, transparent discovered-corpus coverage and unmatched gaps | A 23/28 current-corpus profile fraction is **not** percentage coverage of all Russian institutes; aliases are not distinct institutions |
| Q02 | **Who are the actual laboratories and key researchers?** | Institution → current lab/group → scientific lead + pivotal authors → exact competence; primary affiliation/appointment sources and original linked work, uncertainty for unpublished roles | Coauthor ≠ current lab member; institute homepage ≠ group owner; historical patent author ≠ present team |
| Q03 | **What can Russia really do technically?** | Capability/mechanism description with original evidence, method, apparatus/model, demonstrated boundary, confidence and transfer-relevant results; software included only when high-signal | A paper title, institute prestige, grant plan or patent claims alone prove a working phone technology |
| Q04 | **How strong and active are these teams across 2021–2025?** | Representative influential and technically relevant publications (typically 5–10 per key team; P1 may need 8–12), 2–4 decision-critical deep reads, venue/quality and current activity evidence, selected relevant patents/grants and academic coauthors; topic, original title links, DOI/version/year, affiliation at publication, why selected, important missing/contrary work check, China/global comparison; quantitative annual totals only if a numerical productivity claim is essential and independently reconciled | Do not invent institution yearly totals, rank partial collections, double count RU/EN journals/preprints/corrigenda or call software registrations patents |
| Q05 | **Where is there genuine Russia-versus-China differentiation?** | Same concrete problem and conditions, **strongest China and independent global** original comparators, what is equal/stronger/weaker/complementary, possible Russia residual, confidence, contrary evidence | Russia-unique or Russia-leading labels from high paper count, nonmatched power/heat flux or generic physics |
| Q06 | **Does it transfer to smartphones under real constraints?** | Specific chip/VC/LHP/active/passive/system use case, relevant thickness/weight/volume, heat flux, pump/TEC/noise/power, sensor/packaging, reliability and observable-state limits; theoretical vs measured vs engineering-gated marked separately | Transfer from space, power, cryogenics or lab rig accepted as actual phone-device qualification without conditions |
| Q07 | **Which future thermal research directions warrant early positioning?** | Bounded technical trend or unsolved mechanism, evidence from recent authoritative academic work and China/global developments, realistic 2027+ applicability and counterfactual 'what if not pursued' | Speculative fashions or extrapolations treated as established future products |
| Q08 | **Why collaborate with Russia instead of doing it in China or internally?** | Incremental control point: distinct mechanism labels, inaccessible validated data, model boundary, apparatus, skill acceleration or research time; domestic alternative and build-vs-partner counterfactual; what the internal team must own | "Famous professor" or "Russian breakthrough" sufficient justification; implied data/IP access without evidence |
| Q09 | **With whom and on precisely which research task?** | Named academic organization/lab/PI, narrow falsifiable work package, expected deliverables, input data and validity conditions, risks, data/IP and collaboration feasibility/permission gates; use decision card | An unspecific "joint research on cooling"; current willingness, contract, hardware tests or legal clearance asserted without authorization |
| Q10 | **What should leadership prioritize, reserve, hold or kill?** | Ranked primary/conditional reserve/watch/hold/do-not-invest portfolio; grounded tradeoffs, explicit triggers, stopping criteria, requested next decision and references to canonical Direction/Decision Events | A generic list of equal-value technologies or reopening frozen Phase-1 conclusions without new decisive evidence |

## Final completion policy — avoid false closure

Allowed question states in ledger:

- `NOT_STARTED` — no answer or source chain.
- `IN_PROGRESS` — partially evidenced, material gaps remain.
- `SUBSTANTIALLY_ANSWERED` — bounded, decision-relevant answer exists with accessible original proof, but completeness/independent signoff gates remain.
- `CLOSED` — specified acceptance evidence is linked, material contrary evidence and known gaps are disclosed, leadership answer is actionable within *public-evidence* limitations.

In the ledger, each question has one row: question ID, owner workstream, state, current grounded answer, authoritative evidence pointer(s), material remaining gate and next bounded action. Update rows as research progresses; never auto-promote from count increases. Any `CLOSED` claim requires source/rationale traceability, an audit of contradictory evidence and explicit scope limitation.

**Phase separation:** `PHASE1_FROZEN` in the canonical portfolio means the **existing technology-selection decision** is closed. It does NOT assert all ten **final integrated Russia academic insight questions** are `CLOSED`. New institution research enriches coverage without retroactively reopening P1/P2/P3. Only new decision-grade evidence + a canonical Decision Event can change portfolio status.

**Research-vs-action boundary:** Insight may deliver a conditional partner recommendation using public evidence. It must never claim phone-device validation, a contacted/willing professor, licensing/data rights, export/legal FTO, factory maturity or an executed contract. Current user has not authorized outreach or tests. "Not independently verified public" must not be written as "does not exist".

## Decision-grade evidence contract

Per *recommended* collaboration opportunity:
1. primary institutional identity + named academic researcher and 2021–25/current activity anchor;
2. mechanism/method and original paper/patent/project evidence as title hyperlinks, authors, venue, measured vs modeled facts;
3. strongest China/global and internal-build alternatives with matched/unmatched-condition notes;
4. phone product relevance and explicit full-system overhead;
5. the narrowly incremental external control point *and what our team retains*;
6. expected research artifact, measurable falsification/reopen/stop criteria and public-evidence feasibility;
7. constraints: missing data, repeats, calibration, IP, partner willingness, no external contact.

For *WATCH/HOLD/KILL* lanes, write one concise counterexample-based reason and exact new-evidence reopen trigger; no need for a full academic feasibility package for every weak lead.

## Acceptance checklist

Final integrated insight **ready for leadership review** only if:
- all ten questions have explicit, auditable answers or explicit "unknown / condition-gated" statements with a justified *stop* boundary; do not manufacture precision for Q01/Q04/Q08;
- major academic institution/person omissions and same-institute aliases have been audited; gaps transparent;
- chosen collaboration options have original research and China/global pressure tests;
- a direct phone-transfer claim is distinguishable from a hypothesis, especially when no device tests exist;
- primary/reserve/watch/hold/kill recommendations and required decisions are crisp;
- report/website derives from source-of-truth graph, latest GitHub health and evidence audit have been checked;
- any insight-phase remaining uncertainty is converted to a **future authorization/feasibility gate**, not silently marked proven.

A quantified whole-project "percent complete" is prohibited without defensible denominators by question.
