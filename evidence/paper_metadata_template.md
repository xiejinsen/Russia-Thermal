## Rule

Bibliometric prestige supplements, but never replaces:
- direct technical relevance;
- comparable test conditions;
- recent activity;
- reproducibility;
- mobile-transfer feasibility.


## Display rule

When this paper is cited in a human-facing research file, do not display only the DOI.

Use:

**[Paper title](DOI/publisher URL)** — Authors — *Journal / Conference*, Year.

For >4 authors, use first 3 + *et al.* unless a later author is directly relevant to the partner analysis.
