**Review status:** DIRECT PATENT / INDEPENDENT-CLAIM REVIEWED.

### P1 — target problem
Create a controlled micro/nanostructure on a steel heat-exchange surface using a defined cleaning + nanosecond-laser process.

### P2 — mobile/chip relevance
**PARTIAL.**
Surface-feature scale can enter the phone thickness conversation, but:
- the claim is steel-specific;
- application context is industrial/power heat exchange;
- no sealed VC is claimed.

### P3 — prior-art crowding
Broad laser texturing / heat-transfer-surface roughening is crowded.

Therefore the patent does not rescue a generic "laser surface" strategic thesis.

### P4 — independent claim
The single independent method claim specifies:
- abrasive treatment for 10 min using diamond pastes decreasing from 40 μm to 1 μm;
- ultrasonic clean for 5 min at 35 kHz in 95 wt.% distilled water / 5 wt.% ethanol;
- 24 h normal-condition drying;
- 1064 nm nanosecond fiber-laser processing;
- pulse energy 0.3–1.0 mJ;
- repetition 2–140 kHz;
- pulse duration 1–120 ns;
- scan speed 100–500 mm/s;
- spot 20–100 μm;
- longitudinal/transverse overlap 1–10 times.

### P5 — dependent-claim bounds
The patent record identifies **one claim**; no separate dependent-claim ladder is available to create narrower fallback bounds.

Embodiment data, not dependent claims, provide further process/roughness detail.

### P6 — embodiment / manufacturability
Disclosed steel embodiment:
- developed multimodal roughness;
- mean height ~9–18 μm;
- maximum height ~17.5–120 μm;
- laser melting/ablation products ~15–100 μm.

Phone interpretation:
the lower part of this range fits the Stage-0 functional-layer budget; the upper feature height can consume essentially the whole preferred <=120 μm surface/wick budget.

### P8 — overlap / design-around
Generic laser texturing is not the foreground opportunity.

A likely design-around / joint-foreground direction is:
- copper instead of claimed steel;
- phone-specific sub-mm confinement;
- spatial wetting function rather than industrial anti-deposit roughness;
- low-outgassing sealed process;
- rewetting/liquid-routing performance metric.

This is technical prior-art analysis, not legal FTO.
