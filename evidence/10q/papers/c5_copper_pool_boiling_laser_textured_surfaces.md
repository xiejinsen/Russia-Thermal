# C5 — Copper pool boiling on TPU laser-textured surfaces

> **10Q card:** C5 · **Evidence class:** Paper · **Track:** C. TPU / Feoktistov
>
> Navigation: [Paper 10Q Index](README.md) · [10Q Method](../../mobile_thermal_insight_10q_method.md)

**[Pool boiling on the aluminum alloy, copper and WC-coated copper with micro-finned textures and developed multimodal roughness formed by nanosecond laser radiation](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.expthermflusci.2024.111366)** — E.G. Orlova, D.O. Glushkov, A.O. Pleshko *et al.*, D.V. Feoktistov — *Experimental Thermal and Fluid Science*, 2025.

### Q1 — problem + target mapping
Can the TPU laser-surface family be demonstrated on copper under actual boiling in water?
Target mapping: directly relevant to the copper-material prerequisite for a two-phase device, but not yet to ultra-thin sealed VC manufacturing.

### Q2 — novelty / relevance
For this project the key point is not a globally novel laser-boiling concept. The decision value is that Feoktistov/Orlova already have a public copper + nanosecond-laser + degassed-water boiling bridge.

### Q3 — falsifiable hypothesis
Laser-generated micro-finned / hierarchical copper surface states change pool-boiling HTC / CHF relative to conventionally prepared copper. The paper reports that they do.

### Q4 — lineage
Same TPU line as laser surface engineering, wettability tuning, Feoktistov/Orlova process/IP work, and later field-validation / hydrophobization studies.

### Q5 — mechanism / control point
Controls: copper substrate, nanosecond-laser micro-finned / anisotropic texture, wettability state, degassed distilled water.
Mechanisms: nucleation-site / roughness / wettability effects on bubble dynamics and boiling heat transfer.

### Q6 — experiment
Publicly supported: copper samples, nanosecond laser texture, distilled degassed water, pool boiling, HTC/CHF measurement, and bubble observation.
Exact phone-thickness sample geometry is not established by the currently recovered public record.

### Q7 — data / artifact / reproducibility
TPU's official public summary reports more than about 2.5× HTC increase on copper for the laser-treated surface in the tested setup.
Use this as a source fact about the reported experiment, not as a phone-VC performance claim.

### Q8 — evidence vs hypothesis
**PUBLIC:** laser processing on copper; water boiling on copper; functional boiling improvement in the reported pool setup.
**NOT CLOSED:** thin copper; sealed vacuum manufacturing; outgassing; post-process wetting retention; confined rewetting/dryout.

### Q9 — contribution to project decision
Old state: copper-substrate transfer not publicly evidenced.
New state: copper material applicability is public; thin-phone geometry and sealed-process transfer remain unresolved.
Stage-0 rank unchanged.

### Q10 — next action
Change the partner question from 'Can you process copper?' to:
> Can the demonstrated copper route be fabricated on phone-thickness copper within the <=150 μm functional-height budget, with repeatable throughput and survival through VC processing?

**Decision:** KEEP / PUBLIC COPPER BRIDGE CLOSED / THIN-SEALED TRANSFER STILL PARTNER+EXPERIMENT.
