# Evidence Standard

## Claim classes

1. **Source fact**  
   Directly stated in a primary paper, official institution page, patent, standard, product documentation or measured teardown.

2. **Author/company claim**  
   Interpretation, benefit or performance claim made by an author/vendor; not independently verified.

3. **Cross-source observation**  
   Pattern visible only after comparing multiple sources.

4. **Analyst inference**  
   Our interpretation of what the evidence implies.

5. **Hypothesis**  
   A falsifiable proposition to test through research or PoC.

## Source preference

Priority order:
1. Primary peer-reviewed paper / DOI record
2. Official lab or institution page
3. Patent record
4. OEM / supplier technical documentation
5. Independent teardown / measurement
6. Secondary reporting
7. Aggregator / profile page only when primary evidence is unavailable

## Required metadata for high-value evidence

- claim
- source title
- URL / DOI / patent number
- publication or update date
- authors
- affiliation
- evidence type
- target technical track
- mobile relevance
- directness
- freshness
- verification status
- notes / caveats

## Comparison discipline

For technology comparisons normalize, where possible:
- heat load and heat flux;
- area / thickness / volume;
- thermal resistance or temperature reduction;
- ambient and boundary conditions;
- orientation;
- working fluid;
- cooling power;
- airflow and static pressure;
- noise spectrum / SPL;
- vibration;
- reliability;
- manufacturing process;
- test duration;
- system-level rather than component-only benefit.

## Rule

**Do not turn “interesting physics” into “mobile opportunity” until a mobile-transfer constraint check has been passed.**
