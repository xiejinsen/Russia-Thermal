### Interpretation boundary

These sources are **preliminary evidence of a relevant foundational capability**, not proof that Russia has a general national mathematics advantage over China.

What they do show:
- current Russian groups still connect applied mathematics / mathematical physics directly to thermal-fluid mechanisms;
- exact solutions, stability theory and coupled-interface modeling are being used on evaporation, thin films and minichannels relevant to the current Russia thermal candidates.

Potential strategic use:
- expose failure thresholds / bifurcations before expensive device fabrication;
- construct reduced-order models;
- identify dominant instability mechanisms;
- design discriminating experiments and control strategies.

This foundational layer must be compared with strong China/global applied-math capability before it is promoted to a final Russia advantage.
