### Decision notes

RU-MPEI-009 closes part of the MPEI reliability gap:
- alternate refrigerant (R410A);
- 42-month two-phase periodic operation;
- post-operation morphology evidence.

It does **not** establish smartphone transfer because heat flux and geometry are non-comparable.

RU-TPU-007 strengthens surface-process durability but does **not** establish sealed-VC vacuum/outgassing/two-phase durability.
