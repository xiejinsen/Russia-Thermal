### Decision notes

**Kabov / Chinnov**
- China is already very strong in thin-film boiling, wick design and extreme headline heat flux.
- The broad thesis "Russia is differentiated in thin-film cooling" is **killed**.
- Residual Russian signal is narrower:
  **shear-driven free-surface microfilm + wave/dry-spot/rupture/interfacial-instability physics under extreme confinement**, including a 12.5 μm slit-flow experiment.
- RU-FILM-001 includes Beijing University of Technology coauthors; therefore the mechanism should not be presented as Russia-exclusive knowledge.

**Maydanik / ITP UB RAS**
- China current evidence covers multi-source/dual-evaporator LHP, startup, variable load, NCG/elevation, compensation-chamber behavior, and explicit capillary/pressure-drop failure.
- China also has thinner direct electronics implementations (1 mm dual-evaporator laptop; 0.7 mm mobile LHP in existing register).
- Therefore "LHP routing / operating-limit / failure physics" is **no longer retained as a country-level Russia differentiation candidate**.
- Maydanik remains **WATCH / KNOWLEDGE RESERVE** for foundational LHP design/failure analysis.
