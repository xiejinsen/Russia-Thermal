### Decision notes

- China has strong independent capability in nonlinear/long-wave stability, Marangoni modeling, phase-change numerics and inverse thermal diagnostics.
- Therefore the broad thesis **"Russia has superior mathematical physics for thermal management" is not supported**.
- The residual Russian foundational candidate is narrower:
  **continuous exact/group-invariant analytical modeling of coupled evaporative thermocapillary systems, used for interpretable stability/mechanism analysis and experiment-informed closure**.
- No equally continuous independent recent China line in that exact problem class was recovered in this pass. This is an evidence gap, not proof of absence.
- Inverse thermal diagnostics currently show a stronger direct electronics signal in China.
