### Decision notes

**RU-MPEI-011 / RU-MPEI-012**
close part of the previous MPEI high-flux/thin-channel uncertainty at **capability-lineage level**:
- water;
- ~0.2 mm microchannel;
- Al2O3 nanoparticle coating;
- CHF / heat-transfer-crisis focus;
- RU-MPEI-012 directly includes N.S. Ivanov.

They do **not** establish that the 2026 hierarchical microgroove + Al2O3 / 42-month R410A surface works at phone hotspot conditions in a sealed UTVC.

**RU-TPU-PAT-001-CLOSE**
supersedes the earlier claim-pending state:
- Feoktistov/Orlova inventor linkage is now public;
- the claim is a steel preparation + nanosecond-laser process;
- mobile/copper/vacuum applicability remains unresolved.
