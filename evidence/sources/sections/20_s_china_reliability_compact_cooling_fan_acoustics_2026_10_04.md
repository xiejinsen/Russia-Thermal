### Decision notes

**MPEI reliability comparison**
- China has a coherent copper-water VC reliability/failure/lifetime line, not merely short thermal cycling.
- CN-REL-001 identifies oxygen-driven wick oxidation, hydrophilic-to-hydrophobic transition and capillary-pressure collapse as a VC failure mechanism.
- CN-REL-002 uses 150–200°C accelerated aging plus XPS/EDS and reports an oxygen-footprint lifetime model; ~1% wick-surface oxygen corresponds to a predicted >=13-year operating life at 80°C, with reported ~8% prediction error.
- Therefore MPEI must not be described as uniquely owning "two-phase reliability" in general.
- MPEI's remaining unusual signal is narrower: **actual 42-month periodic operation of one hierarchical evaporator surface with post-operation morphology + capillary-aging evidence**.

**Aeroacoustics comparison**
- China academic work already includes electronic-device cooling-fan tonal noise, inlet non-uniformity, short-duct mode control, narrow-space installed conditions and acoustic source imaging.
- Direct strong academic evidence for **smartphone-scale ~18–25 mm / ~20k rpm internal centrifugal fans** was not recovered in this pass.
- Russia also lacks direct public phone-microfan proof.
- Therefore TsAGI/PNRPU/CIAM should be treated as a **method reserve / joint-test opportunity**, not a proven Russia country advantage.
