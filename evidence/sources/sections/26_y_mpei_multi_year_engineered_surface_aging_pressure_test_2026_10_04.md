### Decision notes

China is already stronger on:
- product-path copper-water VC failure physics;
- vacuum/oxygen reliability linkage;
- accelerated lifetime prediction;
- oxidation QA;
- direct mobile-scale two-phase hardware.

No matched public China analogue was recovered for:
- the **same engineered evaporator surface**;
- **actual multi-year calendar-time two-phase operation**;
- simultaneous thermal-performance + morphology + capillary-aging tracking.

Residual MPEI distinction:
**actual multi-year engineered-surface aging evidence**, not generic reliability leadership.

Absence rule:
"no matched public analogue recovered" is an evidence gap, not proof that China lacks internal data.
