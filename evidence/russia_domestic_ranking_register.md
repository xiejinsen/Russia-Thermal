# Russia Domestic University Ranking Register v0.1

Last updated: 2026-10-03

## Purpose

Provide a Russian domestic-ranking context alongside global university rankings.

Domestic ranking is **context metadata**, not evidence of smartphone thermal capability.

## Primary domestic baseline

### RAEX-100 — 2026

Main ranking:
https://raex-rr.com/education/russian_universities/top-100_universities/2026/

Methodology:
https://raex-rr.com/education/russian_universities/top-100_universities/2026/methods/

RAEX-100 2026 combines:
- educational conditions;
- employer demand for graduates;
- scientific/research activity;
- statistical and reputation/input data.

RAEX reports that its university-ranking methodology is IREG Approved.

## Interpretation rule

For this project:

**technical evidence > relevant subject ranking > overall domestic ranking > global ranking**

Example:
- MPEI is RAEX overall #24 but Energy #3 and has current ordered-porous heat-pipe work.
- MSU is overall #1 but current mobile-thermal-specific public evidence is less direct.
- PNRPU is overall #67 but owns a uniquely relevant aeroacoustic facility.

Therefore no partner/Tier decision should be made from overall university rank alone.
