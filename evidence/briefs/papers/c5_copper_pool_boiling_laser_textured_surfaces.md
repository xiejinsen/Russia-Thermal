# C5. Copper pool boiling on TPU laser-textured surfaces

> **Brief ID:** C5 · **Track:** C. TPU — laser / wettability patterning
>
> Navigation: [Paper Brief Index](README.md) · [Paper 10Q Index](../../10q/papers/README.md)

**[Pool boiling on the aluminum alloy, copper and WC-coated copper with micro-finned textures and developed multimodal roughness formed by nanosecond laser radiation](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.expthermflusci.2024.111366)** — E.G. Orlova, D.O. Glushkov, A.O. Pleshko *et al.*, D.V. Feoktistov — *Experimental Thermal and Fluid Science*, 2025.

**Review status:** PUBLISHER ABSTRACT + TPU OFFICIAL PUBLIC SUMMARY / DECISION-RELEVANT REVIEW.

**Background / problem**
A key TPU transfer blocker was whether the laser-surface capability had actually been demonstrated on copper, rather than only steel / AlMg3 / composite substrates.

**Technical method**
The paper compares distilled degassed-water pool boiling on aluminum alloy, copper, and WC-coated copper. Surface preparation includes abrasive polishing, nanosecond-laser micro-finned / anisotropic hierarchical textures, and hydrophobization for textured samples.

**Main conclusion**
Direct copper laser-texture + water-boiling capability is publicly demonstrated in the Feoktistov/Orlova line.

TPU's official summary reports that nanosecond laser treatment increased the heat-transfer coefficient on copper surfaces by more than about 2.5× relative to the compared baseline in the reported pool-boiling setup.

**What this changes**
Killed as an open PUBLIC question:
> Has the team ever demonstrated laser-textured copper in water boiling?

Answer: **YES.**

Still open:
- thin phone-grade copper thickness;
- <=150 μm total functional-height constraint;
- exact throughput/repeatability at a ~10×10 mm phone-like zone;
- vacuum/outgassing;
- sealed-VC process retention;
- whether the later hydrocarbon-derived wettability-contrast branch maps cleanly onto the copper route.

**Decision impact:** TPU remains Stage-0 #3, but copper-material transfer risk narrows from material applicability unknown to thin-geometry / sealed-process compatibility unknown.

**Mobile relevance:** MEDIUM process-transfer evidence / LOW direct device evidence.
