# C4. Laser-modified heat-transfer surface — 60-day field validation

> **Brief ID:** C4 · **Track:** C. TPU — laser / wettability patterning
>
> Navigation: [Paper Brief Index](README.md) · [Paper 10Q Index](../../10q/papers/README.md)

**[A New Approach to Reducing Slagging Based on Laser Modification of Heating Surfaces: Field Tests and Mathematical Modeling](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.fuel.2024.133778)** — D.V. Feoktistov, D.O. Glushkov, K.K. Paushkina, E.G. Orlova *et al.* — *Fuel*, 2025.

**Review status:** PUBLISHER FULL-RECORD / DECISION-RELEVANT REVIEW.

**Background / problem**  
Most TPU evidence used in this project is laboratory surface physics. This paper asks a different question: can a laser-modified functional heat-transfer surface survive and retain useful behavior in a real operating thermal system for a prolonged period?

**Technical method**  
AISI 310S steel coupons (~25 × 25 × 2 mm) were laser textured into microchannel / anisotropic surface states and placed in an operating ~400 kW brown-coal boiler.

Field exposure:
- ~60 days;
- real ash/slag environment;
- microscopy / XRF / mass analysis;
- heat-transfer modeling tied to measured deposit behavior.

**Main conclusion**  
Laser-textured microchannel surfaces showed substantially lower slag deposition than conventional ground surfaces under the field test.

The source reports:
- up to ~60% reduction in slagging intensity for the microchannel texture;
- predicted/derived heat-transfer-equipment efficiency improvement in the tested boiler context;
- real field survival over a multi-week industrial thermal environment.

**What we learn for our insight**  
This is **not smartphone cooling evidence** and should not be used to transfer the reported thermal improvement to electronics.

What it does change:
- TPU laser-surface capability is not purely a small laboratory coupon capability;
- the team has evidence of manufacturing a surface state that survived prolonged real-equipment exposure;
- process robustness / scale-up confidence is better than our earlier partner model implied.

What it does **not** close:
- copper process;
- sub-150 μm phone geometry;
- vacuum/outgassing;
- sealed DI-water compatibility;
- hydrocarbon-derived hydrophobic chemistry stability;
- two-phase confined rewetting advantage.

**Decision impact:**  
TPU remains **Stage-0 #3 / GO WITH PREREQUISITE**, but its **process-translation confidence upgrades from laboratory-only to field-validated on a non-mobile surface application**.

**Mobile relevance:** LOW direct / MEDIUM process-translation support.
