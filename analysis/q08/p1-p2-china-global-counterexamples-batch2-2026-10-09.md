## Decision implications

P1 is now pressured simultaneously at THREE nonidentical levels:
1. Global high-speed IR + ML dry-spot imaging already existed (batch 1, 2019/2023).
2. Capillary-fed rewetting and multiple dryout mechanisms already appear in original 2024/2026 outside-Russia work (the 2026 multiscale hybrid wick paper is South Korean, NOT Chinese) (this batch).
3. China has assembled ultra-thin VC wick and thermal-design experiments (2025/2026), not just unrelated general boiling studies.

**Residual P1 cannot be generic optical ML, dryout classification, or wick-geometry improvement.** It must be reproducible Russian *specific* mechanism labels with meaningful same-data gain over strong dryout mode classifiers / existing engineering models, and plausible offline phone-transfer use.

P2: China offers matched copper-water VC oxidation failure and accelerated aging (2025/2026). A 2024 five-cycle hydrophilicity-loss experiment is a useful additional independent aging-like mechanism, but FIVE cycles are **not** 42 calendar months. These operate on different degradation clocks and rigs. No second verified 42-month matched VC trajectory was found in this bounded scan; absence in this scan does not mean nonexistent globally. Russia may still contribute long-exposure pathology, but no dynamic precursor or reusable data are proven.

## Head-to-head comparator rules

- Wicks under open capillary-fed boiling != sealed VC product.
- Full high-power VC >600 W/cm² != 0.x-mm phone UTVC; thickness/working conditions must be matched.
- Russia dry spot morphology at HFE/Novec pool boiling != China grooved-water rewetting or multi-scale wick; compare *information value* not cross-rig CHF ratios.
- 42 calendar months with periodic operation != accelerated oxidation lifetime and != five boiling cycles; none measures the same thing without validated mapping.
- Domestic paper affiliation is not equal to accessible partner data; neither does Russia publication imply data/rights access.
- Internal build costs, personnel, test fixtures, models and ownership remain UNKNOWN. Do not invent internal comparative advantages.
