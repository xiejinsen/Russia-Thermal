## Immediate research implication

The existing graph is already deep in:
- passive two-phase thermal transport;
- mechanism / diagnostics;
- modeling;
- reliability / process support.

The largest structural gap for the broadened Atlas goal is **ACTIVE_HARDWARE**.

Priority discovery themes for the next round:
1. microfan / MEMS fan / compact air mover;
2. fan / duct / airflow / aeroacoustic integration for compact electronics;
3. pump-driven microfluidic / liquid cooling with compact-electronics relevance;
4. active solid-state cooling only where a credible device/integration team exists;
5. wearable / tablet / AR-VR / laptop transfer evidence attached to hardware capabilities.

Software/system research should remain bounded to representative important nodes.

## Boundary

Counts above describe the current canonical graph.

They do not establish:
- national publication volume;
- national capability share;
- Russia-vs-China superiority;
- absence of unresearched active-cooling teams.
