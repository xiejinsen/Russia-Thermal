## Why the horizon changes the research emphasis

This report is **not** evidence that all phone workloads will have a specific new thermal profile by 2027–29. It states conditional planning problems. As compute bursts or thermal cycling matter, system-relevant metrics should include:
- power/temperature history and recovery/hysteresis, not one steady-state heat flux;
- safety skin temperature and user touch requirements, even if die hot spot is lower;
- repeated environmental and orientation exposure;
- manufacturing and long-term internal-gas/oxidation consistency;
- valid measured/observed variables available to a real handset controller, rather than opaque laboratory camera channels.

The literature [2025 electronic PCM cyclic load research](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enconman.2025.120107) specifically models SiC MOSFET with 150 C-class conditions; its claimed temperature improvements are **not** phone-scale outcomes. [2025 die-level PCM](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.applthermaleng.2024.125091) reports cycling performance but is **not** Russia/China proof. [2016 surrogate mobile PCM experiment](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1359431115014374) establishes that PCM phone-simulator concept is not novel.

## China / internal-first comparative framing

1. China product hardware and process are baseline for actual handset UTVC, wick integration, corrosion/oxidation QA, and thin-module engineering; do not claim Russia country superiority.
2. Independent global academic baseline is used to falsify nonunique Russian methods (KAIST **South Korea**, Purdue/US PCM), not mislabelled China.
3. Internal architecture, reliability model, controller, phone telemetry and product validation remain domestic/internal.
4. No partner rights, public aligned data, test feasibility, licensing, costs, or actual phone measurements assumed.
