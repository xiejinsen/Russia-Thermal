# Russia Thermal Collaboration Readiness & Industrial Translation Review

Date: 2026-10-06
Research mode: Phase-1 public evidence
Decision goal: identify which Russian institutions / key researchers are most credible for future collaboration, what role each is best suited for, and what public evidence supports execution readiness beyond academic novelty.

## Executive conclusion

The strongest scientific mechanism partner and the strongest collaboration-ready partner are not automatically the same thing.

Current public-evidence convergence:

### Tier A — strongest collaboration packages

1. **Kutateladze Institute — Lab 1.3 / Alexander N. Pavlenko**
   - Best public evidence of sustained industrial + international collaboration.

4. **Kutateladze Institute — Lab 6.6 / Kabov / Chinnov / Kochkin**

5. **ICM / Lavrentyev / NSU modular modeling network**
   - Useful as a supporting analytical / experiment-design network.
   - Strongest role: exact/stability analysis, fluid modeling, model-experiment closure.
   - Main caveat: not one formal consortium; current operational collaboration boundaries are partially confirmation-gated.

## Readiness dimensions

The review uses separate dimensions rather than one prestige score:

- **Mechanism relevance** — direct relevance to surviving mobile-thermal control point.
- **Industrial collaboration evidence** — public evidence of company contracts, consulting, implementation, manufacturing or technology transfer.
- **Engineering translation** — evidence beyond papers: prototype, field use, manufactured hardware, implementation act or applied patent.
- **Team continuity** — multiple current people / students / projects sustaining the line.
- **International collaboration** — evidence of cross-border technical work.
- **Mobile directness** — how close the demonstrated system is to smartphone constraints.
- **Contact readiness** — whether named senior/key people and public institutional pathways are visible.
- **Execution uncertainty** — what still requires direct confirmation.

## Why Pavlenko / Lab 1.3 ranks first for outreach readiness

Public evidence is unusually complete for a research partner:
- long record of government + industry funded work;
- repeated industrial cooperation;
- direct Huawei scientific/technical agreement;
- subsequent Bel Huawei consulting agreement;
- current Chinese university collaboration;
- joint RSF/NSFC project;
- current multi-year multiphase and microelectronics-relevant programs.

This does **not** make Lab 1.3 automatically the best technology answer.
It makes it the strongest currently evidenced **partner package**: mechanism relevance + named technical leader + repeated industrial/international execution.

## Why MPEI remains co-primary rather than secondary

MPEI has less public evidence of direct smartphone/OEM engagement, but its execution chain is unusually useful:
- university research line;
- senior technical lineage under Kuzma-Kichta;
- Ivanov long-duration experimental record;
- Alyautdinova continuing controlled-wettability work;
- Newfrost commercial-contract / implementation evidence;
- externally manufactured thermosyphon model;
- separate current microchannel team.

This is a good pattern for work that needs:
**research -> coupon/device -> manufacturing partner -> repeat experiment**.

For our goal, this may be more valuable than a more prestigious but purely theoretical group.

## TPU interpretation

TPU should not be promoted to primary collaboration merely because laser processing is tangible.

Its strengths are:
- process control;
- copper/metal applicability;
- owned IP;
- field-test experience outside mobile;
- current publications and student pipeline.

The missing public link is:
**phone/OEM/sealed-VC collaboration proof**.

Therefore TPU remains an excellent challenger or second-source process partner.

## Lab 6.6 interpretation

Lab 6.6 is scientifically distinctive enough to retain, but collaboration should be scoped narrowly:
- failure physics;
- extreme-confinement behavior;
- advanced concept / IP review.

Do not start with a full phone cooling architecture.
The public record does not yet close pump/gas-loop/acoustic/volume/productization constraints.

## What "collaboration ready" does NOT mean

The following remain unknown until direct engagement:
- willingness to collaborate with our team;
- legal/export/commercial restrictions;
- available staff bandwidth;
- access to raw datasets;
- background-IP licensing terms;
- foreground-IP ownership expectations;
- actual equipment availability;
- ability to fabricate phone-scale copper-water structures;
- current confidential work with other OEMs.

These are partner-confirmation questions, not desk-research facts.
