## Goal

Extend the leadership-facing Russia-only institution map using the frozen Institution Atlas Profile contract.

This round asked for each institution:
- demonstrated thermal-management capability;
- target-platform transfer distance;
- representative relevant output recovered into the canonical graph;
- patents / collaboration / industry / international signals where publicly evidenced;
- institution / scholar influence context;
- explicit public-evidence gaps.

## Main cross-institution finding

**Institution prestige and thermal-collaboration value remain separate variables.**

BMSTU and ITMO have much stronger general institutional standing than several other Batch-2 nodes, but their current project-specific mobile-thermal evidence still has major target-system gaps.

Conversely, UUST and NovSU have lower general institutional visibility but have clearly current, bounded research programs directly relevant to microchannel cooling and orientation-tolerant electroosmotic heat transport.

This confirms the Atlas design choice:
rankings are context, while Capability -> Claim -> Source remains the technical evidence chain.
