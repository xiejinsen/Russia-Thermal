## Calibration: do not equate keywords with domain relevance

Examples from the staged automatic queue requiring rejection or downgrade after human check:
- **2025 #38**, microfluidic testing for immiscible flow in digital petroleum cores: high keyword match but petroleum reservoir modeling, not terminal thermal-management capability.
- **2024 #49**, microfluidic alginate microsphere interfacial measurement: microfluidics alone does not establish cooling.
- **2024 #90**, microreactor/microextractor flow research: engineering neighboring field; not automatically relevant thermal solution.
- **2024 #57**, building-wall phase-change storage (in raw corpus): phase-change does not equal electronics PCM performance.

Do not use relevance word hits to produce final paper counts. `HIGH` in triage means **high manual-review priority**, not high technical maturity or strong mobile transfer.

## Research judgment

This selected snapshot demonstrates multiple distinct lines in one research institute:

1. Pavlenko/Shvetsov/Zhukov — dielectric boiling, crisis, coatings, microstructured surfaces, current 2025 research.
2. Kabov/Chinnov/Kochkin/Dementyev — extreme confinement, microchannel two-phase flow and interfacial visualization.
3. Surtaev/Serdyukov — wetting and nucleate-boiling surface behavior and microlayer imaging.

This is **recovered activity evidence** for teams, not author productivity totals. It supports Russia's specialized mechanism/diagnostics role; it does not change P1/P2/P3 nor imply smartphone product deployment.
