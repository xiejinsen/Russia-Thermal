### 2021 #155 — high-flux two-phase test-facility development

- Official record: https://www.itp.nsc.ru/publikacii/2021/2021stati/8.html
- Authors: D. V. Zaitsev and V. V. Belosludtsev.
- Paper: "Experimental setup for studying two-phase flows in micro- and minichannels at ultra-high heat fluxes: methodology and first experimental results"
- DOI candidate: https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-6596/2119/1/012133, Journal of Physics: Conference Series 2119.

- Transfer: experimental platform/measurement methodology, not smartphone device capability.

## What this adds

Observed early academic continuity, rather than one recent burst:
- **Pavlenko/Shvetsov/Zhukov**: 2021 capillary porous layers and boiling/thermal crisis -> distinct 2024 n-dodecane porous-coating study -> 2025 HFE-7100 pressure/boiling regime follow-up.
- **Zaitsev/Belosludtsev**: 2021 heated microchannel characterization + specialized flow-boiling test section, primarily enabling method.
- **Dementyev/Ronshin/Chinnov/Kabov**: 2021 two-phase microchannel test & flow-pattern methods -> 2024 high-aspect 12.5 μm adiabatic slit flow pattern work.

**IMPORTANT:** similarity/technical continuity is not itself evidence of one legal collaboration agreement, direct mobile equipment product, or annual publication count.
