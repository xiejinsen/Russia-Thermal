## Identity and research-line problem

MPEI as a whole is a large energy-oriented university. Whole-university annual or Nature Index totals are NOT equivalent to output of mobile-relevant thermal researchers.

Scope needs a **team-based set**, at minimum:
- Ivanov / long-duration thermosyphon and capillary/wettability aging;
- Kuzma-Kichta / active microchannel/boiling and coatings;
- Lyulin / Dedov / thermosyphon and active thermal-device engineering;
- Bulaeva / ordered-wick modeling.

A group's 'main publications' list is a highlighted bibliography, not an exhaustive five-year search. A 2024 Skoltech coauthored paper can be counted once for MPEI under publication-time affiliation, and once for Skoltech in its own institutional ledger, while its global DOI is unique.
