## Reading convention

Read the **latest dated dossier** for a candidate before relying on older institutional profile wording. Treat older dated reports as historical evidence snapshots, not contradictory living authorities. For precise original scientific facts, open the linked paper/publisher/source; for portfolio status follow canonical decisions and leadership brief. If a latest dossier lacks full methods, label it *paper identity verified / mechanism partly inferred*, not deep read verified.
