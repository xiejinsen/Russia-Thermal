## What leadership can now answer

### Q1. Is Russia broadly ahead of China in smartphone thermal management?
**No.**

China is stronger in:
- ultra-thin device engineering;
- copper-water VC product/reliability process;
- scalable surface/wick engineering;
- manufacturing/product integration.

### Q2. Then why collaborate with Russia?
Because a few Russian teams retain complementary depth in:
- reversible/irreversible phase-change failure mechanisms;
- long-duration engineered-surface/capillary aging;
- selected surface-process execution;
- extreme film/instability physics;
- analytical stability/modeling support.

### Q3. Who should we prioritize?
1. Pavlenko / Kutateladze Lab 1.3.
2. Ivanov + Kuzma-Kichta / MPEI.
3. Feoktistov + Orlova / TPU.

### Q4. What exactly would we collaborate on?
- Lab 1.3: failure-state / crisis mechanism and diagnostics.
- MPEI: long-duration surface/capillary aging and reliability labels.
- TPU: bounded thin-copper / wettability / surface-process challenge.

### Q5. What should we not outsource / give away?
Internal ownership should remain on:
- phone architecture / geometry;
- copper-water UTVC design;
- manufacturing/product integration;
- telemetry / controller / algorithms;
- foreground product IP.

### Q6. What do we still not know?
- willingness to collaborate;
- available staff bandwidth;
- current confidential OEM commitments;
- shareable raw data;
- exact fabrication/equipment availability;
- IP/license terms;
- legal/export/contracting constraints.

These require direct contact.
