## 1. NovSU / Yuri Kiliba — electroosmotic assisted heat-pipe cooling

### Paper / capability 10Q pressure test

1. **Problem + target mapping**  
   NovSU targets loss of heat-pipe circulation under changing orientation / acceleration and explores electroosmotic pumping to restore liquid return. This is relevant to portable electronics in principle, but phone fit is indirect.

2. **Novelty / new-regime relevance**  
   The current Russian work is real, current and experimentally grounded. However, electroosmotic pumping for electronics cooling is not a new control principle: closed-loop VLSI cooling with an electroosmotic pump was demonstrated in 2002, and electroosmotic enhancement of micro heat pipes was analyzed directly in 2016.

3. **Falsifiable hypothesis**  
   A phone-relevant electroosmotic assist could still be useful if it removes orientation-sensitive capillary failure with lower total system cost than passive wick redesign or mechanical pumping.

4. **Research lineage / competing route**  
   Global lineage already includes electroosmotic pumps, electroosmotic microchannel coolers and micro-heat-pipe enhancement. Strong competing phone routes include passive UTVC/VC architecture, wick optimization and conventional active micro-pumping.

5. **Key mechanism / control point**  
   Electric-field-driven electroosmotic liquid transport through a porous medium to supplement capillary return.

6. **Evidence gate**  
   Future evaluation would need normalized pressure-flow-voltage-power curves, device volume/thickness, EMI constraints, fluid chemistry, long-duration electrode stability and sealed-system compatibility under phone-relevant orientation cycles.

7. **Data / reproducibility**  
   Current NovSU public evidence includes an experimental pump and a reported 2.4 kPa pressure head at 100 V under its test configuration. The older global EO cooler reports substantially different pressure/flow values under non-comparable geometry and chemistry, so direct ranking is not valid.

8. **Evidence vs hypothesis**  
   Current capability and orientation-tolerance intent are evidence-backed. Smartphone system benefit is a hypothesis.

9. **Decision contribution**  
   The node expands the Russian active-capillary method map but does not establish a unique Russian residual advantage.

10. **Next action**  
    Keep as frontier-watch / supporting active-capillary capability. Do not create a new strategic Direction unless phone-scale system evidence demonstrates an advantage against strong passive and active baselines.

**Evidence maturity:** STRUCTURAL_SIGNAL

## 2. UUST / Olga Solnyshkina — microchannel thermal research

### Capability 10Q pressure test

1. **Problem + target mapping**  
   UUST studies heat/mass transfer in structured microfluidic systems and current micro/mini-channel heat exchangers, with explicit electronics-cooling motivation.

2. **Novelty / new-regime relevance**  
   The program is current and technically credible, but microchannel cooling is already a highly developed global and Chinese field. UUST does not currently expose a distinct phone-specific control point.

3. **Falsifiable hypothesis**  
   UUST would become strategically differentiated only if its structured-channel / microfabrication / diagnostic method produces a phone-relevant pressure-drop, thickness, reliability or manufacturability advantage unavailable from stronger China/global baselines.

4. **Research lineage / competing route**  
   China currently includes near-junction embedded microfluidics at PKU, flow-boiling / GPU microchannel work at SEU, and near-junction / 3D-chip / embedded thermal-management programs at SJTU.

5. **Key mechanism / control point**  
   Channel geometry, structured heat-transfer surfaces, silicon/glass fabrication and pressure-drop-aware thermal optimization.

6. **Evidence gate**  
   Need matched pumping power, thermal resistance, heat flux, package thickness, leakage/reliability and integration data against a strong Chinese embedded-microfluidic baseline.

7. **Data / reproducibility**  
   UUST has a current 2024-2026 project, experimental micro/mini-channel rigs, temperature-field diagnostics and silicon microchannel processing. This establishes research capability, not product-level superiority.

8. **Evidence vs hypothesis**  
   Research-platform capability is evidence-backed. Smartphone competitiveness is unproven.

9. **Decision contribution**  
   UUST strengthens the Russian microchannel research map but reinforces rather than overturns the existing conclusion that China is stronger in current chip/device microchannel integration.

10. **Next action**  
    Keep as supporting research node; no new Direction and no priority-partner promotion.

**Evidence maturity:** STRUCTURAL_SIGNAL  
**Decision impact:** NO_PROMOTION  
**Comparator claims:** CLM-CN-PKU-001; CLM-CN-SEU-001; CLM-CN-SJTU-001

## 5. Reopen conditions

Reopen NovSU/UUST for strategic promotion only if public or partner-confirmed evidence shows at least one of:

- matched phone-scale system advantage versus a strong China/global baseline;
- a distinct control point not covered by known prior art;
- materially lower parasitic-power / thickness / integration cost;
- reliability or manufacturability evidence that changes product feasibility;
- a collaboration-specific non-public capability that can be independently bounded and validated.
