## New node 1 — Bauman MSTU / Nikolay Zubkov

### Evidence recovered

2025:
"Study of capillary pressure of copper heat-pipe wick structures produced by deformational cutting"
- copper M2;
- distilled water / ethanol;
- direct capillary-pressure measurement;
- authors compare favorably with mesh / sintered powder / fiber wick literature.

Current author/publication record also shows:
- internal heat-exchange tube enhancement;
- microstructured flat/slotted channels;
- historical electronics-cooling structures;
- capillary porous structures for heat pipes;
- continuing student pipeline into 2025.

### Why this is genuinely new to the project

Previous active surface/process nodes focused on:
- laser/wettability;
- hierarchical coatings;
- mesh boiling;
- long-duration aging.

BMSTU adds a different control point:
**manufacturing the metal microstructure itself by deformational cutting.**

Potential mobile transfer:
- copper wick formation;
- integrated microgroove / capillary structure;
- potentially lower-process-complexity alternative to sintered powder / mesh / laser structures.

### Boundary

Current evidence does not show:
- sub-mm VC body;
- sealed phone-scale thermal resistance;
- flatness / bonding / stamping compatibility;
- high-permeability + capillary tradeoff at phone dimensions;
- mass-production economics for UTVC.

### Decision

**KEEP as SUPPORTING MANUFACTURING CHALLENGER.**

Do not create a new strategic Direction yet.

- CAP-BMSTU-DEFORM-CUT-WICK

## New node 3 — Frumkin Institute surface chemistry

### Evidence recovered

2024 High Temperature paper:
Kutateladze + Frumkin Institute collaboration on laser-ablated copper cavities with selective hydrophobization.

Frumkin key people:
- Ludmila Boinovich
- Alexandre Emelyanenko
