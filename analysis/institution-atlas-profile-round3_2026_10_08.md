## Result 1 — actual manufacturing and systems engineering are distinct from academic expertise

### Thercon-KTT

Direct current official company source:
https://thercon.ru/about/

Reports full-cycle thermal modeling, design, prototyping, testing and series manufacturing of passive two-phase heat transport, including processor/FPGA and server/industrial computing relevance.

The company self-reports **6 invention patents and 3 utility models**; this is an external company-claimed aggregate, NOT the number of individually verified canonical Patent objects.

It is a strong Russia-side electronics LHP/heat-pipe engineering comparator; sub-mm smartphone VC, unit economics and consumer production volume are not evidenced.

### NPP TAIS

MPEI's official 2021 international heat-pipe conference report documents co-organization by TAIS and MPEI, with Goncharov's technical leadership:
https://mpei.ru/news/Pages/newsItem.aspx?newsID=2610

A 2025 technical volume on two-phase thermal-control systems jointly names Kiseev/Sazhin (UrFU) and Goncharov:
https://publishing.intelgr.com/index.php/15-izdaniya/uchebnye-i-nauchnye-raboty/934-dvukhfaznie-sistemi-termoregulirovaniya.html

This demonstrates public professional and coauthorship signals, not a newly verified commercial collaboration contract.

### NPO Lavochkin

Official Heat Pipe Center:
https://www.laspace.ru/ru/activities/products/teplovye-truby/

Direct experience in spacecraft thermal-control hardware, heat-pipe/LHP design, manufacturing, vacuum and thermal/environmental testing. The aerospace reliability infrastructure and full-cycle engineering is credible, but no phone product/volume-production claim is warranted.
