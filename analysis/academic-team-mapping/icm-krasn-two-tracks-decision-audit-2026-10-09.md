## Phone-focused academic collaboration possibility

- **Track A, academic method candidate:** topology-aware heat-spreader integration and thermal system modelling for dense electronics; output could be a publicly inspectable model of perpendicular hot-source-to-sink paths, with a matched baseline against China 0.5 mm UTVC / existing internal design. - **Track B, mathematical reserve:** rigorous interface/evaporative instability benchmark to test sensitivity of a physical or surrogate thermal model. Requires precise relevance to confined liquid-gas wick system and demonstrable error reduction; no reason to assume exact theoretical solutions alone beat modern China/device-level models.
- **No integrated imaginary team:** the distinct A and B personnel have separate source and group chains; propose no joint project without verified cross-group ownership and necessity.
- **Not unique worldwide** is NOT an automatic dismissal; potential complementarity matters if research artifact demonstrably fills a China/internal gap. No evidence of present data sharing, phone industry contract, collaboration willingness, IP rights or current smartphone prototypes.
