## Core judgment

Tomsk State University offers a *genuine, active, named computational heat-transfer team* whose work covers two complementary methods: (A) passive PCM with fins or metal foam as transient heat buffers; (B) channel/rib cooling models with periodic heat input. The official lab [Lab on Convective Heat and Mass Transfer](https://chmt.tsu.ru/) names Mikhail A. Sheremet as head and Nadezhda S. Bondareva and Nikita S. Gibanov as researchers. Its facilities are computation/software access, not an independently verified smartphone hardware bench. Actual phone hardware, packaging, cycling, autonomous coolant pumps and benchmark gains are unproved. P1/P2 primary decisions remain unaffected.

## Comparator and collaboration decision

China's original phone-scale VC/PCM device and wick work in [Q08 head-to-head](../q08/p1-p2-china-global-counterexamples-batch2-2026-10-09.md) establishes why mathematical PCM/fin optimization cannot be claimed Russian-exclusive. For TSU to contribute, it need not be globally unique: the meaningful question is whether the existing TSU parameter/sensitivity model offers an interpretable, *reproducible*, phone-specific decision benefit above a baseline available in China/internally. No such independent matched gain shown.

Candidate academic deliverables if **future authority** allows:
1. Passive: constrained reduced-order PCM transient thermal-buffer optimization for a smartphone thermal burst/idle pulse train, including enclosure volume/mass, melt point, conduction path, latent energy, re-solidification time and cycle lifetime; report uncertainty and an explicit baseline comparison.
2. Active: channel cooling sensitivity/operating envelope including real pump parasitic power, leakage and minimum channel/packaging constraints; stop if overhead outweighs thermal benefit.

**Public-evidence stop:** Without a repeatable phone-like duty-cycle or form factor mapping and a nontrivial same-input gain over existing baseline, keep as an academic methods WATCH. Do not promote active liquid cooling based on an idealized CFD channel, and do not treat university description of 'phones' as a validated smartphone demonstration.
