## Strong China baseline and Russia-specific incremental value

Generic graphite paper/carbon fleece laminate panels face sophisticated existing China phone graphite/graphene sheet, copper VC, compliant BN TIM, and heat-spreading packaging options. The radiation-transparent, mechanically strong modular detector requirement is DIFFERENT from smartphone: it cannot establish lower thermal contact resistance for thin phone SoC interface at a given pressure/thermal cycle without matched measurements. Comparing the earlier 2024 patent to the 2025 manufacturing method is a patent claim/know-how line, not straightforward measured increase in Wm−1K−1.

On software, Android stochastic power optimization is a direct handset workload but falls under limited scan. Any collaboration must provide a new unique **thermal-aware** controller advantage against current in-house/governor DVFS and energy/thermal policy under actual workload and package sensors; the 2023 paper does not evaluate this requirement.
