### Publisher original 2023 peer-reviewed material

[**Multi-level Electro-Thermal Simulation of Power PCB Electronic Modules for Motor Driving**](https://doi.org/10.37394/23201.2023.22.14) — Konstantin O. Petrosyants, Igor A. Kharitonov, Mikhail S. Tegin, *WSEAS Transactions on Circuits and Systems* **22:126–134 (2023)**. [Original publisher](https://www.wseas.com/journals/articles.php?id=8701), [HSE institution bibliographic portal](https://publications.hse.ru/en/articles/1099741374). Real power MOSFET bridge/motor controller on a PCB. Multilevel model links COMSOL physically derived device and structure heating, SPICE circuit-level transient power, ASONIKA-TM PCB multi-node thermal estimate. Demonstrates coherent modeling workflow of electronic overheating behavior. Original full 2023 PDF openly accessible at publisher and original authors list HSE MIEM affiliation.

**What it does not demonstrate:** a phone CPU/GPU/NPU silicon temperature vs SoC package/VC/skin-energy budget accuracy bound, any integration into handset runtime thermal policy, independent superior results over China PCB/smartphone multiphysics modeling, or thermal aging/long-time field reliability.

## China/domestic counterfactual and actionable research question

Domestic commercial thermal EDA (thermal PCB and SiP), IR calibration and chip/board simulation tools are already widely available. The actual potential Russia-specific control point would be a **reproducible cross-tool electrothermal calibration method under realistic phone power bursts and contact resistances** with independently demonstrated significant delta over our toolchain and a strong academic China comparator. Current HSE evidence offers an academically validated multiscale *method*, no measured unique increment or direct mobile stack implementation.

Academic collaboration would only become a candidate if specific strong phone-scenario regression cases, aligned thermal-camera sensor data, model parameters and independent benchmark uncertainty were obtainable under real approval/IP/data-right gates; no authorized contact or experiments performed.
