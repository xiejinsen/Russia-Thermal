## Decision result

**MISIS has verifiable materials research and different academic scientific groups, not a demonstrated integrated phone micro-thermoelectric cooler.** Most 2021–2025 original results recovered so far concern material thermoelectric performance at ~575–700 K, magnetocaloric cycles (room and cryogenic research), or thermoelectric generation. The sole legitimate collaboration residual at present is source/transport/materials insight under phone-relevant temperature ranges and a rigorously specified full-module COP/power budget; there is no public demonstration that this beats current China/global micro-TEC device science.

## Decision-relevant paper cards and material-to-device caveats

### Khovaylo thermoelectric alloys / composites

- [**Effect of single and dual doping on the thermoelectric properties of FeVSb half-Heusler alloys**](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vacuum.2024.113530), A. El-Khouly, D. Karpenkov, V. Khovaylo et al., *Vacuum* **2024**. Arc/induction-melted Sn/Co doping changed electric and lattice thermal transport; paper reports **zT max 0.084 at 575 K** for one FeVSb composition. A materials proof, not a near-ambient cooling-module COP experiment.
- [**Thermoelectric properties of filled InCo4Sb12 skutterudite with embedded ZnO inclusions**](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0272884224049216), E. Chernyshova, A. Voronin, D. Moskovskikh, T. Sviridova, V. Yushchuk, V. Khovaylo, *Ceramics International* **2024**, publisher PII verified (DOI not independently retrieved during this round). Induction melting, ball milling and SPS produced composites. Reference skutterudite zT **1.41 at 700 K** versus ZnO composite best **1.28**, with **18% hardness increase** in reported sample. The physical trade-off matters to reliability/material science, but this zT measurement near **700 K is not a phone cooler's operating point**; increased hardness or lower cost does not equal phone efficiency.
- [**Mechanochemical synthesis of Ba-doped BiCuSeO oxyselenides**](https://doi.org/10.1039/d5mr00056d), Khanina et al. incl. Voronin/Khovaylo, *Materials Advances* **2025**. Solid-state powder phase synthesis evidence, no compact cooler demonstration. Publisher supplies supplementary information but that is not a device-level COP dataset.

### Karpenkov magnetic caloric group

- [**Adjusting La(Fe,Si)13 compounds and hydrides for multi-stimuli cooling cycles**](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jallcom.2023.171154), Dmitriy Karpenkov et al., *Journal of Alloys and Compounds* **962:171154, 2023**. Original publisher directly states magnetic and compression tests on reinforced La(Fe,Si)13Hδ rods; heat-transfer simulation proposes **100–300 μm diameter rods above 3 Hz**. Magnetic field plus external compressive pressure and heat exchange/frequency constraints are part of a *refrigeration-cycle system*, not a self-cooling passive smartphone plate. The material may operate in room/cryogenic ranges, but a practical phone package is not demonstrated.
- [**Designing high-efficiency cryogenic regenerators**](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jallcom.2025.183808), D. Karpenkov, R. Makarin, C. Tantardini, A. Kvashnin, A. Karpenkov, E. Argunov et al., *Journal of Alloys and Compounds* **1041, 2025**. RCo2 rare-earth hydrogen/helium cryogenic regenerator geometry. The 2025 SSRN preprint and final journal are **one same scientific work**, not two institutional outputs. Different thermal operating regime from handset.

## Strong China / global device baseline

**2024 China, actual thick-film micro-TEC [Nature Communications 15, 3870](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-48346-6)**: Bi2Te3 fabricated micro-TEC with phase-change transfer; original measured max deltaT **40.6 K**, cooling flux **56.5 W/cm2** and more than **30,000** pulsed thermal cycles under its rig conditions. Distinct conditions from MISIS bulk high-T measurements: these results show a strong industry-relevant material-to-device bridge **absent from the currently verified MISIS sample**.

**2025 China, Zhejiang University [Communications Materials 6, 114](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43246-025-00837-9)**: practical fabricated integrated TEC, with an **externally attached phone demonstration**. However the phone thermal experiment uses **20 C circulating cooling water at 1 L/min**, **5 A** TEC supply and external instrumentation, so even this much stronger device demonstration **does NOT establish battery-efficient sealed handset integration**. Do not represent this as a commercial phone device.

**2024 global transistor hot-spot cooling [Nature Communications 15, 4275](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-48583-9)**: optically pumped transient-hotspot Si thermoelectric element validates frequency-domain model. It is actual silicon microdevice research but not an everyday handset system.

The scientific competitor's advantage is **integrated geometry, contact/thermal impedance, microfabrication, cycling and source-to-sink total power**, not paper-count or zT alone. MISIS can only show incremental value if a new RT-range material/transport or experimental protocol meaningfully improves a common phone power/thermal baseline.

## Transfer/falsification gates

**For near-ambient TEC:**
- Require 290–340 K measured zT/Seebeck, electrical resistivity and thermal conductivity at the **actual material and contact stack**, not high-temperature zT.
- Quantify loaded module cooling power vs electrical input and Joule heat: **COP**, hotspot and skin thermal effects, external heat rejection path, supply current, current density and packaging thickness.
- Compare **total phone power** and workload burst conditions against passive copper/graphite/VC/SoC DVFS; an off-phone water chiller or pumped radiator cannot be omitted.
- Establish integrable geometry, series resistance, ceramic/metal contact resistance, reliability cycles and sealing.
- Demand **strong China/globally competitive experimental comparator**; simple thermoelectric material novelty alone is not enough.

**For magnetocaloric/cryogenic:** magnetic field system, compression actuator work, cycle and heat-exchanger mass, field source, geometry, frequency and cryogenic vs ambient mismatch likely invalidate ordinary smartphone relevance absent extraordinary new evidence; classify as low-transfer academic materials background.

**For generator-focused Argunov group:** no independent cooler-device deliverable and no reason to put thermoelectric *generation* into phone active-cooling shortlist without an explicit heat-removal mechanism.

No current public evidence authorizes contact; all above are evidence requirements, not executed experiments.
