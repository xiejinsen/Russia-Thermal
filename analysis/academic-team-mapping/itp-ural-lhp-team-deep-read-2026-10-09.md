## Smartphone research-transfer triage

1. **Demonstrated**: established LHP laboratory; operating-boundary thermodynamics; high-power two-phase closed-loop tests; copper wick/ammonia specific prototype.
2. **Transfer hypothesis**: predict startup/working-fluid/capillary limits for a very thin remote-hotspot cooling architecture, or advise on failure conditions beyond China's own device design; not demonstrated in phone dimensions.
3. **Absent in checked public original set**: sub-mm full-loop phone prototype by this institute; copper/ammonia long-duration chemical compatibility assurance; smartphone system thermal/noise/BOM/folding integration; device-specific matched comparison.
4. **Independent China benchmark**: [A thin and lightweight miniature loop heat pipe for cooling mobile electronic devices](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.device.2025.100783), *Device* 2025, reports **0.7-mm, 3.95-g** miniature LHP and 30 days stability at 90°C. This is a China/phone-relevant original device paper; far closer physically than Russian 12-mm evaporator. Do NOT compare thermal resistance numerically without same loads/methodology. [2025 smartphone-targeted 0.8-mm thin LHP](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.expthermflusci.2025.111510) is an independent global device comparator, not necessary to classify as Chinese.
5. **Potential complementarity, NOT Russia lead**: team's decades-long startup/reliability/working-fluid experience could add edge-condition insight or offer validation of an operating-limit model **if** it yields distinct reusable output and can be adapted to phone thermal constraints. World-first originality is not a prerequisite, but measurable increment vs Chinese device baselines is.
