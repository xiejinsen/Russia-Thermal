## 5. P1 collaboration decision impact

**KEEP P1, no priority upgrade.** The evidence strongly supports an active multi-person Lab 1.3 research ecosystem. The latest original 2026 study is more decision-relevant than generic coating improvements because it quantifies dry-spot area, density and contact-line evolution with high-speed multimodal ground truth and confronts irreversible crisis dynamics.

However the residual against existing China/global thermal baselines is not yet proven:
- does a state-label taxonomy make a **predictively distinct** decision beyond internal temperature/pressure, saturation-state and boiling-aware models?
- are the markers repeatable across coatings, working fluids, confinement and heat-flux trajectories?
- can the lab variables be reduced to feasible phone-sensor observables or used offline to falsify a phone-scale model?
- can the requested synchronized original run-level datasets actually be shared? NOT KNOWN; **no outreach**.

The 2026 paper is not evidence of a smartphone ultra-thin sealed VC, nor proof of China-inaccessible ground truth. Maintain strategic-candidate/foundational transfer only.
