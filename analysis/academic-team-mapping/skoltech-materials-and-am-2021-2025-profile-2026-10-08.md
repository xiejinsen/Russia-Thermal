## 2. Institution, formal labs and actual people

**Skolkovo Institute of Science and Technology (ACT-SKOLTECH)** is a Russian academic research university. Two distinct relevant groups:

**A. Current Additive Manufacturing Laboratory, Center for Materials Technologies:** [Skoltech official lab](https://www.skoltech.ru/en/laboratories/additive-manufacturing-laboratory), head **Stanislav A. Evlashin**, current institute [Associate Professor](https://www.skoltech.ru/en/persons/stanislav-evlashin). Work: ceramics/metals/plastics additive processes, geometry and material parameter design. Original [2023 polymer BN 3D printed functional radiator](https://doi.org/10.3390/polym15051214) explicitly credits Evlashin with study supervision/conceptualization and project administration and Skoltech CMT authors. This lab is also the additive-manufacturing academic node of the [MPEI–Skoltech 3D-printed flat evaporator loop thermosyphon](https://doi.org/10.1134/S0869864324040085) (2024 journal issue, 2025 English Version-of-Record date, one cross-organization scientific work). Its thermal device collaboration is academically verifiable but phone package, sealed VC, ethanol scaling, OEM/willingness unknown.

**B. "Smart Materials and Systems" academic research group/lab:** [current Skoltech official staff](https://www.skoltech.ru/en/persons/abaimov-sergey) lists **Sergey G. Abaimov**, Leading Research Scientist, and describes Smart Materials and Systems Lab, associated with Skoltech Center for Petroleum Science and Engineering since 2022. His current research also includes structural self-sensing, nano-material percolation and rock permeability, which cannot be counted wholesale as phone-thermal research. The thermal film/aerogel original papers in 2022 list Skoltech **Center for Design, Manufacturing and Materials** (period-specific affiliation), not necessarily today's CPSE. Researcher **Mohammad Owais** defended a [Skoltech materials-science PhD in October 2023](https://www.skoltech.ru/en/applicants/mohammad-owais) under Abaimov; 2024 original film paper lists Skoltech affiliation, but his **current 2026 Skoltech staff status is unverified**, hence Owais is recorded as *historical academic author/alumnus*, not a current employee or independent lab head.

The two distinct lines share university-level material capability but no assumption they have identical apparatus, funding, laboratory personnel or a direct phone prototype.

## 4. Strong China/global controls — materials must pass device-level tests

- [**A Novel Thermal Interface Material Composed of Vertically Aligned Boron Nitride and Graphite Films for Ultrahigh Through-Plane Thermal Conductivity**](https://doi.org/10.1002/smtd.202301788) — Bashir et al., Peking University incl. Shulin Bai, *Small Methods* **2024**, original primary publisher; reported **23.7 W m−1 K−1 through-plane** thermal conductivity and ~4.85 MPa compressive modulus, with **CPU heat sink test**, not phone. This is a more application-proximate material/interface benchmark than unintegrated 5.1 W/mK GO/PVA film, **but different geometry and test conditions prevent an unconditional head-to-head thermal conductivity superiority claim**.
- [**Universal Construction of Electrical Insulation and High-Thermal-Conductivity Composites Based on the In Situ Exfoliation of Boron Nitride-Graphene Hybrid Filler**](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsami.4c18250) — Ruiping Hu, Shiyi Wen et al., Lanzhou University, *ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces* **17(4), 2025**, Chinese original hybrid TIM with exfoliated BNNS/graphene, specifically interfacial thermal resistance and electrical insulation; proof China also has recent polymer–BN/graphene methodology, not necessarily exactly same Skoltech chemistry.
- [**Highly thermally conductive and soft TIM based on vertically oriented BN film**](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compositesb.2024.111219) — *Composites Part B* **2024**, original reported **19.1 Wm−1K−1** through-plane in high-volume BN (~37vol%) compliant film, distinct from Skoltech filler fraction and mechanical testing.
- [**2D Materials-Based Thermal Interface Materials: Structure, Properties, and Applications**](https://doi.org/10.1002/adma.202311335) — Chinese review, *Advanced Materials* **2024**, valuable authority map not independent additional prototype.

**Transfer test that matters:** same phone-class interface thickness, electrical insulation, filler orientation and loading, **thermal contact resistance** at realistic clamp pressure/cycling, through-plane vs in-plane pathways, compressibility, aging, leakage/outgassing and actual chip junction + skin performance, against graphitic/BN products already available in China. Neither Skoltech 2022 BN/PVA aerogel, 2023 3D printed BN polymer radiator, nor 2024 GO/PVA film has published proof meeting this matched phone evaluation.
