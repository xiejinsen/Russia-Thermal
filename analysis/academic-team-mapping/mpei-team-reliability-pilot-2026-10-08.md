[English translation](https://doi.org/10.1134/S0040601525600683), *Thermal Engineering* 72(12), 2025, appears to be **same scientific work**; group provisionally as one output and verify before final dedup.
  - **Scope:** coating performance and tilt, not calendar-aging precursor measurement; tested loads and dimensions do not establish phone feasibility.
- [**Long-term operational stability of a hierarchical evaporator surface in a two-phase thermosyphon**](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S295042522600109X), **N. S. Ivanov**, *Progress in Engineering Science* 3(2), 100314, **June 2026**, DOI **10.1016/j.pes.2026.100314**. Publisher reports 42 months periodic operation of R410A stainless-steel thermosyphon, microgroove + Al2O3 nanoparticle surface, integral resistance around **0.015 K/W** and ~3× smoother counterpart, no marked SEM/EDX erosion/contamination, but **lower capillary imbibition rate** after operation.
