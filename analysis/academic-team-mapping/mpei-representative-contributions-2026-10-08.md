## Leadership decision

The Moscow Power Engineering Institute (MPEI) has a credible academic heat-transfer research line around engineered thermosyphon evaporator coatings, wetting and heat-transfer reliability, associated principally with Prof. Yuri A. Kuzma-Kichta and Nikita S. Ivanov. The distinctive P2 research possibility is **42 calendar months of operational exposure followed by weakened liquid imbibition while headline thermal resistance remains comparatively stable**. This does NOT prove an early-warning marker, an actual phone VC lifetime advantage or performance leadership versus China. P2 remains CONDITIONAL RESERVE and requires useful intermediate data access or independently discriminating degradation information before a project is justified.
