Kuznetsov, *Fluid Dynamics*, vol **60**, issue **8** (journal issue **2025**), [original publisher contents](https://www.pleiades.online/contents/fldyn/fldyn8_25v60cont.htm). **Question:** Can film/gas shear interaction cool a locally heated rectangular source, and can a simplified exact solution test numerical algorithms?

**Method:** analytical linearization + thin-layer numerical solutions; small surface deflection and curvature assumption, coupling interfacial shear, FC-72 and gaseous nitrogen, convective heat, surface tension/thermocapillary Marangoni stress and temperature-dependent liquid viscosity. The experimental/computational modeling specifics described in public abstract are not evidence of independently operated handset cooling test stand.

**Finding:** a simplified exact limit is available for regression/verification of full numerical model and flow/heat distributions in given flat channel geometry; no independent measured enhancement at phone thickness/power is established by the original accessible abstract.

**Gap:** original journal's complete per-author 2025 affiliation line was not independently opened in this round. Current official hydrodynamics lab roster **strongly supports present identity**, but formal institute joint project and 2025 publisher historic appointment remain separate.

**Comparative boundary:** strong independent China/global physical VC/heat-pipe and semi-analytical modeling of actual wick/dryout with limited thickness must be the baseline; this film/gas model adds value only if it produces physically meaningful counterexamples or known error bounds in a **same-boundary** engineering subproblem. Open gas shear has extra parasitic power/acoustic and gas separation/sealing conditions; no evidence of a 0.5- or 0.7-mm self-contained phone prototype.
