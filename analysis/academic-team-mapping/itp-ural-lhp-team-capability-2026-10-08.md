## 4. Relative technical value vs strongest China and global baseline

**China phone-relevant LHP prior art is now far beyond a generic "Russia developed miniature electronics LHP" novelty claim:**
- [Cui et al., *Device*, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.device.2025.100783): copper/nickel composite-wick mobile mLHP at **0.7 mm**, **3.95 g**, accelerated **90°C / 30 days** in its tested system.
- [2025 Chinese ultra-thin LHP experimental paper](https://epjournal.csee.org.cn/zh/article/doi/10.3969/j.issn.1008-0198.2025.04.016/): **0.71-mm** flat LHP, reported **16-W** maximum in specified setup.
- [2025 ultra-thin flexible LHP for foldables](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enconman.2024.119332): **0.7-mm** architecture under bending/orientation variations.
- China/global active and passive state-based LHP research already addresses gravity, wick structure, multi-evaporator and dryout boundaries; the competitive question is **which information gain remains Russian?**, not which country has ever made an LHP.

**Cross-platform integrity:** Russian 800-W ELR and Chinese 16-W ultra-thin results are **NOT** head-to-head efficiencies. Very different device thickness, mass, heat source, sink, working fluid, heat path and operating temperature; a high capacity on a much larger device cannot be called better, and small phone heat load is not inherently worse.

**Retained Russian potential:** expert device-operating-envelope analysis, thermal/hydraulic pressure-balance and startup/failure taxonomy, copper wick + refrigerant materials-compatibility methodology and adverse-orientation characterization. The academic team has an unusually sustained evidence chain across 2021–2025; these are **research and validation complements**, not a differentiating phone LHP device deliverable or exclusive capillary physics.
