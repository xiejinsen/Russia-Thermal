## Strict counterfactual conclusion

**P1:** Chinese engineering and global diagnostic/model methods **already cover generic dryout detection/mitigation and state-history modeling**. Kutateladze must not be credited with inventing an otherwise absent technique. Its narrow unproven increment is *reproducible, accessible, mechanism-classified dielectric crisis data* that changes internal phone-UTVC model evaluation under controlled conditions. This is a **conditional opportunity**, not confirmed superiority.

**P2:** Chinese copper-water VC chemistry, lifetime and QA work make a generalized Russian reliability leadership thesis untenable. MPEI's reported 42 calendar months of R410A operation adds a distinct observation *type*, but a before/after pair does not establish time-resolved early prediction or phone transfer. Keep **conditional reserve**.

**Q05 accepted:** Russia's value, if any, is selective mechanism/dataset complementarity rather than handset VC product superiority; across-rig percentage comparison rejected.

**Q08 accepted with limitation:** Partner versus China/internal build cannot be settled until data access, lab rights, repeatability, baseline-increment study and internal expertise inventory. Leadership may authorize only a bounded discussion, not claim a superior partnership ROI.

**Q10 no change:** P1 first feasibility hypothesis; P2 reserve; TPU HOLD. No justification for new canonical event or higher priority.
