Capability statement:
The MISIS energy-efficiency academic center has distinct *materials-level* thermoelectric and magnetic-refrigeration research groups; thermoelectric generators are a separately identified conversion research line, not evidence of an active TEC device. Transfer to phone thermal control remains a hypothesis.

evidence_claims:
- CLM-MISIS-001

Technical scope:
nanostructured thermoelectric bulk materials; magnetocaloric La(Fe,Si)13 and cryogenic regenerators; energy harvesting thermoelectrics (separately excluded from cooling), functional material synthesis/characterization.

Transfer boundary:
Original 2024 FeVSb half-Heusler showed zT~0.084 at **575 K**, and 2024 InCo4Sb12 skutterudite/ZnO zT~1.41/1.28 measured around **700 K**; neither demonstrates near-ambient device COP or a 0.5–1 mm sealed handset package. 2023 La(Fe,Si)13 multi-stimulus magnetic material and 2025 RCo2 cryogenic regenerators do not establish practical phone magnetic refrigerator. Generation ≠ cooling; integrated COP/heat rejection, current, contact resistance, heat sinks, magnetic-field actuator overhead, lifetime, thickness and smartphone platform remain unverified.
