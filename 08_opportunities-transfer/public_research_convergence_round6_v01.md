1. a partner response names a specific unpublished/public paper, patent, process or material;
2. a Stage-0 coupon produces a specific winning geometry/process;
3. legal/FTO review identifies a specific patent family requiring technical mapping;
4. a current role/contact must be refreshed immediately before outreach;

- more Russian institutions;
- more generic VC papers;
- more generic biphilic/laser patents;
- more generic microchannel/film evidence;
