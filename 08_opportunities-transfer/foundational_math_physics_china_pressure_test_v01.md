## 4. What is actually distinctive?

### Candidate control point

**Analytical Failure-Boundary Engine**

Definition:
use exact / semi-analytical solutions and stability theory to predict:
- stable / unstable operating region;
- dominant mechanism;
- dimensionless control variables;
- onset of oscillation / interfacial transition;
- sensitivity to gas flow, heating, fluid concentration and geometry.

This is potentially valuable when:
- full CFD is too expensive for broad design exploration;
- experiment space is large;
- different mechanisms produce similar thermal symptoms;
- failure-boundary interpretation matters more than nominal HTC.

### Why it fits Russia's current surviving thermal story

The three active Russia mechanism candidates are:
1. dryout / rewetting;
2. long-duration surface aging;
3. film instability / dry spot / rupture.

The foundational layer maps **strongly to #3**, partially to #1, and weakly to #2.

Therefore do not force one foundational story across all three.

## 6. Strong China mirror

China clearly has:
- nonlinear/long-wave stability theory;
- Marangoni-instability modeling and experiments;
- interface-resolved phase-change models;
- physics-informed inverse diagnostics;
- fast/sparse thermal-field reconstruction;
- strong high-fidelity multiphase CFD.

Therefore the collaboration pitch should never be:
> "China has engineering, Russia has theory."

That would be false.

A more defensible pitch is:

> **China has broad engineering mathematics and powerful numerical/product infrastructure; selected Russian groups may add an unusually interpretable exact-solution/stability tradition for specific evaporative interfacial failure problems.**

## 8. Smallest discriminating PoC

### PoC-F0 — Blind instability-boundary prediction

System:
simple heated microgap / gas-sheared liquid film with phone-like hotspot length scale.

Protocol:
1. freeze geometry/fluid/boundary-condition ranges;
2. Russian analytical team predicts stable/unstable boundary before experiment;
3. domestic high-fidelity model predicts independently;
4. physical experiment reveals actual onset;
5. compare boundary error, compute time, calibration burden and mechanism interpretability.

Primary metrics:
- classification accuracy stable vs unstable;
- boundary-location error;
- experimental points saved;
- compute time;
- number of fitted parameters;
- ability to correctly identify dominant failure mechanism.

Internal success target:
- blind stable/unstable classification >=80%;
- boundary error <=15–20% over the target regime;
- >=50% reduction in required experimental grid relative to naive sweep;
- materially better mechanism explanation than black-box/numerical baseline.

Kill:
- domestic numerical model gives equal/better accuracy at similar cost;
- exact model requires heavy empirical fitting;
- assumptions break precisely in the phone-relevant regime;
- analytical result does not alter design/experiment decisions.

## 9. IP / control-point implication

Foundational mathematics alone is difficult to patent broadly.

Foreground IP should be tied to:
- a specific phone thermal architecture;
- model-guided geometry / operating region;
- adaptive control using predicted instability boundary;
- diagnostic method identifying approaching dryout/rupture;
- joint model + sensor + actuator chain.

Potential protectable control point:
**failure-boundary-aware thermal design/control**, not the mathematical equation itself.
