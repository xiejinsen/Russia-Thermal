# 1. Kabov / Chinnov — what exactly survives?

## Russia evidence spine

### A. Shear-driven film / CHF methodology

**[Shear-Driven Liquid Films in a Channel under Intense Local Heating: Methodology and Critical Heat Flux Results](https://doi.org/10.1615/InterfacPhenomHeatTransfer.2022045099)** — D.V. Zaitsev, V.V. Belosludtsev, E.M. Tkachenko, Fang Ye, Hang Guo, V.V. Cheverda, O.A. Kabov — 2022.

Key facts:
- explicitly motivated by future high-performance processors / power electronics;
- shear-driven free-surface liquid film;
- intense local heating;
- experimental methodology from convection to high heat flux;
- Beijing University of Technology coauthors are already present.

Implication:
the mechanism lineage is Russian-led/long-running and the joint paper demonstrates **China–Russia knowledge transfer / collaboration readiness**. Chinese coauthorship does not by itself establish an independently equivalent Chinese capability.

### B. Extreme-aspect-ratio slit two-phase flow

**[An experimental investigation of adiabatic two-phase flow patterns in a slit microchannel with 1:800 aspect ratio](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.expthermflusci.2024.111153)** — Dementyev, Chinnov, Kochkin, Ronshin *et al.* — 2024.

Key facts:
- channel height **12.5 μm**;
- width **10 mm**;
- aspect ratio **1:800**;
- five working liquids;
- gas-liquid superficial velocities spanning wide ranges;
- flow-pattern transition and instability mapping;
- Saffman–Taylor instability, transverse-pressure-gradient instability and wettability are identified as pattern-determining factors.

This is unusually close to the **ultra-thin confinement physics** relevant to future two-phase microstructures, although it is adiabatic rather than a finished cooler.

### C. Current microelectronics framing

Kabov / Kuznetsov:
**Heat Transfer and Fluid Dynamics Modeling in Shear-Driven Liquid Film Cooling System of Microelectronic Equipment** — 2025/2026 publication cycle.

Current relevance:
the line remains explicitly tied to microelectronic cooling, not only legacy aerospace/energy film physics.

Reported:

### China comparator 3 — broader current thin-film / structured-surface activity

China now also has:

- hybrid membrane / microchannel high-flux work.

## Kabov decision

### Broad thesis killed

Do **not** say:
> Russia is differentiated because it has thin-film cooling.

China has very strong thin-film boiling / wick / high-flux capability.

### Residual Russia differentiation

Keep only:

> **shear-driven ultra-thin free-surface film / interfacial-instability / dry-spot physics under extreme confinement**, supported by a long experimental lineage and a 12.5 μm slit-flow capability.

### Joint-authorship attribution

The 2022 shear-driven-film paper includes Beijing University of Technology coauthors.

Under the updated project rule:
- this paper may still support a Russian capability because Kutateladze has the pre-existing shear-film lineage and platform;
- it also proves collaboration readiness / knowledge transfer;
- it should count as a strong China comparator **only if independent Chinese capability on the same control problem is separately evidenced**.

Collaboration value therefore can legitimately include the **Kutateladze experimental platform / mechanism depth / tacit know-how**.

### Current country state

**NARROW DIFFERENTIATION / HIGH-RISK MECHANISM RESERVE**

Not yet a Strategic Bet because:
- gas-flow/pump/loop overhead is substantial;
- sealed phone packaging is unresolved;
- dry-spot suppression has not been demonstrated in a smartphone envelope;
- a strong China-style passive two-phase baseline may still win on simplicity.

### Smallest next proof

Before partner promotion:
- reduced microgap/shear-film cell;
- phone-relevant heat-source footprint;
- fixed total parasitic-power budget;
- compare against strong passive VC / thin-film reference;
- measure dry-spot onset, transient hotspot response, thermal resistance, power and volume.

Kill if the shear-film advantage disappears once gas-flow power and loop volume are included.

# 2. Maydanik / ITP UB RAS — does routing/failure physics remain differentiated?

## Russia evidence spine

### Current theory / serviceability

**[An Analysis of the Key Serviceability and Efficiency Conditions of Loop Heat Pipes](https://doi.org/10.56304/S0040363625701152)** — M.A. Chernysheva, Y.F. Maydanik — *Thermal Engineering*, 2025.

Current contribution:
- capillary/pressure-loss/serviceability conditions;
- working-fluid / wick / pipeline relationships;
- LHP efficiency/operating-boundary analysis.

### Current hardware signal

2025 IHPC/IHPS proceedings:
**Copper-Water Loop Heat Pipes with Flat Evaporators: Development, Tests and Application** — Maydanik, Vershinin, Chernysheva.

Reported miniature case:
- flat evaporator thickness **2.3 mm**;
- source area **6.6 cm²**;
- ~20 W natural-convection load;
- ~44 W forced-convection load.

This is much thicker than smartphone targets.

## Strong China comparators

### China comparator 1 — direct small-electronics multi-source routing

**[Study on heat transfer characteristics of a dual-evaporator ultra-thin loop heat pipe for laptop cooling](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.applthermaleng.2024.122395)** — Xuehao He, Wentao Yan, Shuangfeng Wang — 2024.

Reported:
- **1 mm** dual-evaporator ultra-thin LHP;
- CPU/GPU-type multi-source laptop framing;
- successful startup from 5 W–5 W to 20 W–20 W;
- maximum **22 W–22 W**;
- minimum thermal resistance **0.69 °C/W**;
- equal/unequal load and orientation effects.

### China comparator 2 — chip-level dual-evaporator dynamics

**[Performance and energy consumption study of a dual-evaporator loop heat pipe for chip-level cooling](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.applthermaleng.2024.124757)** — Qingdao University of Science and Technology / Shandong University — 2025.

Reported:
- parallel dual-evaporator architecture;
- startup under single/dual loads;
- variable-power operation;
- maximum total load **300 W**;
- explicit fluid-distribution / flow analysis.

### China comparator 3 — explicit multi-source failure boundary

**[Experimental Study on a Dual Compensation Chamber Multi-Evaporator Loop Heat Pipe System](https://doi.org/10.3390/eng7020084)** — Shandong University — 2026.

Reported:
- dual-compensation-chamber / three-evaporator architecture;
- distributed multi-source / long-distance framing;
- charge ratio and startup timing optimization;
- stable total heat load **270 W**;
- single-stage failure threshold around **70 W**;
- failure linked to **cumulative pressure drop exceeding capillary pumping ability / insufficient liquid supply**.

### China comparator 4 — operating-failure lineage

Beihang / Guiping Lin / Lizhan Bai line has long-running primary work on:
- non-condensable gas;
- startup;
- evaporator tilt;
- evaporator/condenser elevation;
- compensation chamber behavior;
- gravity-driven vs capillary-gravity co-driven regimes.

Representative:
**[Effect of evaporator/condenser elevations on a loop heat pipe with non-condensable gas](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.applthermaleng.2020.115711)** — Beihang-led line — 2020.

## Maydanik decision

### Broad thesis killed

The following is no longer defensible as a Russia differentiation claim:

> Russia / Maydanik is uniquely valuable because of LHP routing / operating-limit / multi-source failure physics.

China now has direct academic evidence across:
- multi-source;
- startup;
- orientation;
- NCG;
- compensation chamber;
- variable load;
- capillary / pressure-drop failure;
- sub-mm-to-1-mm device miniaturization.

### Residual Russia value

Maydanik remains valuable as:
- foundational LHP expertise;
- historical design/physics depth;
- possible consulting / model-review / failure-analysis partner.

But this is **knowledge depth**, not a current country-level strategic differentiation.

### Current country state

**DOWNGRADE -> WATCH / KNOWLEDGE RESERVE**

### Collaboration implication

Do not allocate a standalone strategic PoC to Maydanik solely because of LHP physics.

Only re-promote if a partner discussion reveals a specific non-public control point that:
- materially improves a phone-scale topology;
- is not already available from domestic teams;
- can be expressed as a measurable operating-limit advantage.

# 4. Management implication

The emerging leadership message is becoming sharper:

> Russia's credible differentiation is **not a set of product technologies**.  
> It is increasingly concentrated in a few experimentally deep **failure / instability / aging mechanisms**.

Current pattern:

- **Pavlenko** -> dryout / rewetting failure;
- **MPEI** -> long-life surface aging;
- **Kabov/Chinnov** -> film instability / dry spots / rupture.

This is a coherent Russia-side pattern and may become the final management narrative if the remaining evidence survives.

China's complementary strength remains:
- device miniaturization;
- ultra-thin passive devices;
- manufacturing/process reliability;
- product-scale integration;
- high-flux device engineering.

The final collaboration thesis may therefore converge toward:

**Russia failure-mechanism depth**
×
**China/mobile device engineering**
×
**our system/product boundary**
-> **phone thermal robustness at failure limits, not generic cooling components.**

## Round-3 execution update — post-2017 miniaturization blocker closed

New public evidence materially changes one execution uncertainty without changing the strategic reserve decision.

- 2023: two-phase gas-liquid flow in a **10 μm-high × 10 mm-wide** microchannel.
  - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1615/InterfacPhenomHeatTransfer.2023047135

This closes:
> "Does the current scientific line retain genuinely miniaturized experimental capability?"

Answer:
**YES — at mechanism / microfabricated-channel level.**

### Current execution lineage

Current Kochkin/Kabov/Chinnov-related work also includes:
- gas-driven liquid films in flat minichannels;
- wave/entrainment transitions;
- localized 3×3 mm heating;

### Still not closed

No decision-grade public integrated-system data were recovered for:
- actuator/compressor electrical power;
- complete system pressure drop;
- condenser/separator/loop volume;
- phone-enclosure acoustics.

Therefore:
**HIGH-RISK MECHANISM / IP RESERVE remains correct.**

The next useful evidence is a fixed-power/fixed-volume feasibility comparison, not more generic microchannel searching.
