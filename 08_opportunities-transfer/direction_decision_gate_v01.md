# Direction Decision Gate — After Patent / Prior-Art Round v0.1

Status: first formal direction pruning.

## Decision categories
- **KEEP** — continue active research
- **REFRAME** — broad direction is crowded; keep only a narrower hypothesis
- **DOWNGRADE** — secondary / optional
- **KILL** — stop treating as a Russia-specific strategic direction

---

## 1. Generic mobile LHP miniaturization

**Decision: KILL as a Russia-specific direction**

Why:
- China already has ~0.7 mm mobile/flexible LHP research;
- global prior art is mature;
- Russian public mobile-miniaturization evidence is not ahead on thickness;
- generic “Russia knows LHP” is not enough.

What survives:
**H3 — multi-hotspot two-phase heat routing**.

The retained question is not “make a thinner LHP”; it is:
> can Russian operating-limit / multi-source expertise create a dynamically routed architecture that beats VC and current ultra-thin LHP under moving hotspots?

---

## 2. Generic synthetic-jet cooling

**Decision: KILL as a Russia-specific direction**

Why:
- synthetic-jet electronics cooling has decades of global prior art;
- GE and other groups developed thin-form-factor electronics synthetic jets years ago;
- 2024–2025 Chinese MEMS/piezoelectric synthetic-jet work explicitly targets smartphones/tablets;
- Kutateladze's synthetic-jet heat-transfer knowledge remains useful, but the generic mechanism is not Russian whitespace.

What survives:
synthetic jet may remain a **benchmark actuator** inside H4 or hybrid PoC, but not a standalone Russia-collaboration thesis.

---

## 3. Film / droplet / gas hybrid cooling

**Decision: REFRAME and KEEP**

Why:
- broad mechanism is old and patent-crowded;
- Kutateladze nevertheless owns a dense, continuing technology lineage;
- exact phone-scale closed-loop implementation remains unproven;
- Chinese adjacent spray patents are not obviously identical to the gas-shear / film / staged-load approach.

New thesis:
> sealed, compact, adaptive film/droplet cooling for variable phone hotspots.

Key proof required:
- close the loop;
- remove bulky separator/condenser assumptions;
- show low pump/gas power;
- survive orientation and ingress constraints.

---

## 4. Sub-mm phase-change surfaces / capillary physics

**Decision: KEEP**

Why:
- prior art is crowded at the device level, but surface/CHF/dryout physics still admits meaningful performance differentiation;
- Kutateladze has current dielectric-fluid / porous-surface / modified-surface depth;
- smartphone value can be tested inside existing Chinese-style sub-mm envelopes.

Main risk:
China already has strong wick/surface research; Russian advantage must be measured, not presumed.

---

## 5. Confined microfan aeroacoustics

**Decision: KEEP, but narrow**

Why:
- China has strong aeroacoustics too;
- broad fan-noise capability is not unique;
- phone-scale confined tonal-noise control remains poorly evidenced publicly.

Retained thesis:
> source-level tonal/noise control under phone-scale impedance and volume.

---

## 6. Compute + cooling joint adaptive control

**Decision: KEEP**

Why:
- generic DVFS is crowded;
- explicit joint control of workload placement + active cooler + skin temperature + acoustic objective is less mature publicly;
- Russia's SPbU line may contribute model-light online adaptation.

Main risk:
OEM proprietary control may already be stronger than public academic work.

---

# Current surviving Russia-specific theses

After first pruning:

### Tier A — strongest to test
1. **sealed adaptive film/droplet hybrid cooling**
2. **sub-mm phase-change / capillary-surface enhancement**
3. **compute + cooling joint adaptive control**

### Tier B — promising but differentiation must be proven
4. **multi-hotspot two-phase heat routing**
5. **confined microfan aeroacoustics / tonal control**

### Killed as standalone Russia-specific themes
- generic mobile LHP miniaturization
- generic synthetic-jet cooling
- generic VC
- generic graphite/TIM
- generic pumped liquid cooling
- generic DVFS
- generic microfan

The kill decision does **not** mean the technology is bad. It means it is not currently a compelling reason to collaborate specifically with Russia.
