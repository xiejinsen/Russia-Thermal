# Siberian Theory–Fluid–Experiment Capability Network v0.1

Last updated: 2026-10-04

Status: **PARTIALLY VERIFIED CAPABILITY NETWORK — not a confirmed single consortium**

## Purpose

Test the hypothesis that the strongest Russia thermal-management opportunity may be organized as a Siberian theory–fluid–experiment ecosystem rather than isolated professors/labs.

Candidate nodes:
- Kutateladze Institute of Thermophysics SB RAS;
- Lavrentyev Institute of Hydrodynamics SB RAS;
- Novosibirsk State University;
- Institute of Computational Modelling SB RAS / Altai mathematical-physics line.

The standard for promotion is not geographic proximity. We require:
- current or historical direct coauthorship;
- joint project / shared platform;
- dual appointments / institutional links;
- explicit model ↔ experiment closure;
- current 2023–2026 continuity.

# 1. Network verdict

## A. Kutateladze ↔ Lavrentyev Institute of Hydrodynamics

**Status: VERIFIED — CURRENT DIRECT TECHNICAL LINK**

### Current evidence

**Heat Transfer and Fluid Dynamics Modeling in Shear-Driven Liquid Film Cooling System of Microelectronic Equipment** — O.A. Kabov, V.V. Kuznetsov — 2025/2026 publication cycle.

Public metadata shows:
- O.A. Kabov — Russia / Kutateladze thermal-physics line;
- V.V. Kuznetsov — Lavrentyev Institute of Hydrodynamics;
- both listed as authors/corresponding authors in current records.

Technical overlap:
- locally heated liquid film;
- forced gas flow;
- microchannel;
- exact linear approximation;
- numerical thin-layer model;
- Marangoni / surface deformation;
- microelectronics cooling.

This is a direct current **experiment/mechanism ↔ mathematical-fluid model** bridge.

### Historical continuity

Earlier direct joint work includes:
- Kabov + Kuznetsov on shear-driven liquid-film microelectronic cooling;
- Kuznetsov + Kabova on full Navier–Stokes modeling of nonisothermal gas–liquid film flow in microchannels.

### Decision

This is not a speculative relationship.

**Kutateladze ↔ Lavrentyev is a real cross-institute technical collaboration line.**

For the current project:
- Kutateladze = mechanism / experiment / diagnostics;
- Lavrentyev = mathematical fluid dynamics / microfilm numerical modeling.

## B. Kutateladze ↔ Novosibirsk State University

**Status: VERIFIED — CURRENT INSTITUTIONAL / TALENT / EXPERIMENTAL BRIDGE**

### Current evidence

NSU currently hosts laboratories and publication records directly connected to Kutateladze personnel and research.

Examples:
- NSU Energy-Intensive Thermal Processes Laboratory explicitly studies:
  - boiling / evaporation / condensation;
  - micro- and minichannels;
  - thin liquid films ~10–100 μm;
  - wettability control;
  - interferometry / confocal / schlieren / PIV / LIF / IR / high-speed imaging;
  - electronics cooling including computers and mobile phones.

- Current Kutateladze researchers also hold NSU affiliations in primary papers.
  Example:
  Anton Surtaev / Vladimir Serdyukov in 2024 biphilic boiling paper:
  - Kutateladze Institute;
  - NSU Physics Department.

- NSU research laboratories explicitly reference Kutateladze Institute staff and links.

### Decision

NSU is more than a nearby university.

It functions as:
- talent / student pipeline;
- academic appointment bridge;
- experimental and modeling platform;
- publication affiliation layer.

For collaboration planning, NSU can support:
**joint student / postdoc / model–experiment execution**, but should not replace the actual mechanism owner in partner attribution.

## C. ICM SB RAS / Altai exact-solution line ↔ Kutateladze

**Status: VERIFIED HISTORICAL THEORY–EXPERIMENT LINEAGE; CURRENT FORMAL NETWORK UNVERIFIED**

### Direct historical evidence

There is strong direct collaboration history, not merely citation overlap.

Examples:
- Goncharova + Kabov:
  mathematical / numerical modeling of convection in a horizontal layer under co-current gas flow;
- Goncharova + Kabov + Pukhnachev:
  special exact solutions for 3D thermocapillary flows;
- Goncharova + Kabov:
  two-layer flows with evaporation based on exact 3D solutions;
- Goncharova + Rezanova + Lyulin + Kabov:
  modeling of two-layer liquid–gas flow with evaporation;
- Bekezhanova + Kabov:
  stability of film flow including interface-energy effects.

A 2016 paper explicitly states:
> physical experiments at the Institute of Thermophysics SB RAS were used to investigate the liquid-layer / gas-flow system while mathematical modeling used special exact convection solutions.

This is direct **theory ↔ experiment closure**.

### Current continuity

The current 2024 and 2026 Bekezhanova / Stepanova / Goncharova exact-solution line still:
- studies the same evaporative gas–liquid thermocapillary problem class;
- cites Lyulin / Kabov experimental work;
- compares exact-solution predictions with experimental evaporation data;
- carries the exact-solution / stability methodology forward.

### Missing evidence

This pass did **not** recover decision-grade proof of:
- a 2023–2026 joint grant between ICM SB RAS and Kutateladze on this thermal problem;
- a current jointly staffed laboratory;
- current coauthorship between Bekezhanova/Stepanova and Kabov/Kochkin/Chinnov;
- a formal current collaboration agreement.

### Decision

The correct wording is:

> **historically integrated theory–experiment lineage with current methodological continuity; present-day formal collaboration status unverified.**

# 4. Collaboration architecture hypothesis

## Option 1 — single-lab outreach
Contact Kutateladze only.

Advantages:
- simplest;
- strongest direct experimental relevance;
- already has mechanism and partner-ready labs.

Risk:
- misses analytical/modeling leverage.

## Option 2 — modular Siberian team

Anchor:
**Kutateladze**

Add:
- **Lavrentyev** for microfilm / high-fidelity modeling;
- **NSU** for execution / talent / diagnostics;
- **ICM/Altai** as optional analytical-stability module.

This is the preferred **research hypothesis**, but not yet an outreach commitment.

### Why this may matter

A phone thermal collaboration could be structured around one failure problem:

**local hotspot → film instability / dry spot**

with four layers:
1. exact/stability boundary — ICM/Altai;
2. detailed fluid model — Lavrentyev;
3. experiment/diagnostics — Kutateladze;
4. students/test execution — NSU.

Then our side provides:
- smartphone geometry;
- heat-source transient;
- package/material boundary;
- manufacturing reference;
- product constraints.
