# Major Russian University Scan — Round 1

Last updated: 2026-10-03

## Objective

Systematically check the mandatory major-university set for current (2023–2026) capabilities transferable to smartphone thermal management.

This round intentionally separates:
- university reputation/ranking;
- technical evidence;
- direct mobile relevance.

## Main new findings

### 1. MPEI deserves deeper attention

MPEI's Engineering Thermophysics department remains active in heat/mass transfer:
https://www.mpei.ru/lang/en/structure/Institute_of_Thermal_and_Nuclear_Power_Engineering/Engineering-Thermophysics/Pages/default.aspx

Official May 2026 forum report documents work on:
**optimization of heat pipes based on ordered porous structures**
https://www.mpei.ru/lang/en/main/News/Lists/NewsList/NewsDispForm.aspx?ID=1011

This complements the previously stored MPEI/Skoltech 3D-printed microstructured thermosyphon paper:
https://doi.org/10.1134/S0869864324040085

**Analyst inference:** MPEI may be a second Russian surface/wick/manufacturing candidate and should be compared against the Pavlenko route rather than treated only as a generic thermophysics university.

### 2. NSTU-NETI is more relevant than initially visible

Official Priority-2030 material shows a 2025–2030 strategic program around:
- power electronics;
- heterogeneous integration / flip-chip;
- high power density conversion;
- electronics/materials infrastructure.

Source:
https://en.nstu.ru/news/news/priority-2030-nstu-neti-strengthens-leadership-in-power-electronics-materials-science-and-bioenginee/

Its thermal-power curriculum/project examples explicitly include:
**development of a cooling system for equipment with high heat dissipation**:
https://en.nstu.ru/education/faculty-of-power-engineering/msc/13-04-01-heat-and-power-engineering/thermal-and-electric-energy-production_ms/

This is not yet enough for HIGH-SIGNAL because current primary thermal papers/PI still need identification.

### 3. Samara University exposes an additive microchannel manufacturing route

Official September 2026 disclosure:
**first test radiator prototype for CubeSats**, additively manufactured with internally configurable microchannels.

Source:
https://www.ssau.ru/index.php/english/news/26098-samara-university-produces-first-test-radiator-prototype-for-cubesats

Transfer caveat:
space-radiator architecture is not smartphone cooling.

Potential value:
- additive thin heat-exchanger geometry;
- internal microchannel manufacturing;
- lightweight metal structures.

### 4. SPbPU has current thermophysics capability relevant to electronics

Official center:
https://ie.spbstu.ru/en/nauchno_obrazovatelnyy_centr_teplofizika_v_energetike/

The center explicitly discusses heat-transfer measurements/applications including electronic devices and Li-ion batteries.

Classification remains KEEP because direct mobile cooling evidence is not yet established.

### 5. TSU remains a modeling / thermal-control candidate

Russia-China heat-transfer collaboration signal:
https://en-news.tsu.ru/news/tsu-and-chinese-scientists-plan-to-conduct-a-joint-project-in-heat-transfer/

2026 official update on ongoing convective heat/mass-transfer project through 2027:
https://en-news.tsu.ru/news/algorithms-created-at-the-faculty-of-mechanics-and-mathematics-will-regulate-the-microclimate-in-bui/

The program targets digital prototypes from compact heat storage to server/power-equipment cooling.

### 6. MIPT has an early microchip-transfer-physics signal

2025 conference program includes:
**modeling heat transfer from a microchip** in a gas channel where dimensions approach molecular mean free path.

Source:
https://conf60.mipt.ru/event/4921/conference/view_division/3512497

This remains exploratory until a peer-reviewed/current lab line is found.
