# Russia Institution Scan — Additional Candidate Queue v0.2

These are candidates for later verification, **not high-confidence collaboration targets yet**.

## Tomsk State University (TSU)

Official 2024 news reports a Russia–China thermal-management / heat-transfer cooperation discussion with possible focus including **cooling electronic elements**. It also says TSU would contribute mathematical modeling while Chinese partners would contribute experiments.

Source:
https://en-news.tsu.ru/news/tsu-and-chinese-scientists-plan-to-conduct-a-joint-project-in-heat-transfer/

Why keep:
- explicit electronics-cooling interest;
- modeling strength;
- China collaboration context.

Why not promote yet:
- no sufficiently strong recent phone-scale experimental capability identified in this round.

## Institute of Computational Modelling SB RAS — Krasnoyarsk

2024 paper:
E. N. Vasil'ev, thermoelectric cooling system for heat-loaded electronic elements.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.31857/S0544126924050068

Why keep:
- direct electronics thermal-control topic;
- COP / resistance matching is relevant to TEC evaluation.

Why not promote yet:
- currently evidence points more to modeling than differentiated device capability.

## Southern Federal University (SFedU)

2025 created/hosted a broader Russian microfluidics community around Smart Microfluidics 2025.

Source:
https://sfedu.ru/press-center/news/79223

Why keep:
- microfluidic design/manufacturing ecosystem;
- may expose labs/companies not discoverable through heat-transfer keywords.

Why not promote yet:
- primary emphasis is chemistry/biomedicine/materials, not thermal management.

## NUST MISIS / Kotelnikov IRE RAS

2024 work proposed a magnetocaloric microcooler model for microfluidic biomedical devices.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.30898/1684-1719.2024.11.12

Why keep:
- unusual solid-state/local-cooling mechanism.

Why not promote yet:
- requires ~1.8 T magnetic field in the demonstrated model; current mobile feasibility appears poor and direct electronics relevance is unproven.

## Screening rule

A candidate moves from this queue to a deep-dive card only if at least one of the following is verified:
- recent direct electronics-cooling work;
- unusual mechanism with plausible phone transfer;
- unique experimental facility;
- active industrial collaboration;
- repeat recent publications/patents forming a coherent capability.
