## Potential relevance to our Tier-A surface/wick direction

Possible complement/alternative to Pavlenko:
- Pavlenko: boiling, CHF, wettability, surface modification;
- MPEI: ordered porous geometry / capillary structures / heat-pipe system design.

### Hypothesis
An MPEI-style architected/ordered porous wick may improve:
- permeability / capillary-pressure balance;
- liquid-vapor path separation;
- rewetting;
- manufacturing repeatability.

This is a hypothesis, not a demonstrated advantage.

## Required next evidence

Before promotion:
1. exact pore geometry, size and thickness;
2. fabrication route;
3. permeability / capillary-pressure data;
4. heat-pipe working fluid;
5. thermal resistance / dryout limit;
6. orientation;
7. smallest demonstrated device thickness;
8. current PI/supervisor and lab;
9. patent ownership;
10. whether the approach can be fabricated below 0.3–0.5 mm total VC thickness.
