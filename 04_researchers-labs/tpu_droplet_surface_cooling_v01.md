## Positive evidence — controlled wettability

2026:
**Heat-transfer enhancement and evaporation mechanisms on roughness-controlled wettability-contrast surfaces**

Primary:
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijheatmasstransfer.2026.128413

Official TPU discussion:
https://news.tpu.ru/news/novyy-podkhod-dlya-effektivnogo-okhlazhdeniya-mikrochipov-predlozhili-uchenye-tpu/

Reported mechanism:
- laser texturing;
- hydrophobization;
- locally patterned superhydrophilic/superhydrophobic regions;
- aluminum-magnesium substrate.

The paper reports strong cooling-efficiency changes in its droplet-evaporation geometry.

### Transfer caveat
This is open droplet evaporation at elevated surface temperatures, not a sealed 0.3–0.5 mm smartphone VC.

## Negative evidence — acoustic excitation

2026:
**Analysis of the influence of acoustic impact on the conditions of heat and mass transfer during the evaporation of water droplets from a heated plate**

Primary:
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijheatmasstransfer.2026.129217

Official TPU:
https://news.tpu.ru/news/uchenye-tpu-proverili-mozhno-li-okhlazhdat-elektroniku-s-pomoshchyu-zvuchashchikh-kapel-vody/

Conclusion:
additional acoustic forcing changed evaporation/cooling only weakly; TPU explicitly concluded that this route is not promising in its tested form.

This is useful negative evidence.

## Additional negative / boundary evidence

2026 TPU paper:
**Thermal and secondary splashing of water droplets on heat-transfer surfaces**
https://doi.org/10.18799/24131830/2026/1/5462

In the tested liquid-infused surfaces, lubricant depletion at elevated temperature reduced cooling effectiveness, limiting long-duration/repeated-load use.

## Direct electronics-cooling paper

2026 Langmuir:
**Analysis of the Influence Water Drop's Initial Temperature on the Intensity of Heat Dissipation from the Heat Exchange Surface during Evaporation**

Primary:
https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.langmuir.6c01997

The paper frames droplet cooling around shrinking integrated circuits and reports only modest near-surface cooling from changing droplet initial temperature.

## Funding-source correction / caveat

Several 2026 TPU cooling papers acknowledge RSF grant **25-79-10045**.

However the official RSF project title is:
**Development of basic elements of the theory of thermochemical processing of wood...**

Official:
https://rscf.ru/project/25-79-10045/

The RSF project page itself lists several of the heat/mass-transfer and droplet publications.

Therefore:
- the grant number is real;
- the publications are listed under that project;
- **but it should not be described as an electronics-cooling grant.**

This corrects a potentially misleading interpretation from TPU news summaries.
