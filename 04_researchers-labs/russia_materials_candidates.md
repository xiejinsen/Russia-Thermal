# Russian Thermal Materials / TIM Candidates v0.1

Status: first sweep. Current evidence is weaker than Russia's two-phase-flow evidence.

## 1. Skoltech — BN / graphene / CNT polymer thermal materials

### 2023 direct research
**Thermal and Electrical Properties of Additively Manufactured Polymer–Boron Nitride Composite**

DOI:
https://doi.org/10.3390/polym15051214

Affiliations include:
- Skoltech Center for Materials Technologies;
- Kurchatov Institute;
- other Russian materials institutes.

Skoltech lab:
https://sci.skoltech.ru/compositematerialsandstructures

Key researchers linked to the work:
- Julia V. Bondareva
- Stanislav A. Evlashin
- Alexander A. Safonov
- collaborators in modeling/materials institutes.

The paper studies DLP additive manufacturing of BN-filled polymer composites and explicitly frames electronics overheating / heat dissipation as an application.

### 2023 doctoral thesis
Mohammad Owais, Skoltech:
**Material Design and Optimization of Thermal Management Materials Based on Boron Nitride, Graphene, and Carbon Nanotubes Polymer Nanocomposites**

The thesis explicitly targets:
- thermal management materials;
- TIMs;
- graphene / BN / CNT polymer composites;
- microelectronics / mobile-phone heat dissipation.

### Interpretation
This is relevant materials science, but it does **not yet establish a Russian advantage** over China's very deep graphene/TIM ecosystem.

Claimed design goals include:

- mechanically integrated thermal panels;

## 3. Moscow State University — graphite foil process development

MSU ISTINA records a 2023–2026 project:
**Development of technological processes for production of new graphite-foil materials and products based on them.**

Project participants include the Department of Chemical Technology and New Materials.

Public project/profile evidence:
https://istina.msu.ru/workers/183477229/all/

Reported project data include in-plane conductivity measurements of graphite foil in the hundreds of W/(m·K), dependent on density and processing.

### Mobile relevance
Potential relevance exists for:
- graphite-sheet manufacturing;
- flexible spreaders;
- density / thickness / conductivity tradeoffs.

### Limitation
The current public evidence does not yet show:
- direct smartphone integration;
- clear performance beyond advanced Chinese pyrolytic graphite / graphene products;
- a unique manufacturing advantage.

## Initial materials conclusion

**Cross-source observation:**
Russia has credible thermal-material research, but the first sweep does not yet show a materials advantage comparable in strength to its multiphase / fundamental heat-transfer capability.

China already has:
- mature graphite/graphene phone integration;
- large-scale phone OEM adoption;
- active academic graphene/TIM research.

Therefore Russian materials work should remain a **secondary track** unless we find:
- unusual through-plane conductivity;
- ultra-low-density structural spreaders;
- novel electrically insulating high-k materials;
- manufacturable sub-100 μm structures;
- new capillary/TIM dual-function materials;
- or unique IP.

## Next search

Focus on:
- through-plane TIM performance;
- mechanically compliant TIM;
- graphite/metal hybrid spreaders;
- capillary + heat-spreader multifunctional materials;
- reliability / cycling / pump-out;
- scalable Russian manufacturing partners.
