## 1. Skoltech — BN / graphene / CNT polymer thermal materials

**Material Design and Optimization of Thermal Management Materials Based on Boron Nitride, Graphene, and Carbon Nanotubes Polymer Nanocomposites**

Official Skoltech defense page:
https://www.skoltech.ru/en/applicants/mohammad-owais

Official thesis PDF:
https://www.skoltech.ru/app/data/uploads/2023/09/thesis3.pdf

The thesis directly covers:
- thermal-management materials;
- TIMs;
- graphene / BN / CNT polymer nanocomposites;
- microelectronics / compact-device thermal applications.

### Interpretation
Relevant materials capability is established, but no Russia-specific advantage over China's mature graphite/graphene/TIM ecosystem is established.

## 2. SPbU — graphite-paper / carbon-fleece conductive cooling panel

Russian patent:
**RU2834604C1**
**Method for manufacturing a device for mounting and conductive cooling of pixel sensors and detector microelectronics systems**

Direct patent text:
https://patents.google.com/patent/RU2834604C1/ru

SPbU portal:
https://pureportal.spbu.ru/ru/publications/-------------%28db861e86-924b-4f9a-8c2c-1159ba324c89%29/export.html

Inventors:
- Sergey N. Igolkin
- Vladimir I. Zherebchevsky
- Grigory A. Feofilov

Patent embodiments disclose:
- alternating graphite-paper and carbon-fleece layers;
- graphite-paper thickness examples in the 25–100 μm range;
- a prototype panel around 290 × 200 × 0.26 mm;
- graphite paper with stated conductivity around 1350 W/(m·K) in the example;
- large-area thin conductive-cooling panel construction.

These are patent disclosure values, not independent product measurements.

### Mobile relevance
Potentially relevant to:
- lightweight heat spreading;
- thin structural thermal panels;
- foldable / distributed heat spreading.

### Main limitations
- demonstrated application is detector hardware, not smartphones;
- bend reliability, through-plane resistance, adhesive/contact stack and phone assembly compatibility are unknown;
- no evidence yet that it exceeds advanced Chinese phone graphite/graphene solutions.

## Initial materials conclusion

**Cross-source observation:**
Russia has credible thermal-material activity, but the current evidence does not show a materials advantage as strong as the multiphase / phase-change signal.

China already has:
- mature graphite/graphene phone integration;
- active academic graphene/TIM research;
- strong mobile manufacturing/productization.

Therefore materials remain a **secondary track** unless we find:
- unusual through-plane conductivity;
- ultra-low-density structural spreaders;
- electrically insulating high-k materials;
- manufacturable sub-100 μm structures;
- capillary + heat-spreader multifunctional materials;
- or distinctive IP.
