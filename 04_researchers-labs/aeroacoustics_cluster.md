# Russian Aeroacoustics Cluster — Mobile Active-Cooling Transfer v0.2

Status: strong aeroacoustic capability; direct microfan transfer remains unproven.

## 1. TsAGI — Acoustics Department

Official:
https://www.tsagi.ru/en/research/aeroacoustics/

Current official responsible specialist:
**Victor F. Kopiev**, Head of TsAGI Acoustics Department.

Documented capabilities:
- fan/rotor and flow-noise research;
- active and passive acoustic suppression;
- acoustic-field measurement;
- sound-absorbing structures;
- aeroacoustic modeling and experiments;
- industrial noise applications beyond aircraft.

### Fresh evidence
Kopiev et al., 2025:
**Active control of flow-induced cavity noise using plasma actuator**
DOI: https://doi.org/10.31857/S0320791925030104

The experiment demonstrated active suppression of a dominant tonal component. The mechanism and scale are aerospace-specific; its relevance to phones is methodological, not a direct product solution.

## Mobile relevance

### Transferable capability
Potentially high:
- source localization;
- tonal-noise decomposition;
- broadband turbulence-noise measurement;
- duct acoustic modes;
- impedance / liner methods;
- active-flow/noise control methodology.

### Not established
- sub-centimeter fan aerodynamic efficiency;
- smartphone duct pressure-flow optimization;
- psychoacoustic response at near-ear / handheld distance;
- low-cost product design.

## Proposed collaboration experiment

### Question
Can aeroacoustic optimization shift the thermal/noise Pareto frontier of a phone microfan without reducing useful pressure-flow performance?

### Equal-envelope test
Compare baseline and optimized microfan/duct variants with fixed:
- total device envelope;
- electrical input power;
- target static pressure or flow point;
- inlet/outlet opening budget.

Measure:
- pressure-flow curve;
- thermal resistance / sustained SoC-equivalent heat load;
- overall SPL;
- tonal prominence;
- blade-passing-frequency components;
- vibration;
- perceived-noise proxy.

### Important strategic point
The likely collaboration value is **method + experimental science**, not importing an aircraft fan design.
