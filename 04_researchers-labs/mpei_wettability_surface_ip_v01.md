## Core correction

Previous project framing treated MPEI mainly as:
- ordered porous wick modeling;
- 3D heat-pipe structures.

Patent and current-role evidence now shows a distinct experimental line around:
- micro/nanoparticle coatings;
- capillary surfaces;
- hydrophobic/hydrophilic control;
- thermosyphons;
- condensation / evaporation / boiling.

Do not merge this line with Bulaeva/Savchenkov/Savchenkova without organizational evidence.

## Why this matters for smartphones

Potential contribution:
- controllable wetting state;
- hierarchical surface;
- thermosyphon-level integration;
- current active team;
- existing background IP.

But current evidence is **not phone-scale**:
- thermosyphon application;
- water/industrial phase-change context;
- no <0.5 mm sealed VC;
- no dielectric-fluid cycling evidence.

## Relationship to Pavlenko

Pavlenko advantage:
- stronger direct HFE-7100 / dielectric boiling / CHF-dryout evidence.

Ivanov/MPEI advantage:
- more explicit current patent chain around adjustable wetting / hierarchical coating;
- direct thermosyphon integration;
- potentially process-oriented partner.

## Relationship to TPU

TPU:
- laser/biphilic surface + droplet physics.

MPEI:
- micro/nanoparticle coating + tunable wetting + thermosyphon integration.

Both face strong Chinese/global prior art.
