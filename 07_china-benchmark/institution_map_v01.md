Status: first lab-level map for Russia–China benchmarking. No overall ranking.

## 1. Xi'an Jiaotong University — mobile LHP / flexible two-phase routing

### Current evidence
A 2025 Device paper from Xi'an Jiaotong University reports a **0.7 mm, 3.95 g miniature loop heat pipe** for mobile electronic devices.

Primary:
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.device.2025.100783

Official XJTU researcher/news page:
https://gr.xjtu.edu.cn/yxping/zh_CN/article/314353/content/23859.htm

### Main researchers visible in recent papers
- Xiaoping Yang
- Qingjie Cui
- Xiang Ma
- Ziyi You
- Yonghai Zhang
- Jinjia Wei

### Technical features
- integrated wick design;
- nickel-fiber / copper supplementary wick;
- solder-free integration;
- 30-day accelerated aging at 90°C;
- flexible / bendable LHP line;
- explicit smartphone/mobile-device targeting.

A related flexible line:
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enconman.2024.119332

### Industrial relevance
The 2025 Device paper includes GoerTek-affiliated coauthors, which is a useful signal of product-chain interaction.

### Benchmark implication
For Russia's Maydanik LHP line, **miniaturization alone is not a differentiation argument**. Russia should be tested for:
- multi-source routing;
- startup / serviceability modeling;
- novel capillary topology;
- controllability;
- failure-boundary knowledge.

- manufacturability.

Generic “graphene is highly conductive” is not sufficient.

## 5. Peking University — embedded microfluidics / extreme heat flux

### Team
Bai Song group, College of Engineering, Peking University.

Official PKU page:
https://mech.pku.edu.cn/xwzx/xwkx/0baed6d32511433dab994392c9de3f0b.htm

Primary:
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41928-025-01449-4

### Architecture
Three-level embedded silicon microfluidics:
- tapered manifold;
- microjet layer;
- sawtooth microchannels.

### Reported performance
- heat flux up to 3000 W/cm²
- pumping power ~0.9 W/cm² at the extreme condition
- standard MEMS-compatible fabrication
- direct silicon integration.

### Mobile relevance
This is **not** a phone cooling architecture due to packaging / coolant / integration differences.

But it proves China also has world-class fundamental microfluidic heat-transfer research.

### Benchmark implication
Kutateladze's microchannel / film / droplet line should not be described as unique merely because it is microscale. Its possible differentiation is more specific:
- gas-liquid / film instability;
- thin-film evaporation;
- droplet-flow architecture;
- dielectric fluids;
- unusual channel aspect ratios.

Named recent researcher:

### Benchmark implication
This materially changes the Russia–China gap assessment.

Russia's TsAGI/PNRPU capability may still be valuable, but **aeroacoustics is not absent in China**.

The Russian differentiation hypothesis must be made more specific:
- micro-scale fan / duct source identification;
- active noise control;
- tonal-noise mechanisms under highly confined phone geometry;
- measurement methods that can be adapted to near-field handheld acoustics;
- unique test facilities / methods / IP.

### Interpretation

However it shows China also has active DVFS / adaptive runtime research.

### Global thermal-software benchmark note
Recent international mobile DVFS work has advanced beyond energy-only objectives toward explicit thermal adaptation and proactive throttling. This means the Russia–China collaboration opportunity should target:
- thermal prediction;
- active-cooler actuator control;
- skin-temperature / noise constraints;
rather than generic adaptive DVFS.

# Institution-level conclusion v0.1

China currently shows a **multi-layer thermal ecosystem**:

1. mechanism research;
2. sub-mm device design;
3. advanced thermal materials;
4. product supply-chain collaboration;
5. OEM phone integration;
6. active cooling;
7. runtime optimization.

This raises the bar for Russian collaboration candidates.

A Russian team becomes strategically interesting when it offers:
- a mechanism not well represented in this ecosystem;
- deeper failure/instability physics;
- unusual measurement / experimental methods;
- IP whitespace;
- or a cross-layer combination that China has not publicly productized.
