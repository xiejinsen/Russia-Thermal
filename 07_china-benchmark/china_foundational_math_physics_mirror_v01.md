## 1. Nonlinear / interfacial stability — China is strong

### HIT + Institute of Mechanics CAS — 3D evaporating-film stability

**[Three-Dimensional Long-Wave Instability of an Evaporation/Condensation Film](https://doi.org/10.3390/fluids9060143)** — Weiyang Jiang, Ruiqi Huang, Qiang Yang, Zijing Ding — *Fluids*, 2024.

Affiliations:
- Harbin Institute of Technology;
- State Key Laboratory of Aerodynamics;
- Institute of Mechanics, CAS.

Method:
- nonlinear long-wave evolution equation;
- 3D evaporating/condensing film;
- phase-change boundary condition;
- stability/dynamics analysis.

Implication:
China independently owns meaningful thin-film mathematical-stability capability.

### Inner Mongolia University — linear + weakly nonlinear stability

**[Instability of nanofluid film flow under external electric field: Linear and weakly nonlinear analysis](https://doi.org/10.1088/1674-1056/addcc0)** — Xinshan Li, Danting Xue, Ruigang Zhang, Quansheng Liu, Zhaodong Ding — *Chinese Physics B*, 2025.

Method:
- long-wave approximation;
- asymptotic expansion;
- nonlinear evolution equation;
- linear stability;
- weakly nonlinear analysis;
- numerical confirmation.

Implication:
China also has formal analytical/nonlinear-film stability methods, not only engineering CFD.

### University of South China / Chongqing University — Marangoni instability

**[Coupling effect of substrate thermal properties and droplet geometry on Marangoni instabilities inside an evaporating droplet at quasi-steady state](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.icheatmasstransfer.2025.108745)** — Tianshi Wang, Jintao Chen, Xiaomin Kang *et al.* — *International Communications in Heat and Mass Transfer*, 2025.

Method:
- 3D model;
- hydrothermal waves;
- longitudinal rolls;
- Bénard–Marangoni cells;
- substrate / geometry parameter effects.

The same Chinese line also has earlier direct Marangoni-instability experiments.

Implication:
Russia cannot claim exclusive competence in Marangoni/nonlinear interfacial instability.

## 2. Mechanistic phase-change modeling — China is very strong

### Xi'an Jiaotong University — interface heat-flux phase-change model

**[Development and validation of finite-interface-heat-flux phase change model](https://doi.org/10.7527/S1000-6893.2026.32977)** — Zicheng Tang, Zeran Han, Dan Zheng, Ting Ma — *Acta Aeronautica et Astronautica Sinica*, 2026.

Source facts:
- converts interfacial heat flux to phase-change source terms;
- validated against 1D Stefan benchmark;
- 2D pool boiling;
- 3D microchannel boiling;
- flow-boiling experiment;
- public reported deviations include ~3.33% interface-position, ~1.2% time-averaged Nu, ~4.48% bubble-diameter benchmark and ~14.4% wall-superheat experiment minimum deviation.

Implication:
China has strong physics-based phase-change numerical modeling with explicit experimental validation.

Russia does not have a broad advantage in numerical two-phase modeling.

## 3. Inverse thermal problems / diagnostics — China has a stronger direct electronics signal

### HUST — integrated-circuit heat-source inversion

**[Heat source field inversion and detection based on physics-informed deep learning](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.icheatmasstransfer.2025.108824)** — Yimeng Chi, Mingliang Li, Rui Long *et al.* — *International Communications in Heat and Mass Transfer*, 2025.

Affiliation line:
Huazhong University of Science and Technology.

Target:
integrated-circuit / multi-heat-source thermal diagnosis.

Method:
- physics-informed neural network;
- reconstruct source location/shape/size/power from thermal field;
- multi-source configurations.

Public reported result:
- heat-source shape/position similarity >90% in tested multi-source cases.

Implication:
for **inverse thermal diagnostics tied to electronics**, the public China signal is currently stronger than the Russian evidence recovered in this scan.

Verdict:
**CHINA-BASELINE DOMINANT / no Russia advantage claim.**

## 5. Critical comparison

What China already falsifies:
- "Russia is uniquely strong in stability theory";
- "China mainly does empirical thermal experiments";
- "Russia has unique Marangoni modeling";
- "Russia has superior general multiphase simulation";
- "Russia has unique inverse thermal mathematics."

What remains less matched:
- a **continuous, current exact/group-invariant analytical-solution lineage** specifically for two-sided evaporative thermosolutal / thermocapillary channel flows;
- use of those exact solutions as interpretable mechanism benchmarks tied to a long experimental lineage.

Important wording:
**no equivalent recent China line recovered** is not the same as **China does not have it**.

---

## 6. Current China-side conclusion

China's foundational thermal mathematics is already broad and strong.

China is particularly strong at:
- engineering-oriented mechanistic models;
- experimental validation;
- numerical/interface-resolved tools;
- inverse problems / PINN / sparse thermal reconstruction;
- nonlinear stability in selected interfacial systems.

Therefore any Russian foundational advantage must be framed as:
**method specificity + lineage + interpretability + useful experiment reduction**, not national mathematical superiority.
