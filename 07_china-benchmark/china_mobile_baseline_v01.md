# China Mobile Thermal Baseline v0.1

Status: first structured benchmark. This is not an overall China ranking.

## Executive observation

China already shows strong public evidence across the full chain from **mechanism research -> ultra-thin component design -> materials -> smartphone integration -> active cooling productization**.

Therefore, a Russian capability should not be considered differentiated merely because Russia has research in the same category.

The benchmark question is:

> What can a Russian group add that is not already available through Chinese academic / OEM / supply-chain capability?

---

## 1. Ultra-thin vapor chambers: strong academic + product baseline

### Academic — 0.35 mm UTVC
Zhang et al., 2024:
**High performance ultra-thin vapor chamber by reducing liquid film and enhancing capillary wicking**

DOI:
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.applthermaleng.2024.122813

Reported:
- overall thickness: 0.35 mm
- vapor-space thickness: 0.2 mm
- composite mesh wick
- visualization of boiling/evaporation / liquid-film behavior
- effective thermal conductivity up to 12,454 W/(m·K) at 3 W in the reported test.

Researchers include South China University of Technology / City University of Hong Kong-linked authors.

### Academic — composite-wick optimization
South China University of Technology, 2024:
**Sensitivity Analysis and Optimization of Heat Transfer Performance of Ultra-Thin Vapor Chamber With Composite Wick**

DOI:
https://doi.org/10.1115/1.4065170

### Product — HONOR
HONOR Magic V3 official disclosure:
- titanium vapor chamber;
- **0.22 mm** stated VC thickness;
- 40% lower weight claim.

Official:
https://www.honor.com/uk/phones/honor-magic-v3/

Interpretation:
China's mobile thermal ecosystem already operates at sub-mm / few-tenths-mm device thickness, so Russian passive devices must be benchmarked against this scale.

## 2. Loop heat pipe / mobile two-phase routing: China is already at ~0.7 mm

### 2025 Device paper
**A thin and lightweight miniature loop heat pipe for cooling mobile electronic devices**

DOI:
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.device.2025.100783

Reported:
- thickness: **0.7 mm**
- mass: 3.95 g
- nickel + copper-fiber wick strategy
- accelerated aging: 30 days at 90°C
- explicit mobile-electronics target.

Authors include Xi'an Jiaotong University-affiliated researchers.

### 2025 HUST
Huazhong University of Science and Technology:
**Experimental Study of a New Ultra-Thin Loop Heat Pipe**

DOI:
https://doi.org/10.3969/j.issn.1008-0198.2025.04.016

Reported:
- thickness: 0.71 mm
- heat input: 4–16 W stable start/operation
- maximum heat transfer: 16 W
- minimum thermal resistance: 1.36 °C/W.

### Foldable-device line
2025:
**Bending the heat: Innovative ultra-thin flexible loop heat pipes for enhanced mobile device cooling**

DOI:
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enconman.2024.119332

Reported:
- 0.7 mm ultra-thin flexible LHP
- target includes foldable mobile devices
- bending / tilt explicitly studied.

### Implication for Russia benchmark
The Ural Maydanik team's LHP heritage is significant, but a 2.3 mm miniature evaporator is **not itself a China-beating miniaturization result**.

Potential Russian differentiation must instead be tested in:
- multi-source heat routing;
- startup / operating-limit physics;
- wick/capillary design;
- flexible topology;
- stability / control;
- unique IP.

## 5. Active cooling is already a commercial Chinese capability

### Huawei micropump liquid cooling case
Huawei Pura 80 / Mate 70 / Mate 80 accessory line:
- ultra-thin liquid-cooling layer;
- precision micropump;
- heat-absorbing / phase-change layer;
- temperature + usage-scenario based intelligent pump start/stop;
- reverse-wireless-power supply.

Official:
https://consumer.huawei.com/en/accessories/pura80-micropump-liquid-cooling-case/
https://consumer.huawei.com/cn/support/content/zh-cn16042940/

The support page explicitly describes a dual system:
**phase-change thermal storage + pump-driven liquid cooling**.

### REDMAGIC 11 Pro — inside-phone active cooling
Official REDMAGIC material states:
- mass-produced flowing liquid cooling;
- fluorinated coolant;
- 13,116 mm² 3D VC;
- 24,000 RPM waterproof turbo fan;
- liquid metal;
- integrated airflow duct.

Official:
https://global.redmagic.gg/pages/cooling-technology

A REDMAGIC technical article further states a **piezoelectric ceramic micropump** drives the liquid circulation:
https://mea.redmagic.gg/blogs/product-information/freeze-frame-redmagic-aquacore-cooling-system

All performance improvements / noise numbers from these pages remain vendor claims until independently measured.

### Strategic implication
“Active liquid cooling in a phone” is no longer only a future research concept.

A Russian active-cooling proposal must therefore answer:
- what mechanism is genuinely different?
- what metric frontier improves?
- can it beat current Chinese architectures on thickness/noise/power/reliability?

## Preliminary China capability profile

### Strong public evidence
- sub-mm VC / LHP miniaturization;
- graphite / TIM research and supply ecosystem;
- large-area VC product integration;
- active fan cooling in smartphones;
- micropump liquid cooling;
- PCM + liquid hybrid accessory cooling;
- product-level thermal control algorithms;
- foldable-phone thermal structures;
- high-heat-flux academic microfluidics.

### Therefore, Russia must be tested for
- unique physical mechanisms;
- unusual experimental capability;
- better operating-limit science;
- aeroacoustic depth;
- multi-source routing;
- extreme micro-scale flow understanding;
- novel control algorithms;
- IP whitespace.

## Important caveat

This baseline is intentionally incomplete. It establishes that China is a **high bar**, not the final China map.
