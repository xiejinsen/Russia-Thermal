## Human-factor constraint update

2026 evidence shows that phone thermal experience is spatial and time-dependent.

DRS 2026 study:
https://doi.org/10.21606/drs.2026.2351

Key observation:
- at >=42°C, the thenar eminence showed disproportionately greater thermal dissatisfaction than thumb/index finger;
- longer contact increases discomfort.

2026 mobile-gaming thermal-experience study:
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ergon.2026.104014

Reported under the tested gaming conditions:
- comfort below ~38°C;
- deteriorating experience around 39–43°C;
- strong discomfort around 44–48°C.

Therefore the product objective should include:
- hotspot location;
- time-at-temperature;
- not only max average back-cover temperature.
