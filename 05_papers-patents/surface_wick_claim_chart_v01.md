## Claim-space implication

A defensible collaboration claim should likely require a combination of:
1. target working-fluid behavior;
2. sub-mm sealed geometry;
3. measurable dryout/rewetting function;
4. transient/moving-hotspot condition;
5. manufacturing-state retention / reliability;
6. preferably system-level control interaction.

A claim defined only by surface morphology or contact angle is unlikely to be a strong strategic control point.
