# Surface / Wick Patent & Prior-Art Map v0.1

Last updated: 2026-10-04

Status: research patent landscape for technical strategy. **Not a legal freedom-to-operate opinion.**

## Executive conclusion

The patent field around:
- hydrophilic / hydrophobic / biphilic heat-transfer surfaces;
- micro/nanostructured boiling surfaces;
- laser-modified wicks;
- composite capillary structures;
- capillary/permeability gradients;
- ultra-thin vapor chambers;

is **highly crowded**.

Therefore the Russia-specific collaboration thesis cannot be:
> invent a hydrophilic surface, a biphilic pattern, an ordered porous wick, or a composite wick.

The more credible joint-IP space is narrower:

> **fluid-specific dryout / rewetting / wetting-state retention under sub-mm sealed confinement, combined with phone hotspot geometry, transient loads, reliability and modern UTVC manufacturing.**

### [Heat Transfer Wall of a Heat Exchanger and Method for Forming a Coating to Intensify Heat Transfer](https://patents.google.com/patent/RU2793671C2/en)

**Inventors:** A.A. Nikiforov, A.N. Pavlenko, M.Yu. Kuprikov *et al.*  
**Patent:** RU2793671C2  
**Assignee:** Kutateladze Institute of Thermophysics SB RAS / A.A. Nikiforov

Grant/publication: 2023-04-04

Inventors include:
- Alexey Nikiforov
- Alexander N. Pavlenko
- Mikhail Kuprikov
- Nikolai Pecherkin
- Andrey Kataev
- Oleg Volodin
- Irina Mironova

Assignee includes Kutateladze Institute of Thermophysics SB RAS.

Independent-claim-level concept:
- aluminum-containing metal substrate;
- ceramic microarc-oxidation coating;
- capillary-porous surface with vaporization centers;
- surface porosity 6–12%;
- water contact angle below 40°.

Dependent-claim geometry includes:
- coating thickness ~7–35 μm;
- pore size ~100 nm–10 μm.

### Why this matters

This materially corrects an earlier impression that the Pavlenko-line surface structures are only hundreds of microns thick.

The public IP record shows that the wider team has worked on **tens-of-microns** capillary-porous heat-transfer coatings.

### Transfer caveat

RU2793671 is not a phone-VC patent:
- water-oriented wettability evidence;
- aluminum-containing substrate;
- microarc oxidation;
- no 0.3–0.5 mm sealed VC claim identified.

Still, it strengthens manufacturing plausibility for very thin modified surfaces.

## 3. Russia — TPU / Feoktistov surface patent — claim mapping closed

### [Method for Forming Micro- and Nanostructures on the Heat-Exchange Surface of a Steel Product](https://patents.google.com/patent/RU2812668C1/en)

**Inventors:** Darya A. Kuznechenkova, Evgeniya G. Orlova, Dmitry V. Feoktistov  
**Patent:** RU2812668C1  
**Assignee:** National Research Tomsk Polytechnic University

Filing: 2023-11-01  
Grant/publication: 2024-01-31

### Independent-claim control point

The patent contains one independent method claim covering a steel-surface preparation + nanosecond fiber-laser process:
- abrasive preparation for 10 min with diamond-paste grain decreasing from 40 μm to 1 μm;
- ultrasonic clean for 5 min at 35 kHz in 95 wt.% distilled water / 5 wt.% ethanol;
- 24 h normal-condition drying;
- 1064 nm nanosecond fiber laser;
- pulse energy 0.3–1.0 mJ;
- repetition 2–140 kHz;
- pulse duration 1–120 ns;
- scan speed 100–500 mm/s;
- spot diameter 20–100 μm;
- longitudinal/transverse overlap 1–10 passes.

The disclosed embodiment reports mean roughness height around 9–18 μm and maximum feature height around 17.5–120 μm.

### Partner / mobile interpretation

**Closed:** inventor/team linkage. Feoktistov and Orlova are named inventors, so this is valid TPU/Feoktistov-line background-IP evidence.

**Still open:**
- copper applicability;
- vacuum/sealed-process behavior;
- whether the laser-only texture can generate the desired phone-VC wetting function;
- relationship to the separate hydrocarbon-derived hydrophobic layer used in TPU's 2026 wettability-contrast work.

The patent is important because it supplies a **low-organic laser-texture control route**. It does not establish a biphilic sealed-VC solution.

### IP implication

Generic micro/nanotexturing remains crowded. The plausible joint foreground space is narrower:
> copper-compatible, low-outgassing spatial wetting / rewetting control that survives sealed-VC manufacturing and cycling.

## 9. Strategic IP conclusion

### Do not target broad claims on:
- hydrophilic coating;
- hydrophobic coating;
- biphilic pattern;
- laser roughening;
- composite wick;
- porous gradient.

### More credible joint-IP targets

**J1 — fluid-specific wetting-retention architecture**
- target dielectric/low-boiling fluid;
- wetting state defined before and after vacuum bake/sealing/cycling;
- structure tuned for nucleation + rewetting rather than contact angle alone.

**J2 — dryout/rewetting topology under extreme confinement**
- <0.5 mm-class device;
- fixed vapor-space budget;
- local liquid reserve / return path;
- transient hotspot recovery.

**J3 — moving-hotspot liquid routing**
- multiple phone heat sources;
- non-static hotspot sequence;
- capillary/wetting topology designed for spatial transitions.

**J4 — physics + control co-design**
- predicted hotspot controls active thermal actuator or local liquid redistribution;
- claims must avoid broad software-only thermal control.

**J5 — manufacturability-retained surface state**
- post-forming / vacuum / sealing / aging process that preserves the fluid-specific surface function.
