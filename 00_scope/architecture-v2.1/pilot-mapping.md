## Pilot decision question

Can V2.1 represent the current Pavlenko opportunity with fewer manual truth stores while preserving every important caveat and comparator?

Evidence:
- Pavlenko / Lab 1.3 primary papers;
- dry-spot / crisis 10Q and briefs;
- official institution/team sources;
- Huawei collaboration source cards;
- independent China dryout/rewetting comparator papers;
- relevant patents only where they materially support capability/background IP.

Current interpretation:

- Direction gate;
- Stage-0 scorecard;
- leadership traceability;
- final readiness wording.

History:
- broad dryout uniqueness Kill;
- residual irreversible-crisis diagnostics Narrow/Keep.

## Target object envelope

Expected:
- 4–8 SOURCE objects;
- 4–10 CLAIM objects;
- 3 ACTOR objects minimum:
  - Kutateladze Institute;
  - Lab 1.3;
  - Pavlenko;
- 1–2 CAPABILITY objects;
- 1 DIRECTION;
- 1–2 EXPERIMENT objects;
- 2–4 DECISION_EVENT objects;
- 1 MIGRATION_RECEIPT.

## Required claims

At minimum:
1. direct current dielectric dry-spot/crisis experiment exists;
2. the experiment reports observable reversible-to-irreversible dry-spot behavior;
3. current team/lab attribution is verified;
4. China has strong independent dryout/rewetting/wick comparators;
5. broad Russia dryout superiority is unsupported;
6. phone-scale sealed copper/water transfer is unproven;
7. Huawei collaboration is a collaboration precedent, not product adoption.

## Capability boundary

CAPABILITY may state:
- current dry-spot / boiling-crisis diagnostic capability;
- relevant phase-change failure-mechanism research.

It must not state:
- phone-ready UTVC capability;
- Russia-wide dryout leadership;
- product superiority.

## Direction boundary

DIRECTION:
Failure-aware UTVC / irreversible-dryout collaboration hypothesis.

It may own:
- residual differentiation;
- strongest China baseline;
- internal platform ownership;
- phone-transfer maturity;
- next validation gate.

It must not duplicate paper facts.

## Generated pilot outputs

Required:
- one Actor/Capability view;
- one portfolio row;
- one Russia-China-smartphone collaboration row;
- one decision chronology;
- one restart-snapshot excerpt.

## Pilot success

A fresh reader can answer:
- what is real;
- who owns the capability;
- what China already has;
- what remains differentiated;
- why the Direction survives;
- what remains unproven;

without reading historical round files.

## Pilot failure

Fail if:
- any current caveat disappears;
- country superiority is inferred automatically;
- Huawei collaboration becomes product evidence;
- V1 current conclusion changes;
- manual management-table editing is still required.
